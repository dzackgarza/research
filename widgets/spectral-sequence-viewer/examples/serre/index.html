The order of \pi_4(S^2)

The Hopf fibration S^1\to S^3\to S^2 identifies \pi_4(S^3) with \pi_4(S^2): both adjacent homotopy groups of the circle vanish. We will compute the former group. The answer is \mathbb Z/2, and the integer 2 comes from the cup square of a degree-two class.

Turn the question into homology

Serre’s thesis detects the first 2-torsion above \pi_3(S^3) in degree four. His general calculation modulo 2 leaves its exponent open. To determine the group integrally, we use the homotopy-fiber calculation in Theorem 4.11 of the worked account, taking the last step in homology.

Choose a map S^3\to K(\mathbb Z,3) representing the integral fundamental cohomology class, and call its homotopy fiber F. The map induces an isomorphism on \pi_3. Since K(\mathbb Z,3) has no other nonzero homotopy groups, the long exact sequence makes F 3-connected and identifies \pi_4(F) with \pi_4(S^3). Hurewicz now identifies \pi_4(F) with H_4(F;\mathbb Z).

Pulling back the based path fibration of K(\mathbb Z,3) gives a fibration \mathbb{CP}^{\infty}\to F\to S^3, since \Omega K(\mathbb Z,3)\simeq K(\mathbb Z,2)\simeq\mathbb{CP}^{\infty}. We read its integral Serre spectral sequence twice, first to compute a coefficient and then to take its cokernel.

The cup square supplies the coefficient

In cohomology the fiber contributes \mathbb Z[x], with |x|=2. The sphere contributes the unit and a class u in degree three. Hence only columns 0 and 3 occur, and only d_3 can act. Since H^2(F) and H^3(F) vanish, d_3 must carry x isomorphically to u. Choose the sign of u accordingly. The product rule gives d_3(x^2)=2ux, since x has even degree.

Read total degree four in homology

Write b_k for the homology class dual to x^k, and s for the fundamental class dual to u. The E_3 groups are free abelian, and the homological and cohomological differentials are adjoint under evaluation, up to orientation sign. Thus the homological d_3 takes sb_1 to 2b_2. Its target (0,4) has no outgoing differential.

The only other possible column in total degree four is (3,1), and H_1(\mathbb{CP}^{\infty})=0. Thus the filtration of H_4(F) has a single nonzero quotient. Returning through Hurewicz and the two homotopy exact sequences gives \pi_4(S^2)\cong\pi_4(S^3)\cong H_4(F)\cong\mathbb Z/2.

For Serre’s original loop-space and universal-cover method, see J.-P. Serre, Homologie singulière des espaces fibrés. Applications (1951), Chapter V, §§1 and 5, Proposition 4 and its example.
