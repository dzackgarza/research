## Enriques Surfaces and Their K3 Covers {#sec:chapter-3}

Building on the foundational lattice theory established in @sec:chapter-1 and @sec:chapter-2, we focus on the concrete geometric constructions, orbit classifications, and boundary correspondences that distinguish Enriques surface moduli theory from general K3 theory.
Every degree 2 numerically polarized Enriques surface can be realized via a canonical toric construction, which we describe in this chapter.

### Notational Conventions

We establish standard notation to be used throughout the remainder of this work: for lattice theoretic constructions, we write $U$ for the hyperbolic plane, $\gens{a_1, \ldots, a_n}$ for the diagonal lattice with Gram matrix $\diag(a_1, \ldots, a_n)$, $L(m)$ for rescaling of lattice $L$ by factor $m$, $L^{\oplus n}$ for the $n$-fold orthogonal direct sum, and $L\dual$ for the dual lattice $\Hom_{\ZZ}(L, \ZZ)$.
We write $\I_{p,q}$ and $\II_{p, q}$ for the unique odd (resp.
even) nondegenerate unimodular lattice of signature $(p, q)$ over $\ZZ$.
For root lattices, we use $A_n, D_n, E_6, E_7, E_8$ for the classical root lattices, $\Phi(L)$ for the root system of all roots in $L$, and $W(L)$ for the Weyl group generated by reflections in all roots.
These are typically indexed by vectors of norm $v^2=-2$ or those of norm $v^2=-4$ with divisibility 2. We write $A_L \da L\dual / L$ for the discriminant group, $q_L: A_L \to \QQ/2\ZZ$ for the discriminant quadratic form, and $\ell(L) \da \ell(A_L) = \rank_{\FF_2} A_L$ for the length of the discriminant group.
For group actions, we denote $\Orth(L)$ for the orthogonal group of lattice $L$, $\OStab(L) = \ker(\Orth(L) \to \Orth(A_L))$ for the stable orthogonal group, $\div_L(v)$ for the divisibility of vector $v$ in $L$, and $L[k] = \{v \in L\st  v^2 = k\}$ for vectors of squared length $k$.
In applications to K3 and Enriques surfaces, $\lkt$ denotes the K3 lattice, $(r,a,\delta)$ are the classification invariants for 2-elementary lattices, $\ien$ is the Enriques involution, and $\fent$ is the moduli space of Enriques surfaces with polarization of degree 2.
