## Enriques Surfaces and Their K3 Covers {#sec:chapter-3}

Building on the foundational lattice theory established in @sec:chapter-1 and @sec:chapter-2, we focus on the concrete geometric constructions, orbit classifications, and boundary correspondences that distinguish Enriques surface moduli theory from general K3 theory.
Every degree 2 numerically polarized Enriques surface can be realized via a canonical toric construction, which we describe in this chapter.

### Notational Conventions

We establish standard notation to be used throughout the remainder of this work: for lattice theoretic constructions, we write $U$ for the hyperbolic plane, $\generators{a_1, \ldots, a_n}$ for the diagonal lattice with Gram matrix $\diag(a_1, \ldots, a_n)$, $L(m)$ for rescaling of lattice $L$ by factor $m$, $L^{\oplus n}$ for the $n$-fold orthogonal direct sum, and $\dualof{L}$ for the dual lattice $\Hom_{\ZZ}(L, \ZZ)$.
We write $\I_{p,q}$ and $\II_{p, q}$ for the unique odd (resp. even) nondegenerate unimodular lattice of signature $(p, q)$ over $\ZZ$.
For root lattices, we write $A_n,D_n,E_6,E_7,E_8$ for classical negative-definite ADE lattices, $\Phi(L)$ for a chosen simple system of norm-$(-2)$ roots, $R(L)$ for the maximal set of anisotropic vectors defining integral reflections, $R_n(L)$ for the norm-$n$ stratum, and $W(L)$ for the subgroup generated by all integral reflections (@def:root-lattice and @def:weyl-group).
In even $2$-elementary applications the **primitive negative-norm** root vectors have square $-2$, or square $-4$ and divisibility $2$; their nonzero integer multiples also belong to $R(L)$. We write $A_L \definedas \dualof{L} / L$ for the discriminant group, $q_L: A_L \to \QQ/2\ZZ$ for the discriminant quadratic form, and $\ell(L) \definedas \ell(A_L) = \rank_{\FF_2} A_L$ for the length of the discriminant group.
For group actions, we denote $\Orth(L)$ for the orthogonal group of lattice $L$, $\discriminantkernel{L} = \ker(\Orth(L) \to \Orth(A_L))$ for the discriminant kernel, $\div_L(v)$ for the divisibility of vector $v$ in $L$, and $L[k] = \{v \in L\st  v^2 = k\}$ for vectors of squared length $k$.
In applications to K3 and Enriques surfaces, $\lkt$ denotes the K3 lattice, $(r,a,\delta)$ are the classification invariants for 2-elementary lattices, $\ien$ is the Enriques involution, and $\fentwo$ is the moduli space of Enriques surfaces with polarization of degree 2.
