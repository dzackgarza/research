### dlt Models

Let $T$ be an even lattice of signature $(2, n)$, and let $\eta \in T$ be a primitive isotropic vector.
The boundary lattice associated to $\eta$ is
$$
\bdlattice{T}{\eta} \da  \eta^{\perp_T} / \gens{\eta}
$$
which has signature $(1, n-1)$ and admits a root system $\Phi(\bdlattice{T}{\eta})$.
For an arithmetic subgroup $\Gamma \leq \Orth(T)$, the stable boundary group at $\eta$ is
$$
\Gamma_\eta \da  \Stab_\Gamma(\eta)/U_\eta
$$
where $U_\eta$ is the maximal unipotent normal subgroup of $\Stab_\Gamma(\eta)$ that acts trivially on $\bdlattice{T}{\eta}$.
The group $W(\Gamma_\eta) \leq \Orth(\bdlattice{T}{\eta})$ generated by reflections in roots stabilized by $\Gamma_\eta$ defines the stable reflection group, and the associated Coxeter chamber $\thecone{C}(\Gamma_\eta) \subset \bdlattice{T}{\eta, \RR}$ is determined by the set of simple roots for $W(\Gamma_\eta)$ (see [@AEGS25]).

Let $\thecone{C}_\eta$ be the open positive cone associated to a cusp $\eta$.
Any rational polyhedral fan $\Sigma(\eta)$ supported on the rational closure $\thecone{C}_{\eta, \QQ}$ that is $\Gamma_\eta$-invariant determines the structure of the toroidal compactification of $\FG = \dmodgamma{ \halfpd{T} }{ \Gamma }$, with the Coxeter fan subdividing $\thecone{C}_{\eta, \QQ}$ into chambers indexed by $W(\Gamma_\eta)$ [@AEGS25].

#### Boundary Models: Stable Involution Pairs and Quotients

::: {.definition title="$(K+D)$-Trivial Polarized Involution Pairs"}
A $(K+D)$-trivial polarized involution pair is a triple $(X, D, \iota)$, where $X$ is a normal surface, $D \subset X$ a reduced effective divisor, and $\iota: X \to X$ is an involution satisfying $\iota(D) = D$.
The canonical class and divisor are related by $K_X + D \sim 0$, and the fixed locus of $\iota$ consists of an ample Cartier divisor $R$, possibly with isolated fixed points.
For all $0 < \eps \ll 1$, the pair $(X, D + \eps R)$ is log canonical.
Each irreducible component occurring in stable degenerations of K3 surfaces with nonsymplectic involution is locally modeled by such a triple.
:::

Given such a pair, the finite quotient $\pi: X \to Y = X/\iota$ has image divisor $C = \pi(D)$ and branch divisor $B = \pi(R) \in |-2(K_Y + C)|$.
The pair $(Y, C)$ is a log del Pezzo surface of index at most 2, and $(Y, C + \frac{1+\eps}{2} B)$ is log canonical for $\eps > 0$.
There is an exact relation
$$
K_X + D + \eps R = \pi^*\left( K_Y + C + \frac{1+\eps}{2}B \right).
$$
This induces a bijection between $(K+D)$-trivial involution pairs and such log del Pezzo pairs with branch data.
For instance, in the boundary analysis of $\fent$, the image pairs $(Y, C)$ arising in this way include the rational log del Pezzo surfaces of type $A_n$, $D_n$, $E_n$, and their foldings, as classified by the corresponding diagrams in @AEGS25.
