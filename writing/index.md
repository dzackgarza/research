This volume is one research program in two halves.
The first half is the theory the second one evaluates: categories and their limits, bilinear and quadratic forms, integral lattices with their discriminant forms, and the arithmetic invariants that separate a lattice from its genus.
The second half is the Coble--Enriques program: the moduli spaces, the K3 covers and their lattices, the reflection groups and degenerations that make up the boundary, and its results.
