This volume is one research program in two halves.
The first half is the theory the second one evaluates: categories and their limits, bilinear and quadratic forms, integral lattices with their discriminant forms, and the arithmetic invariants that separate a lattice from its genus.
The second half is the Coble--Enriques program: the moduli spaces, the K3 covers that carry their lattice theory, the reflection groups and degenerations that make up the boundary, and the results obtained so far, with the open problems and the computations that produced them kept in their own parts.

The chapter list below is the reading order, and it is the only statement of it: a page's position claims that everything it uses is defined above it, so a page that is not in the list is not published at all.
The parts are separated by subject, not by status: a page that is conjectural says so in its front matter and sits with the research programs, and a computational page records whether it is a reusable method or the output of one.

Three manuscripts under `writing/` are not part of this site and are not listed here: the dissertation, the research statement, and the talks.
They build through `~/.pandoc` rather than through Quarto, so their sources are edited in place and are rendered by their own recipes.
