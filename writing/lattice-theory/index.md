# Integral lattices and their arithmetic {#sec-lattice-theory}

Throughout, $R$ is a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, and the main case is $R=\bZ$ with $K=\bQ$; a statement that names $\bZ$ is not available for a general base.
The sign convention is that of @conv:root-sign, and $L^\#$ and $A_L^\sharp$ are as in @def:metric-dual.
