# Basic invariants of a lattice {#sec-basic-invariants}

Throughout, $R$ is a Dedekind domain with fraction field $K$, the main case being $R=\bZ$ and $K=\bQ$, and $L\in\mathbf{Lat}_R$ is a lattice as in @def:lattice.

::: {.notation #def:lattice-basic-invariants title="Norm and norm shells"}

Let $L$ be an integral lattice.
The *norm* of $v\in L$ is the value of the form at the diagonal, $\beta_L(v,v)$; the notation $q_L$ is reserved for the quadratic discriminant form of @def:discriminant.
For $a\in\bZ$ the *norm shell* of $a$ is the set of vectors of that norm,
$$
L[a]\definedas\theset{v\in L\st \beta_L(v,v)=a}\subseteq L.
$$
When $L$ is even (@def:even-lattice) every norm is even, so $L[a]$ is empty for odd $a$.
:::

::: {.definition #def:lattice-divisibility title="Divisibility of a vector"}

Let $v\in L$.
The *divisibility ideal* of $v$ is the image of the functional $\beta_L(v,-)$,
$$
I_v\definedas\operatorname{im}\qty{\beta_L(v,-)\colon L\longrightarrow R}\triangleleft R.
$$
For $R=\bZ$ it is generated by a positive integer, the *divisibility* of $v$,
$$
\div_L(v)\definedas\gcd\theset{\beta_L(v,x)\st x\in L},
\qquad
I_v=\div_L(v)\bZ,
$$
so $\div_L(v)$ is the largest integer by which every value $\beta_L(v,x)$ is divisible.
:::

::: {.definition #def:lattice-discriminant-class title="Discriminant class of a vector"}

Let $L\in\mathbf{Lat}_{\bZ}^{\mathrm{nd}}$ be integral and let $0\ne v\in L$.
The *dual vector* of $v$ is
$$
v^*\definedas\tfrac{1}{\div_L(v)}\,v\in L^\#,
$$
which lies in the metric dual by @def:lattice-divisibility: every value $\beta_{L}(v^*,x)=\beta_L(v,x)/\div_L(v)$ is an integer.
The *discriminant class* of $v$ is its image
$$
[v^*]\definedas\pi_L(v^*)\in A_L^\sharp
$$
under the cokernel morphism $\pi_L\colon L^\#\twoheadrightarrow A_L^\sharp$ of @def:discriminant.
:::
