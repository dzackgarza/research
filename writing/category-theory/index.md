# Categorical and arithmetic foundations

## The category and functor diagram

Categories, functors and invariant maps from modules, through the form categories, to
integral lattices and their discriminant and genus invariants. The discriminant
construction is drawn on category cores. The genus is the fibre of the displayed map on
isometry classes over the image of a lattice class.
