# Categorical and arithmetic foundations

Categorical and arithmetic foundations support the lattice research code, its Sage implementation, and its Lean formalization.

**Foundations.** Higher categories and universes, categorical foundations, loops and suspension, identification.

**Properties, structures, and algebra.** Truncation and classifiers, the mathematical framework, modules and forms, joins meets and closure, distinguished functors, elements and containment.

**Forms and lattices.** Bilinear and quadratic forms, bilinear forms on algebra modules, lattices and discriminant forms, isometries and arithmetic invariants.

**Realizations.** The Lean and Sage integration model, the category graph, the Sage category framework inventory and classes, the categorical DSL.

**Authoring and reference.** The mathematical language style guide, and the sources the framework draws on.

**Realizations.** The Lean and Sage realizations of this mathematics, and the category and functor diagram.
