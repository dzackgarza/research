## Coble geometry and moduli

### CDL25

**Cossec, Dolgachev, and Liedtke. *Enriques Surfaces I*.** [Zotero record](zotero://select/library/items/V69CMFHT). The attached PDF is the April 2024 draft.

A main reference for the Coble moduli construction, its relation to Enriques moduli, and the table of Coble lattices.

- [§5.2, Lattice Polarized K3 Surfaces — PDF p. 545](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/IPZVZIB4?page=545).
- [§5.4, Moduli of Coble Surfaces of K3 Type — PDF p. 566](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/IPZVZIB4?page=566).
- [Table 5.1, Coble lattices — printed p. 553, PDF p. 569](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/IPZVZIB4?page=569).

### DK25

**Dolgachev and Kondō. *Enriques Surfaces II*.** [Zotero record](zotero://select/library/items/8ER9XSAF). Links refer to the attached draft.

The principal book-length treatment of rational Coble surfaces used in the project, including their K3 covers and Coble–Mukai lattices.

- [§9.1, Rational Coble Surfaces of K3 Type — PDF p. 293](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/V3WS4ENC?page=293).
- [§9.2, Coble–Mukai Lattice — PDF p. 302](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/V3WS4ENC?page=302).
- [§9.6, Automorphisms of Unnodal Coble Surfaces — PDF p. 343](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/V3WS4ENC?page=343).

- [§2, Halphen surfaces — PDF p. 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/NHGMTEJQ?page=6).
