# Coble surfaces {#sec-coble-surfaces}

This part is the Coble--Enriques program: the moduli spaces it compactifies, the lattices whose arithmetic classifies their boundary strata, and the results obtained so far.
It starts from the three surface classes the construction needs and the K3 cover that carries every lattice-theoretic statement afterwards, then treats the Coble surfaces themselves, the polarizations and period domains, the reflection groups and Coxeter systems that produce the boundary, the Kulikov and stable-pair degenerations, and the cusp correspondence between the Enriques and Coble sides.
The last parts collect the results for $F_{\mathrm{En},2}$, the computational methods beside the results they produced, and everything still conjectural.

The pages are the paper: one topic per page, with no separate notes tree, and the last dated PDF builds sit in `coble/reference/` as a record rather than as a build target.
Front matter states what a page is: `unit: method` marks a reusable computation, the pages beside it are its output, and `status: conjectural` marks a research program rather than a result.
The lattice theory these pages evaluate is the preceding part; nothing here is stated about a lattice that is not defined there or on the page itself.
