# Review mihf item 2: conditional status cleanup

*The remaining hypotheses, excluding descent.* Clause 1 needs $\Aut$ of a labelled object to be exactly $\{\id,\iota\}$, which is a genericity statement at a special member strictly larger (@thm:sign-is-isotropy's normaliser and the plane-model image of 18c07a9cd7); it needs no object with $n>1$ anti-bicanonical components, since @lem:every-object-of-the-functor-is-labellable requires connectedness of $C$ and the functor does not impose it; and it needs openness, nonemptiness and connectedness of $\mcM^{\mathrm{gen}}$ and $U^{\mathrm{gen}}$, none of which is proved. The descent formerly called (v) adds no hypothesis: $\qty[\widetilde\Orth^+(T_\Co)\backslash U^{\mathrm{gen}}]$ is a stack with isotropy, and @thm:quotient-local-system-descent proves that its tautological system is a local system with the prescribed $2$-morphism comparisons and cocycles. A generic open substack is also not the owner's object: @def:gamma-co-groups(1) defines $\Gamma_\Co$ on the universal family over the whole stack. So this theorem is a statement about $\mcM^{\mathrm{gen}}$ and about nothing else, and @conj:gamma-co-orbifold-equality remains the global statement with the preceding hypotheses open.
