# Review mihf item 1: pair homotopy, endpoints and transport

A Noohi homotopy pulls back to a principal bundle on $I^2$, which again has a global section by finite-grid trivializations. The endpoint element is now a continuous function $u\mapsto g(u)$, and this varying arrow is exactly what the deformation of [@Col09, Definitions 3.2--3.3] permits. If $G$ is path connected, use the pair construction directly and do not change the section. Choose $\beta\colon I\to G$ with $\beta(0)=e$ and $\beta(1)=g$, and put $\sigma_u(t)=\beta(1-ut)\tilde x$, so that $\sigma_u$ runs from $g\tilde x$ to $\beta(1-u)\tilde x$. The family $\Theta_u=(\beta(1-u),\widetilde\gamma\cdot\sigma_u)$ is a homotopy of pairs. At $u=0$, $\sigma_0$ is the constant path $g\tilde x$ and $\Theta_0=(g,\widetilde\gamma)$; at $u=1$, $\Theta_1=(e,\widetilde\gamma\cdot\sigma_1)$ is a loop of $U$ at $\tilde x$. The path $\beta$ supplies a section of the pullback along $t\mapsto\beta(t)\tilde x$ by $t\mapsto\varphi_{\beta(t)}(\tilde x)(x)$ for $x$ in the fibre at $\tilde x$, so transport along $t\mapsto\beta(t)\tilde x$ is $\varphi_g(\tilde x)$. Reversing that path gives $\sigma_1^*=\varphi_g(\tilde x)^{-1}$, and composition yields
$$\sigma_1^*\circ\widetilde\gamma^*=\varphi_g(\tilde x)^{-1}\circ\widetilde\gamma^*,$$
exactly the initial transport. This is the statement of clause 3; no Noohi covering hypothesis is asserted for the principal bundle.
