Research #799, open item (1) only. Realize the reflection in
  delta_c = 2(H - E_1 - E_2 - E_3)
on M = H^2(X)^iota by a plane quadratic Cremona transformation at one
general point of the labelled ten-nodal-sextic base B. The three lines
through pairs of p_1,p_2,p_3 must give disjoint smooth (-1)-curves on the
blowup, and the image sextic must be irreducible with exactly ten ordinary
nodes; in particular a tangency condition must be proved, not inferred
from divisor arithmetic. One general point suffices. Do not assemble the
classical monodromy theorem in this turn and do not treat the widened stack.
