# K1 Cremona realization

Objects: a labelled irreducible ten-nodal plane sextic Z, its nodes p_i,
the blowup S of P^2 at all ten nodes, and the standard quadratic map q at
p_1,p_2,p_3. Done means one general member for which q gives an actual
second plane presentation and q(Z) is ten-nodal.

Required local condition: on every fundamental line L_ij, the two base
points each have local intersection multiplicity 2 with Z and the
remaining degree-two intersection divisor is reduced and disjoint from
the base points. Then the proper transform of Z meets that of L_ij in two
simple points away from all exceptional divisors. After the opposite
blowdown these give two branches with distinct tangent directions, hence
an ordinary node.

General position: the nonsingular irreducible Severi locus is Harris--
Morrison Theorem (1.49); general maps have surjective Kodaira--Spencer map
by Corollary (3.42), and Lemma (3.44) analyzes tangency deformations. On
P^1 the normal bundle has degree 16, leaving enough sections after fixing
finitely many node preimages to make the finitely many bad tangency and
incidence conditions proper.

Divisor frame: A = 2H-E_1-E_2-E_3, G_12 = H-E_1-E_2 and its cyclic
analogues, G_k = E_k for k >= 4. The three G_ij are the strict transforms
of the fundamental lines; they are smooth, disjoint and square -1. The
other old nodes are isomorphically transferred because they avoid the
fundamental lines.

Scope: item (2), the assembly using #804, is explicitly deferred by the
user. The widened stack remains excluded.
