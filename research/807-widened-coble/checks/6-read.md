## Statement and cases

The statement quantifies over every complex Coble surface S, in Dolgachev–Zhang's sense, for which |-2K_S|={C} with C one connected smooth curve. It does not include the empty system (already impossible for a Coble surface), a reduced but singular member, or a system whose unique member is non-reduced. Classical means existence of a plane presentation by ten distinct points of P^2 at which the plane sextic has ordinary nodes. Z_8 and Z_64 are checked separately, then the E_8 proposition handles every surface carrying that configuration, so non-classicality is not inferred from infinitely near centres alone.

## Surface reduction

Dolgachev–Zhang Lemma 1.2(1) gives p_a(C)=0. Adjunction gives C^2=-4 and K_S^2=-1. Smoothness implies reducedness, hence K3 type by Definition 6.1 and the normal K3 cover by Lemma 6.2. Proposition 6.4(2) gives terminality. Theorem 6.3 excludes rational type and Theorem 4.3 gives a plane blow-down. The total-transform calculation applies at infinitely near centres. The plane image has arithmetic genus 10 and geometric genus 0, not arithmetic genus 0.

## Plane models and both examples

At every successive centre the class calculation gives multiplicity two. Exhaustion of all intermediate singular points uses smoothness of C. Conversely, each double-point blow-up lowers delta by one, so ten blow-ups resolve an admissible rational sextic; C=-2K, C^2=-4, and the anti-canonical system is empty.

The exact affine substitution was recomputed for both examples. Absolute irreducibility follows because P is not a square in C(w); the residual conics certify geometric genus 0. The restriction to z=0 is x^6, and the only infinity point is o. Both A_15 resolutions give R_i=E_i-E_{i+1}; the line class is H-E_1-E_2-E_3. Their dual graph is E_8.

## E_8 obstruction

The 240 roots represent the 120 nonzero classes with q=1 in E_8/2E_8, proving the modulo-two root lemma. The negative E_8 block is unimodular, so Pic(S) splits. Ten disjoint exceptional classes give a repeated mod-two component in rank three. The lifted construction gives Gamma^2=-2; Riemann–Roch makes Gamma or -Gamma effective. Pushforward then gives E_j+pi_*G=E_i as divisors, impossible for the unique effective representative E_i. This excludes every ten-distinct-point presentation, not only the original one.

## Open, dense, connected

Grivaux Proposition 3.1 applies on the free PGL_3 locus; freeness for the ten-node configurations follows from the line-plus-point fixed-locus argument and Bezout. Completeness proves openness of distinct centres and nonzero Hessians. Huybrechts Ch.6 Corollary 2.7, Remark 2.6, Proposition 2.8 and §2.4 supply the universal deformation, involution on its base, local period isomorphism and (1,1)-hyperplanes. The relative quotient construction produces the required family over the fixed locus. The bounded-square argument proves local finiteness of the (-4) and (-6) hyperplanes; Baire gives dense Picard-general fibres, and part 1 makes these classical. Harris–Morrison §6.E, Initial reductions and (6.45), gives full symmetric node monodromy, hence path connectedness of the labelled classical locus. Thus non-classical objects are limits, not another component.

## Source and monodromy boundary

Dolgachev–Zhang Theorem 6.7 is only a negative-curve-orbit theorem with a further invariant-Picard generality assumption. The non-classical examples fail that assumption. Example 6.10 has a nonempty but non-reduced unique anti-bicanonical divisor and is excluded. No identity with the classical quotient is asserted. The W(E_10) statement concerns the invariant-lattice transport/isomorphism aggregate, not automatically universal-stack monodromy and not Gamma_Co on the anti-invariant lattice.
