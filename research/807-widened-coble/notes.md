# Notes

## 1. Exact object and what counts as done

Over C, an object is a Coble surface S in the sense of Dolgachev–Zhang, Introduction, for which |-2K_S|={C} with C one connected smooth curve, together with its anti-bicanonical double cover and covering involution. Classical means that some plane presentation is the blow-up of P^2 at ten distinct points that are ordinary nodes of an irreducible plane sextic. A response must decide existence of non-classical objects, decide whether they form an additional connected or irreducible component of the cover-retaining stack, and must not replace stack monodromy by a plane-model group.

## 2. Source audit

Dolgachev–Zhang 6.1 defines K3-type by reducedness. Lemma 6.2 gives the normal K3 double cover. Theorem 6.3 excludes rational type for every reduced-member surface. Proposition 6.4(2) characterizes terminal surfaces by a unique reduced disjoint union of (-4)-curves. Theorem 6.5 gives the fibre conditions for K3-type; Theorem 6.6 constructs a non-minimal rational Jacobian elliptic double cover in a non-K3-type case. Theorem 6.7 is a finiteness theorem for general terminal elliptic K3-type surfaces, not a plane-model or moduli theorem. Example 6.10 has unique anti-bicanonical divisor R_1+...+R_4+2R_5, non-reduced, so it is outside #807 although its anti-bicanonical system is nonempty.

## 3. Proposed decision to check

The current main-line page claims the third alternative: non-classical objects exist (Z_8 and Z_64), but classical objects are open, dense, and connected, hence there is no additional component. Existence uses explicit sextics and an E_8 obstruction. Density uses the nine-dimensional fixed-involution deformation and Noether–Lefschetz hypersurfaces. Connectedness of the classical locus uses Harris, Lemma (1.2), whose full symmetric monodromy on the nodes was read, and Harris–Morrison §6.E, Initial reductions and Uniform position principle (6.45).

## 4. Monodromy boundary

If no further component exists, there is no new component whose monodromy is to be computed. The unconditional aggregate generated by transports in arbitrary families and by isomorphisms of objects on the invariant lattice is W(E_10), but it is not to be identified with the monodromy of the universal stack local system without the stack chart/descent inputs. The anti-invariant stack group Gamma_Co is a separate open problem.
