# research#788 (G4), original question and acceptance

Does every isometry of T_Co extend to an isometry of T_En that fixes the (-2)-vector alpha with T_Co = alpha^perp? And is the image R in O(T_Co) of Stab_{Gamma_{En,2}}(alpha) the preimage rho^{-1}(P) of P, the image of Gamma_{En,2} in O(q_{T_En}) = O(q_{T_Co}), with [O(T_Co) : R] = 2^7 * 17 * 31?

This is statement G4 of research#786, Part I §3. Uses: Nikulin 1979, 3.6.2, 3.6.3, 1.14.4, 1.5.2 (as cited in AEGS, Nagoya Math. J. 259 (2025), Lemma 2.4, and DK13 p. 513).

A page proof exists: prop:gamma-co-images (writing/coble/lattices-and-moduli/lattices.md). The acceptance is that it be read against Nikulin's cited statements (by numbered statement, hypotheses checked: T_Co even, indefinite, 2-elementary), each step proved on the page or cited, and the index 2^7 * 17 * 31 either proved on the page or a certified card value; or that the page proof be refuted and the correct statement put on the page. G5 (research#779) and B2 cite the read statement.

Later owner clarification, issue comment 7033502405: the acceptance asks for each Nikulin statement by number, with its hypotheses checked; a printed page is not part of a citation. Latest owner comment 7136466479: the original G4 proof and consumer citation obligation do not require proving G5 or geometric monodromy equality.
