# Special lattices and arithmetic transformations {#sec:special-lattices}

::: {.proposition #prop:special-lattice-relations title="Standard special lattice relations"}

Let $E_{10}\definedas U\perp E_8$ be the Enriques lattice of @def:enriques-lattice.
Then @prop:doubled-enriques-lattice gives
$E_{10}(2)\isoto U(2)\perp E_8(2)$.
Additivity of the discriminant cokernel gives
$A_{E_{10}(2)}\isoto A_{U(2)}\perp A_{E_8(2)}$.

For $d>0$, let $L_{2d}$ be the degree-$2d$ K3 lattice of @def:degree-2d-k3-lattice.
The orthogonal-complement calculation of @prop:degree-2d-k3-complement gives
$L_{2d}\isoto\generators{-2d}\perp U^{\perp2}\perp E_8^{\perp2}$.
:::
