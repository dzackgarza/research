# Lattice Theory {#sec:lattice-theory}

::: {.convention #conv:integral-lattice-specialization title="Integral lattice specialization"}

In this chapter the base ring and fraction field in @def:lattice and @def:metric-dual are
$$
R=\bZ,
\qquad
K=\bQ.
$$
Thus an unqualified lattice is an object of
$$
\mathbf{Lat}_{\bZ},
$$
and scalar extensions are formed from this integral object.
:::
