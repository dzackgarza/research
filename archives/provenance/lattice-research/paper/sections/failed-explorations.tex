\section{Failed Explorations}

Failed explorations are preserved when they teach mathematics: a false conjecture, a
proof gap, an exhausted search range, a missing-source result, or a failed
computational strategy. Broken code is fixed or deleted; the mathematical lesson is
recorded here or in a workstream report.

\begin{claimledger}
\claimitem{F-template}{Failure record template}{State the attempted strategy, exact
failure point, evidence, and what future work should avoid or try next.}{Linked
workstream report, computation, review finding, or source search.}
\end{claimledger}
