\section{Workstreams}

Workstreams are branch-level investigations attached to approved goals. Each
workstream has a phase card with \texttt{phaseKind: workstream}, a report artifact, and
an agent roster.\workstreamnote{See workstream reports and the owning phase card.}

\begin{claimledger}
\claimitem{WS-intake}{Intake branch}{Refines the research question, goals, non-goals,
hard constraints, and initial branches before execution.}{Plan frontmatter
\texttt{intakeStatus}; onboarding artifact.}
\claimitem{WS-source}{Source branch}{Finds exact theorem statements, definitions,
hypotheses, and Sage/source owners before claims enter specs or proofs.}{Source
workstream report and theory references.}
\claimitem{WS-computation}{Computation branch}{Runs bounded exact computations or
experiments to build intuition and produce replayable witnesses.}{Computation report,
tests, notebooks, or code artifacts.}
\claimitem{WS-review}{Review branch}{Checks reports, claims, citations, computations,
and logical steps before promotion.}{Review log and unresolved-objection notes.}
\end{claimledger}
