# Research Relevance Check

This repo exists to advance mathematical research through a reliable semantic
implementation layer.

Before continuing artifact-heavy or engineering-heavy work, answer:

1. What mathematical object, operation, invariant, morphism, proof obligation, or
   computation is advanced?
2. What exact source file, spec method, proof note, or backend bridge is affected?
3. Why is this necessary now for the current phase?
4. What would be lost mathematically if this work were skipped?
5. Is the work preventing known bad research drift, or merely producing success-shaped
   output?

If the answers are vague, stop.
Do not create another artifact.
Read source/math or perform cleanup that reduces artifact load.
