# Fixtures

`coble_literature_fixtures.json` — literature-extracted properties of the K3
lattice, the AEGS invariant/coinvariant pairs, the Coble surface definitions,
and the record `T_En_cusp_orbits_sterk` (Sterk 1991 / AEGS §3.2: the two
0-cusps of O(T_En), separated by divisibility 1 and 2, and the two 1-cusps,
with explicit representatives in the U ⊕ U(2) ⊕ E8(2) framing). Every record
cites its sources inline (file, lines, bibliographic key). Migrated 2026-08-20
from the lattice-research corpus (`tests/fixtures/coble_literature_fixtures.json`),
sited with the spec corpora as product data for cusp/orbit specifications:
the owned surfaces these rows specify are
`Aut(L).isotropic_line_orbit_representatives()` /
`isotropic_plane_orbit_representatives()` and `div()` on lattice elements.
