research#658, item 1e: the input that is missing, and what was checked

Item 1e asks whether Gamma_Co <= ker rho_{T_Co}.  The proposal rmk:gamma-co-nodal-pair-proposal
(lattices.md) reduces it to one geometric statement and names three gaps.  This note records what
was searched for the first of them, so that the next session does not repeat the search and does
not mistake a neighbour for the input.

THE INPUT NEEDED (either of two, and only these two will do):

  (i) a printed statement, by authors, number and page, that the local monodromy of a degeneration
      of surfaces acquiring two ordinary double points at once is the product of the two
      commuting Picard--Lefschetz transformations in the vanishing classes; or
  (ii) a computed loop: an explicit family of Coble surfaces over a punctured disc whose monodromy
      on T_Co is computed to be s_e, with the computation on main.

WHAT WAS CHECKED IN tmp/sources.  Two passes: first the markdown conversions (not stubs: AEGS25 has
2432 lines, Sterk 1991 has 1526, DK13 has 499), then -- because a conversion can be partial -- every
PDF, converted with pdftotext: 28 PDFs, 28 conversions, all searched.

  - no occurrence of "vanishing cycle" or of a Picard--Lefschetz formula in any held conversion;
  - DK13 contains no occurrence of "monodromy" at all -- it computes no monodromy anywhere, so its
    Prop. 3.2 and (3.6) cannot supply (i);
  - AEGS25's Picard--Lefschetz material (its lines 1036-1060) is about the Picard--Lefschetz
    transforms of Kulikov degenerations of K3 surfaces and their combinatorial types; it is not a
    statement about a family of Coble surfaces acquiring two ordinary nodes at once;
  - the derivation that computations/reports/smwo_direct-period.md cites for the two-node product,
    .ultramath/smw0o7jbf/m5/A20-direct-period-theorem.md sec. 6.1(b), is not on main
    (`git cat-file -e omm/main:...` fails), so it is not an input the book may cite.

The full-PDF pass adds the following, and it is the strongest form of the negative available here:
seven held documents contain a Picard--Lefschetz formula at all -- Sterk's 1988 thesis, Shah 1980,
Nikulin 1979, Alexeev--Engel 2022 and 2023, Alexeev--Engel--Thompson 2023, Yu--Zheng 2023 -- and
none of them states the monodromy of a degeneration acquiring two ordinary double points at once.
A search of all 28 conversions for "simultaneous", for "two nodes" beside monodromy, and for a
"product of ... reflections", returns nothing on point; the classical statements are the one-node
formula, whose hypothesis is that the critical values are distinct.

A FOURTH HELD SOURCE, CHECKED FOR THE MODULI STATEMENT: [@DZ01], Dolgachev--Zhang, Coble rational
surfaces, held as tmp/sources/dolgachev-zhang-2001-coble-rational-surfaces.pdf (2594 lines of
pdftotext).  It is the standard reference on the geometry the book's Coble surfaces belong to --
Lemma 6.2 there characterises the Coble surfaces of K3-type as those whose double cover is a K3 with
at worst ordinary double points, which is exactly the book's construction -- but it contains no
occurrence of "moduli", "period", "Torelli" or "deformation" at all: it is a classification paper,
not a moduli paper.  So it cannot supply the statement of which Heegner divisors form the
discriminant, which is what 1e now needs.

  Caveat on that source: its pdftotext conversion drops minus signs (K_X^2 = -1 prints as "KX2 =
  1"), so it is usable for word searches and not for symbol searches; nothing above relies on a
  symbol search in it.

CANDIDATES TO OBTAIN -- named as candidates, not cited, because no printed page has been seen:

  - SGA 7 II, the Picard--Lefschetz exposé (Deligne--Katz);
  - Lamotke, The topology of complex projective varieties after S. Lefschetz, Topology 20 (1981);
  - Voisin, Hodge Theory and Complex Algebraic Geometry II, sec. 3 (its Thm. 3.16 is one node,
    not two).

  None of these is held in tmp/sources.  Under the standing rule, none may be written as a citation
  until its printed page is read; naming them here is a to-do list, not a reference.

THE OTHER TWO ROUTES THAT WOULD SETTLE THE ITEM:

  - prove the containment G1 <= Gamma_Co (the proposal's gaps 2 and 3), which with the lattice
    computation would refute the conjecture; or
  - prove Gamma_Co <= O~^+(T_Co) directly, which would settle item 1e the other way and restore the
    conjecture as a theorem.  Nothing in this repository does either.

Until one of these exists on main, item 1e stays NOT MET, and no page may state that the conjecture
is false: conj:gamma-co-stable is open and rmk:gamma-co-status says so.
