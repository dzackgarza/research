ITEM 4. The base of D2b is all of Delta_2.
VERDICT: NOT MET.  The item asks for every point of Delta_2 to be such a pair and
  conversely.  The converse direction is exactly what fails at the level of the
  marked space: Delta_2 is the image of D(T_Co)/Gamma_{Co,2} under the quotient by
  R_gEn (@lem:d2-degree-two-base(2), @lem:d2b-forgets-to-d2a(4)), and the quotient
  is not an isomorphism because Gamma_{Co,2} lies in O^+(T_Co) while R_gEn does not
  (@lem:d2b-forgets-to-d2a(2)-(3), @thm:coble-monodromy-versus-enriques(2)).  The
  other direction, that every point of Delta_2 is a pair, is the surjectivity of the
  normalization map and is @lem:d2-degree-two-base(1)-(2) up to the normalization
  step, which remains U8.  So the item is NOT MET both because U8 is open and because
  the identification it states is false in the orbifold reading.
