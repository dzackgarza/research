research#797 -- the fppf projective-return attempt

RESULT
The unconditional fppf effectivity statement is NOT proved. The page now proves the exact positive criterion: a descent-datum-compatible relatively ample line bundle on the original Coble family descends and makes the descended algebraic space a projective scheme, with the anti-bicanonical double cover and involution recovered. It also proves that the anti-bicanonical line bundle is not such a polarization. The smallest remaining input is qst:coble-projective-return: existence of that compatible relatively ample bundle, equivalently projectivity of the descended family.

WHY THE ANTI-BICANONICAL BUNDLE DOES NOT SUPPLY THE INPUT
On each Coble fibre, K_S^2=-1 and C=-2K_S has C^2=-4 (thm:coble-smooth-antibicanonical-is-a-plane-blowup(1)). For m nonzero, O(-mK_S) has self-intersection -m^2<0, so Nakai--Moishezon prevents ampleness; m=0 is O_S, also not ample. Thus neither O(-K_S) nor O(-2K_S) is a polarization.

WHY KNOWN DESCENT THEOREMS STOP
Stacks Lemma 37.48.2 (05WN) refines an fppf cover only by a composite of finite locally free and open morphisms; it does not produce one finite locally free cover. Stacks Lemma 39.25.3 (0CCJ) applies to a surjective finite locally free X->Y and a projective (or otherwise ample) V/X, and makes the descent datum effective as a scheme. Its conclusion does not preserve a relative ample sheaf or projectivity. At the first finite-locally-free level of the refinement the input is projective but the output's projectivity is unproved; hence the second level lacks the projective/ample hypothesis. This is the same return theorem, not a proof of it.
The counterexamples Stacks 08J0 and 08KE show that base-change stability, geometric-fibre projectivity and Zariski-local projectivity do not imply the return. The analytic fine-moduli result only gives analytic representability.

EXACT RESIDUE
qst:coble-projective-return: for every fppf cover Y'->Y over Z[1/2] and every descent datum of def:coble-moduli-functor-z, either (equivalently) the descended algebraic space of 0D1G/0ADV is a scheme projective over Y, or there is a Y'-relatively ample invertible sheaf on S' with a line-bundle descent datum compatible with the object datum. No theorem read supplies either form.
