=== part D. the volume of the restricted chamber
thm:coxeter-polytope-volume(3): the polytope has infinite volume if and only if its
closure in the projective model has a vertex outside the closed hyperbolic space, and by
(1) it has finite volume if and only if the closure is the convex hull of finitely many
of its ordinary and ideal vertices.  The vertices are the extreme rays of the cone
C = {x : (nu_i,x) >= 0}; since the walls span K, C is pointed, and every extreme ray is
cut out by an independent set of dim K - 1 = 8 walls, so the enumeration over 8-subsets
is complete.  A ray is ordinary when v^2 > 0, ideal when v^2 = 0, and outside when v^2 < 0.
