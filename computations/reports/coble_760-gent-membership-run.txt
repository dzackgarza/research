=== verdict
13 and 14 are Gamma_Co,2-equivalent: the witness is in Gent.
12 and 13 are in one O(T_En)-orbit, but the witness is not in Gent, so the Gamma_Co,2-orbit
question for the pair (12,13) is OPEN, and the vacuity argument of bdb78a77ce does not hold:
it needs all three roots in one Gamma_Co,2-orbit, and 12 is not shown to be.

Because every reflection in a printed root is in Gent and the 12 <-> 13 witness is not, that
witness is not in the group those fourteen reflections generate; the depth-3 class counts of

What decides the pair: with g any isometry of T_En carrying r12 to r13, the class of r12 is
Gamma_Co,2-equivalent to that of r13 exactly when the image of g in O(A_{T_En}, q) lies in
M . rho(Stab_O(r12) cap Stab_O(Z eta_5)) --- a coset condition in the finite group O(q).  This
record settles the coset for the two candidates it has; evaluating it needs generators of that
stabilizer's image.  That object is Stab_O(r12) cap Stab_O(delta) cap Stab_O(Z eta_5): the
library builds the intersection as a predicate but does not resolve it into generators, and its
group_generators() raises 'has no chosen resolution relative to Category of groups'.  With those
generators the same subspace-orbit test decides the pair; coble_760-transporter-in-gent-run.txt
runs it for the stabilizer of r12 alone, which is where the pair turns out to be Gent-equivalent.
