Acceptance 1, stable-group part. (C3') of tbl-o2-ledger -- a complete integral family for Stab_{Otilde}(L)
and for its three character subgroups -- is NOT MET. What is proved: lem:o2-level-two-membership (the
four level-two membership laws), thm:o2-stable-line(1)-(3) (the semidirect structure
Stab_Otilde(L) = (id_P perp Gamma_K(2)) x 2K_ev, the unipotent factor generated by nine explicit
transvections, the three character subgroups as index-two kernels with explicit character witnesses),
thm:o2-stable-line(4) (all four discriminant images trivial, so the image side of the stable level is
closed), and thm:o2-stable-line(6) (the stable-group family is equivalent to (U1) modulo finitely many
explicit integral isometries, so it is not a smaller problem). What is missing is exactly
qst:o2-stable-line-family: whether the 35 elements r_dj, t_K(e,2dj), t_K(f,2dj), r_(e+dj), r_(f+dj)
generate Gamma_K(2) = ker(Orth(I_1,8) -> Orth(I_1,8/2)). rmk:o2-stable-line-level-four records the
necessary condition at level four (the 35 span 21 of the 45 admissible dimensions; the extra element
r_(e+d_1+d_2) r_(e+d_1-d_2) lies inside the span), which neither proves nor refutes it.

So acceptance 1 remains NOT MET, on two items: the plane family of R_Gamma ((U2)) and the line family,
now for O it is (U1) and for Otilde it is (U1'), the two being equivalent modulo explicit finite data.
The orbit counts of thm:o2-lines and thm:o2-planes are unchanged and are not at issue here.
