**The chain, each step with its source, all established:**

1. @lem:face-at-H (the-heegner-line :621-:639): at every member, the only effective `(-2)`-wall
   through `H_b` is `alpha_b^perp` (its proof uses only that
   `NE-bar(\hat X_b) cap H_b^perp = R_{>=0} alpha_b`, proved there from the resolution's structure);
   and `H_b.gamma > 0` off that ray.
2. The distance bound (m4's Theorem CH.2, verified here): every effective root
   `delta != +-alpha_b` has `H_b.delta in Z \ {0}` (pairing of integral classes), hence
   `dist(H_b, delta^perp) = |H_b.delta| / sqrt(-delta^2) >= 1/sqrt 2` for EVERY such delta. So no
   effective wall approaches `H_b` at all, at any member. The corpus's hedge in
   `the-heegner-line:638` ("a (-2)-class with `H_b.delta != 0` still has its wall crossing every
   neighbourhood of `H_b` if the walls accumulate there") cannot occur; @prop:chamber-pinned's
   hypothesis is automatic.
3. @prop:chamber-pinned (the-heegner-line :641-:649) then holds unconditionally: `c_b` is the ONLY
   component of the positive cone minus the effective walls whose closure contains `H_b` and which
   is `alpha_b`-positive. So `c` is a function of `(H_b, alpha_b)`, at every member, at the
   corpus's own chamber semantics.
4. Stabiliser invariance. Let `g` be a label stabiliser (any O(L_{K3})-element commuting with the
   two involutions and fixing `H` and `alpha`; @def:direct-group's exact definition). Then `g` maps
   effective-root chambers to effective-root chambers, preserves the sheet of the positive cone
   containing `H`, and preserves alpha-positivity; so `g c` is again an `alpha`-positive component
   with `H` in its closure, which by step 3's uniqueness is `c` itself: `g c = c`. No substitution
   of a weaker chamber or of another group is made anywhere.
5. Therefore the extra condition defining @def:direct-group is vacuous:
   `Z_{O(LK3)}(I_Co, I_dP)_{H,alpha,c} = Z_{O(LK3)}(I_Co, I_dP)_{H,alpha}`, and the two images
   coincide: `Gamma^dir_{Co,2} = Gamma_L` — under the corpus's own reading of item 6 with the
   `chamber-not-transported` semantics, at every tuple realisation at which steps 1-4 apply (with
   the H-null-effectivity analysis of the dossier's Lemma 4 extending step 3 from members to the
   rest of `O`). This is a lattice-level statement at @def:direct-group's own definition (whose own
   proviso "provided the labeled rigidity theorem holds" is thereby not needed for the group
   equality; the rigidity input enters only comparisons with the geometric group, where it belongs).
