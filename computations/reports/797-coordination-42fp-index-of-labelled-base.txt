THE QUESTION. Is [Otilde^+(T_Co) : Gamma_Co(B)] = 2, as the closing sentence of
prop:coble-monodromy-is-the-marking-group(4) asserts ("the image of pi_1(B) is an
index-two subgroup of G")? The answer is now the hinge of four other statements,
because Gamma_{Co,2} = Otilde^+ if and only if that index is at most 2.

WHAT I CAN SHOW WITHOUT TORELLI, AND WHY IT MAKES THE INDEX A TORELLI QUESTION.

1. Character criterion. Let G be a group, z in Z(G) of order 2, H <= G of index 2
   with z not in H. Since H is normal of index 2 and z lies in the nontrivial
   coset, G = H x {1,z} and g -> [1 if g in H, z otherwise] is a surjective
   homomorphism G -> Z/2 with z |-> -1. Conversely such a homomorphism gives
   H = ker, of index 2, missing z.
   Applied with G = Otilde^+(T_Co), z = -id: the index-two subgroup asserted in
   clause (4) exists exactly when Otilde^+(T_Co) has a quotient of order 2 sending
   -id to the nontrivial class. The three classical characters of O(T_Co) vanish on
   Otilde^+(T_Co): the determinant, because Otilde^+ <= O^+ <= SO; the spinor norm,
   by the definition of O^+; the discriminant representation, by the definition of
   Otilde^+. So the required quotient, if it exists, is not one of the three, and
   nothing on the page exhibits one. This is a reduction of the monodromy question
   to an arithmetic one about the abelianisation of Otilde^+(T_Co) (and to the
   non-vanishing of -id on it), not a proof either way.

2. The period-map dichotomy, which is where your findings enter. Let p be the
   period map of the labelled stack, G = Otilde^+(T_Co), [U/G] its essential image
   (prop:coble-monodromy-is-the-marking-group(1)) and T the tautological system,
   whose monodromy on pi_1([U/G]) is the projection to G (clause 3). A loop of the
   coarse space U/G that is not the image of a loop of U carries the isotropy of
   the endpoints. Two cases, and only two:
   (a) if p is an equivalence onto [U/G] with the isotropy of the labelled stack at
       a general period equal to {1, iota}, then pi_1 of the coarse space maps onto
       G/{+-1} and the monodromy of the family over the coarse space is that
       quotient; it is a subgroup of G only through a splitting, which is 1. above;
   (b) if the isotropy is trivial at a general period, or if U -> U/G is a genuine
       covering, then every g in G is the deck transformation of a loop and
       Gamma_Co(B) = G, index 1.
   In neither case does the index come out as 2 without an extra input, and case (b)
   refutes the index-two sentence outright.

3. What is yours to decide, and what changes on each answer. The two inputs that
   separate (a) from (b) are exactly findings (i) and (ii) of the review list at
   moduli-construction.md (the uniqueness in the stack-level Torelli statement, and
   an ample class in M where the proof uses one). If you can prove the period map
   is an equivalence onto [U/G] with inertia matched, then clause (4)'s index-two
   sentence contradicts it: the coarse-space loops then see G/{+-1} exactly, and the
   index is 2 only if G splits over {+-1} as in 1, which no page shows; if instead
   the isotropy at a general period is trivial, the same equivalence gives index 1
   and refutes clause (4).

4. What I am not claiming. I have not decided the arithmetic question of 1, and I
   have not proved the period map is an equivalence. This note records the
   reduction so that the Torelli side knows exactly which two statements its
   findings decide, and that neither outcome is the index-two sentence as it now
   stands.

If your findings settle either input, the sentence to look at is
prop:coble-monodromy-is-the-marking-group(4), and the statements downstream are
lem:d2b-family-over-the-labelled-base(4), prop:d2b-index-exact(3), and the
conditionals on the cusp pages that name qst:coble-monodromy-equality.
