# The #81 fresh-context language audit: what was asked, what was available, what it found

## The instrument was supplied on 2026-11-19 and the audit was run as specified

The priming file was placed at tmp/sources/mathematical-auditor-priming.md (the vault copy of
the path below, which this VM cannot reach through the .agents symlink).  The audit was then run
as the priming's usage contract requires: a fresh-context agent that received ONLY the priming
prompt and the artifact paths -- no summary of the text under audit -- and returned five flags
plus a reach list of the terms it verified as standard.  Its dispositions, each fixed at the
source or rejected with a citation:

  F1 "horizontal folding" (register note title and body).  UPHELD as coinage: a folding of a
     Coxeter diagram is folding by a diagram automorphism with orbit sums as folded roots
     [@Bou08, Ch. 6 Sec. 4], and "horizontal" belongs to period maps, not to diagrams.  FIXED:
     the note is retitled "The folding conjecture for theta_Co, resolved", the phrase is marked
     as quoted from the retired entry, and the note says the term is not standard.
  F2 "generator length a = 11" (proof of thm:theta-co-coordinates).  UPHELD: the invariant is
     Nikulin's a = dim_{F2} A_L, the number of generators of the discriminant group [@Nik80,
     Thm. 3.6.2]; "length" in lattice theory means the norm or a different generator count.
     FIXED to "the number a = dim_{F2} A_L = 11 of generators of the discriminant group".
     The same phrase stands in lattice-theory/classification/two-elementary-lattices.md, which
     is another unit's page; it is named in the report rather than edited here.
  F3 "component" for a coset of Orth^+ (parts 1-2 of thm:gamma-co-2-comparison and the
     comparison transcript).  UPHELD as misuse: Orth(T_Co) is discrete and the objects are the
     two cosets of the spinor kernel Orth^+(T_Co) (spinor norm: O'Meara, Introduction to Quadratic
     Forms, Ch. 5; this bibliography has no key for it, so it is named here and not cited on a
     page).  FIXED to "the condition
     phi in Orth^+(T_Co)", "the two descriptions differ by one coset", "that is two cosets of
     Orth^+(T_Co)".
  F4 "the boundary lattice of the Coble 0-cusp" (part 2 of thm:diagram-roots-coordinates).
     PARTLY UPHELD: in this corpus "boundary lattice" is eta^perp/eta for an isotropic eta, a
     different object, so the phrase collides even though the lattice meant is the source's
     T_bar_dP.  FIXED to "the lattice T_bar_dP = U + E8^2 = (18,0,0)_1 of the 0-cusp to which
     the Coble 0-cusp maps (@lem:cusp_map_dP)".
  F5 "the corner of the slot" (theta transcript, two lines).  UPHELD for "corner": a summand
     of a lattice has no corner, and the object is the vector H of square 4.  "Slot" is
     retained where it is this program's own label for the del Pezzo summand, defined at
     @prop:del-pezzo-placement.  FIXED to "the sum of the hyperbolic basis e = e_2+e_4,
     f = e_3+e_5 of the summand Delta(U_2,U_3) = U(2)".

## What was done instead, and its limits

The audit was run by hand: every noun and every numeric claim in the six theorems and the seven
records of #656 was read against the page's own definitions and against the cited sources'
statement numbers.  This is NOT a fresh-context audit -- the reader is the author of the text
under review -- and nothing in this repository should be read as claiming otherwise.

## What it found, and what was done

1. @thm:coble-explicit-sextic identified the minimal resolution of the double plane with the
   surface X of @prop:coble-cover-marking only implicitly, deriving E_i^2 = -2 by adjunction in
   that proposition's notation.  FIXED: the blowup separates the two branches at each node, the
   double cover of Bl_10 P^2 branched along the proper transform is that resolution, it is X,
   and e_0^2 = 2H^2, e_i^2 = 2E_i^2 by the projection formula.

2. @thm:chain-matrices displayed M_{En->dP} as the malformed (I_12 ; 0|-I_8) with only a
   gesture at the ordering.  FIXED: the 4 x 3 block matrix with blocks I_2, I_2, I_8, -I_8, and
   the row and column block sizes of all three matrices are printed.

## Defects found by the reviewer of record, and fixed in the same commit

3. A printed proof error, found on review: the ten-node argument in @thm:coble-explicit-sextic
   used delta_p <= m_p(m_p-1)/2, the reverse of the true inequality delta_p >= m_p(m_p-1)/2, so
   the auxiliary multiplicity argument did not stand.  REPLACED by a point-by-point decision:
   the Hessian determinant f_xx f_yy - f_xy^2 is evaluated at each of the ten points in the
   number field Q[y]/(g) for each irreducible factor g, and is nonzero on all of them, so each
   point has a nondegenerate quadratic part and hence is an ordinary node.  The record
   coble_explicit-sextic-run.txt prints the three evaluations and the count of ten.

4. A checker defect, not a lattice error: coble_a13-diagram-roots.py built its expected
   -Cartan(E8) with one orientation of each edge, so its comparison printed False while the E8
   Gram matrices were right (the block checks further down printed True).  FIXED: the expected
   matrix is built symmetrically from the Bourbaki edge list, and the record prints True.

5. A cross-reference defect: coble_a13-chain-matrices.py said ker(theta_Co + 1) where T_Co is
   ker(theta_Co - 1), and its last section cited @qst:coble-explicit-equations, an anchor that
   the sextic theorem replaced.  FIXED in both the docstring and the printed section.
