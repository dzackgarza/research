ORIGINAL STATEMENT, CLAUSE BY CLAUSE

1. Every family of classical Coble covers over a connected base has
   invariant-lattice monodromy contained in the stated finite group.
   VERDICT: the containment is MET, and the group is INFINITE, so the
   finite alternative is REFUTED. :1247-1253, using
   lem:coble-family-base-change (:1020),
   lem:pi1-of-a-groupoid-quotient-is-generated-by-three-contributions
   (:1210), lem:d2-label-lift (:1100), and
   lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families (:1134). W(E_10) = W_10
   contains the image of Aut(S_b) (rmk:coble-automorphisms-infinite(1)),
   which contains the infinite-order Bertini product
   (lem:coble-automorphism-group-is-infinite(2)), and the universal family
   has monodromy exactly W(E_10) (:1252). What is
   finite in this picture is the isotropy action on the anti-invariant
   summand T_Co (:1148, :1155), not the action on M. An earlier revision of
   this item called W(E_10) "the stated finite group"; that is the error the
   review at comment 7054280771 charges at :1148, :1155 and :1253.

2. The universal family over the classical substack has monodromy
   exactly W(E_10) = Orth^+(K^{perp M}).
   VERDICT: MET. :1247 and :1265, using
   lem:coble-image-on-m-is-the-weyl-group (:1188).

3. "Every family of Coble covers" read for the widened functor of
   def:coble-moduli-functor, including non-plane-model members.
   VERDICT: NOT MET. The quotient presentation and the atlas are proved
   only for the classical substack (:773); essential surjectivity of
   barB onto the widened class is open (rmk:coble-class-smooth-antinbicanonical).

4. The invariant local system is isomorphic to the constant system M by
   an isometry respecting the eleven classes.
   VERDICT: MET on the labelled base B (:1134(2)); NOT MET on the
   classical substack, where the monodromy is W(E_10), not the identity.

5. The monodromy on M is trivial.
   VERDICT: NOT MET; refuted by the relabelling action S_10 and the
   non-permutation isotropy action recorded at :1143-1148.

6. The monodromy on M is a stated finite group.
   VERDICT: REFUTED on the classical substack. The stated group is W(E_10)
   (:1252) and it is infinite (:1253), so the monodromy on M is not a
   finite group there either. NOT MET for the widened stack, where no group
   is computed at all (:1253).

7. Every input is cited by a numbered statement that has been read.
   VERDICT: MET for the classical-substack result. The cited statements
   are the repository labels above, Nikulin Thm. 3.6.2, Vinberg Table 5,
   Grivaux Prop. 3.1, and Noohi Definitions 13.8, 17.2, 17.5, 18.2 and
   Lemma 18.9.

3. "Or refuted with a counterexample."
   VERDICT: MET for the issue's finite-group alternative, which is the
   verdict that alternative asks for. rho is onto W(E_10) (:1252), so there
   is a loop gamma: S^1 -> M^cl_Co with rho([gamma]) = sigma_I^* sigma_J^*,
   whose characteristic polynomial (t-1)^7(t+1)^2(t^2-14t+1) is not
   cyclotomic (lem:coble-automorphism-group-is-infinite(2)), so
   rho([gamma]) has infinite order. The pullback of the universal family
   along gamma is a family of classical Coble covers over the connected
   base S^1 whose monodromy on M is of infinite order: the monodromy is
   neither trivial nor a finite group, so the issue's second clause is
   refuted and not satisfied. This is a refutation of that clause only; the
   exact-group statement of item 2 is proved, not refuted.

OVERALL: not ALL MET, and the issue's second clause is REFUTED. The
classical-substack theorem is proved (item 2): the monodromy group on M is
W(E_10), and every family of classical Coble covers over a connected base
has monodromy a subgroup of it (item 1). That group is infinite, so
"trivial or a stated finite group" fails in both of its alternatives, and a
family over S^1 refutes it outright (contract item 3); the finite-group
verdicts which an earlier revision of this report gave at items 1 and 6 were
false. The widened-functor clause of the first statement remains open
(item 3), and no independent review has returned MET.
