WHAT IS NOT MET

1. The widened stack. rmk:coble-class-smooth-antinbicanonical remains the scope
   boundary: the atlas is proved onto the classical substack only, and no
   statement here is made for Coble surfaces outside it.
2. Input (I), :1402. "The reflection group of E_10 = II_{1,9} is O^+(E_10)",
   equivalently the ten simple reflections generate it. This is the one input
   of the theorem that no numbered statement of the corpus carries.
   - [@Vin72, Table 5] does NOT carry it, and was cited for it until ed52a2cb42.
     That paper's forms are (1) -x_0^2 + x_1^2 + ... + x_n^2, n <= 17, and
     (3) -2x_0^2 + x_1^2 + ... + x_n^2, n <= 14, both odd; E_10 is even
     unimodular, and Tables 4-7 give the reflection subgroups of those odd
     forms. The corpus's other uses of that table, at prop:gamma-co-heegner-
     strict and on the-heegner-line-and-the-coble-arithmetic-group, are for the
     odd I_{1,8} and are correct.
   - Two steps are on hand: a unimodular form of signature (n,1) is reflective
     for n <= 19 [@Vin85, Introduction, no. 6], and
     O^+ = W_r rtimes Sym(P_r) with Sym(P_r) embedding in the automorphism group
     of the diagram, which is trivial for E_10 (@prop:reflective-semidirect(1)-(2)).
   - What is missing: that the reflection group W_r of E_10(2) is generated by
     the ten simple reflections, i.e. that every vector of square -2 in E_10 is
     a W(E_10)-translate of +- a simple root. That is the standard theory of the
     real roots of the E_10 root system; no copy of a source stating it, by
     numbered statement, is at hand in this environment, so it is recorded as an
     input rather than cited.
3. The theorem's statements about the isotropy: the image of Aut(S_b) in
   O(q_{T_Co}) is not computed (lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families(4)),
   and the theorem does not use it. Recorded because the issue's clause (c)
   would need it if the group on M were to be read through the isotropy.
