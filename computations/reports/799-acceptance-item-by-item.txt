THE PRODUCT ORDER, CHECKED PATH BY PATH

  def:transport-and-monodromy (local-systems-of-lattices-and-monodromy.md
  :18-32) puts on pi_1(B,s) the product in which gamma*delta traverses
  delta and then gamma, and prop:transport-homotopy-invariant(2) gives
  (delta followed by gamma)^* = gamma^* o delta^*. So the transport
  representation there is a homomorphism.
  not:paths-of-a-groupoid-quotient (moduli-construction.md :1225) writes
  the products of Pi_1(Y) in the order of traversal, and def:haefliger-path
  (:1242) and lem:haefliger-paths-exhaust-the-fundamental-groupoid(1)-(2)
  use that same convention. Hence on pi_1 of the stack
    T(omega eta) = T(eta) o T(omega),
  T is an anti-homomorphism, and rho(omega) := T(omega^{-1}) is the
  homomorphism; it has the same image.
  For lambda_r = [Pc_s][r][Pc_t]^{-1} in traversal order the two path
  segments have identity transport in the global frame of
  lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families(2), so T(lambda_r) = m(r) and
  rho(lambda_r) = m(r)^{-1} = m(r^{-1}). Because R_B is a groupoid,
  r -> r^{-1} permutes its points, so the subgroup generated by the m(r)
  does not depend on which of T and rho one reads. This also disposes of
  the residual choice "is T([r]) = m(r) or m(r)^{-1}", which depends only
  on which end of the constant path carries the identification.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE THEOREM, AND THE INDEX

  The theorem's statement now reads rho through rho(omega) = T(omega^{-1}),
  and it states the index asked for: rho(pi_1) = Stab_{O(M)}(K) cap O^+(M)
  and [Stab_{O(M)}(K) : rho(pi_1)] = 2. The upper bound is
  lem:transport-order...(3)-(5): every m(r) fixes K, lies in O^+(M) and
  restricts to O^+(K^{perp M}) = W(E_10) ([Vin72, Table 5] with
  K^{perp M} = E_10(2) of lem:coble-image-on-m-is-the-weyl-group(1));
  restriction is injective because K is primitive. The lower bound is the
  ten simple reflections, each an involution, so the inversion in the
  relabelling convention does not change them. The index 2 is the equality
  case of lem:coble-image-on-m-is-the-weyl-group's bound, the other coset
  represented by -id_{K^{perp M}} + id_K.

ORIGINAL STATEMENT, CLAUSE BY CLAUSE

1. Every family of classical Coble covers over a connected base has
   invariant-lattice monodromy contained in a stated group.
   VERDICT: MET, with the group W(E_10), which is infinite, not finite.
   thm:coble-monodromy-on-m-classical (:1373), using #804 and
   lem:cremona-second-plane-model-general (:908).

2. The universal family over the classical substack has monodromy
   exactly W(E_10) = O^+(K^{perp M}).
   VERDICT: MET modulo one imported input. thm:coble-monodromy-on-m-
   classical (:1373), read through lem:transport-order-on-the-classical-
   coble-stack (:1328), and stating the index [Stab_{O(M)}(K) :
   rho(pi_1)] = 2 as well. The upper bound uses input (I): the reflection
   group of E_10 = II_{1,9} is O^+(E_10), equivalently the ten simple
   reflections of lem:coble-image-on-m-is-the-weyl-group(1) generate it.
   Reflectivity of E_10 is [@Vin85, Introduction, no. 6] (unimodular forms
   are reflective for n <= 19) and the semidirect decomposition with
   trivial diagram symmetry is prop:reflective-semidirect(1)-(2); what no
   page here has is the identification of the reflection group with the
   group of the ten simple reflections. The theorem's statement now says
   "on the imported input (I) named in the proof".

3. "Every family of Coble covers" read for the widened functor of
   def:coble-moduli-functor, including non-plane-model members.
   VERDICT: NOT MET. The presentation and atlas are classical only;
   rmk:coble-class-smooth-antinbicanonical remains the scope boundary.

4. The invariant local system is isomorphic to the constant system M by
   an isometry respecting the eleven classes.
   VERDICT: MET on the labelled base B, NOT MET on the classical
   substack. lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families(2) is the labelled
   frame; (3)-(4) refute constancy on the stack.

5. The monodromy on M is trivial.
   VERDICT: REFUTED. Relabelling permutes e_1,...,e_10 and the product of
   two Bertini involutions has infinite order
   (lem:coble-automorphism-group-is-infinite(2)).

6. The monodromy on M is a stated finite group.
   VERDICT: REFUTED on the classical substack; the exact group is the
   infinite W(E_10). The widened stack is NOT MET, not asserted.

7. Every input is cited by a numbered statement that has been read.
   VERDICT: NOT MET, on one input, and MET otherwise. No printed page
   number is added anywhere; the sources cited by the new lemma are
   def:transport-and-monodromy, prop:transport-homotopy-invariant(2),
   not:paths-of-a-groupoid-quotient,
   lem:pi1-of-a-groupoid-quotient-is-generated-by-three-contributions,
   lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families(2)-(3),
   prop:spinor-plus-cone-components, prop:coble-invariant-lattice,
