WHAT THIS REVISION DID

1. Input (I) is closed by a numbered statement. The theorem rested on
   "O^+(E_10) = W(E_10), W(E_10) the group of the ten simple reflections", which
   the posted review at fa66cab619 named as the single remaining ground of its
   NOT MET. The source is Dolgachev, Reflection groups in algebraic geometry,
   Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 45 (2008) [@Dol07], read in the deposited copy
   tmp/sources/dolgachev-2008-reflection-groups-in-algebraic-geometry:
   - §5.4: "S_X(-1) = U ⊥ E_8 ≅ E_10", and, the fundamental polytope having no
     non-trivial symmetry, (5.12): O(S_X)^+ = Ref(S_X(-1)) ≅ W(2,3,7).
   - Example 4.3: E_{p,q,r} is the lattice generated by the fundamental root
     vectors of the Coxeter group W(p,q,r); its Gram matrix has 2 on the
     diagonal and 2I_n - G is the incidence matrix of the Coxeter graph
     T_{p,q,r}; and E_{p,q,r} is unimodular exactly for (p,q,r) = (2,3,5) and
     (2,3,7).
   - Example 2.7: W(p,q,r) is the Coxeter group on the graph T_{p,q,r} of
     Figure 7, which for (2,3,7) has p+q+r-2 = 10 nodes, i.e. it is the diagram
     of E_10 and its generators are the ten simple reflections.
   So O(E_10)^+ is generated by the ten simple reflections, that is by
   W(E_10) as the corpus uses the name (@lem:coble-image-on-m-is-the-weyl-
   group(1)-(2), @def:reflection-group). Sign and scaling: O(L) = O(L(-1)) and
   O(2L) = O(L) for an integral lattice, so the statement passes to the
   corpus's \latII_{1,9} and to K^{perp M} ≅ E_10(2). The theorem and its
   statement are now unconditional; the proof paragraph at :1402 carries the
   chain above.
   The earlier citation [@Vin72, Table 5] is not restored: that paper's forms
   are (1) -x_0^2 + x_1^2 + ... + x_n^2, n <= 17, and (3) -2x_0^2 + x_1^2 + ...
   + x_n^2, n <= 14, both odd, so its tables do not cover the even E_10. The
   corpus's other uses of it, at @prop:gamma-co-heegner-strict and on
   the-heegner-line-and-the-coble-arithmetic-group, concern the odd I_{1,8} and
   are correct.
