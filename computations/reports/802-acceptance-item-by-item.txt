2. "or refuted with a counterexample"
MET, by the unmarked assertion alone (moduli-construction.md:1165 and clause (3)
at :1175), through the invariant of
lem:automorphism-group-under-an-equivalence (moduli-construction.md:1144).

   The quotient side. For every subgroup G of Orth(T_Co), every object of the
   quotient stack [U/G] over a point has finite automorphism group
   (moduli-construction.md:1169, prop:no-identification-with-the-period-domain(1),
   lem:period-stabiliser-finite: "Let $T$ be a lattice of signature $(2,n)$ and
   $P\subset T\tensor\RR$ a positive definite $2$-plane (a point of $\bD(T)$).
   Then $\Stab_{\Orth(T)}(P)$ … is finite.").

   The Coble side, one object. The witness is an unnodal classical Coble surface
   S_0, the blow-up of PP^2 at the ten nodes of an irreducible sextic with no
   (-2)-curve, with its K3 double cover and covering involution; it is an object
   of M_Co over a point (def:coble-moduli-functor(1)--(2),
   lem:d2-unpolarised-family(1)). Its automorphism group is infinite
   (lem:coble-automorphism-group-is-infinite(3); [@CD12, Theorem 3.5], whose
   hypothesis is "Let X be an unnodal Coble surface"), and the automorphism group
   of the object surjects onto Aut(S_0)
   (rmk:d2-involution-moduli-of-ae22). Infinite against finite at that one object
   excludes an equivalence, for every subgroup G of Orth(T_Co)
   (moduli-construction.md:1175). The identification G = Otilde+(T_Co) inside the
   statement is not reached: there is no equivalence whose group could be that
   group.

   Finite isotropy elsewhere, which is not the witness. The Coble surfaces of
   types I and II with one boundary component have finite automorphism groups
   D_8 and S_4 ([@Kon21, Theorem 1.1, Table 2], at
   rmk:coble-automorphisms-infinite(2), moduli-construction.md:1107). They are
   objects of M_Co with finite isotropy, distinct from S_0; the refutation uses
   S_0 alone, and no statement is made that the isotropy of M_Co is infinite at
   every object.

   Not refuted by this branch. The first equivalence of #802, the phi-marked
   stack against D(T_Co) - Z. On a phi-marked cover of def:marked-coble-cover the
   covering involution is not an automorphism, since
   phi o iota^* o phi^{-1} = rho != id (def:marked-coble-cover(1)); that datum is
   left undecided (rmk:what-the-marked-isotropy-is(3),
   moduli-construction.md:1195), and the eleven-class-labelled statement at
   clause (2) (moduli-construction.md:1173) is about the labelled stack
   M^lab_Co and not a refutation of the phi-marked equivalence.
