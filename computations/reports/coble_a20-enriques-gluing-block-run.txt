=== (a) is the splitting A_{S_En} = A_{U(2)} + A_{E8(2)} invariant under O(q_{S_En})?
  orbits of O(q_{S_En}) on the 1024 classes: 3, sizes [1, 496, 527]   (14 s)
  orbits meeting the U(2) block: 3
    orbit of size   1: 1 of its classes inside the U(2) block
    orbit of size 496: 1 of its classes inside the U(2) block
    orbit of size 527: 2 of its classes inside the U(2) block
  the decomposition is O(q_{S_En})-invariant: False

  witness, in one orbit of size 496:
    x = [0] + [1]   (inside the U(2) block, q = 1)
    y = [2] + [4] + [7] + [9]   (outside the U(2) block, q = 1)
  so some isometry of q_{S_En} carries a class of the U(2) block out of it, and
  block-compatibility is not a property of the pair of forms.

=== (b) is the Enriques gluing gamma = A_{S_En}.isometry_to(A_{T_En}(-1)) block-compatible?
  q_{T_En}(-1) on the U(2) block: ['[0] in Fraction Field of Integer Ring / (2)Integer Ring', '[1] in Fraction Field of Integer Ring / (2)Integer Ring']
  built: OwnedTorsionFormIsometryMor_with_category_with_category.element_class
  isometry-to-the-twist check q_{T_En(-1)}(gamma z) = q_{S_En}(z) on all 259 classes of the
    two blocks: True   (q_{T_En(-1)} = -q_{T_En}, so this is q_{T_En}(gamma z) = -q_{S_En}(z))
  gamma is a typed isometry of torsion forms, hence bijective; the order of each image
  below is the check that it is injective where it matters.

  where the images land
    gamma(U(2) block)    order   4   inside the U(2) block: False   inside the E8(2) block: False
    gamma(E8(2) block)   order 256   inside the U(2) block: False   inside the E8(2) block: False
    gamma(U(2))  == A_{U(2)} of T_En : False
    gamma(E8(2)) == A_{E8(2)} of T_En: False
  block-compatible on this gamma: False

=== (c) a block-compatible anti-isometry exists, and it is not a computation
  A_{S_En} and A_{T_En} are both the orthogonal sum of the SAME two forms, A_{U(2)} and
  A_{E8(2)}: S_En = U(2) + E8(2) and T_En = U + U(2) + E8(2) with U unimodular, so the sum
  of the identity map of each summand is an isometry A_{S_En} -> A_{T_En} carrying the
  U(2) block onto the U(2) block (lem:coble-discriminant-blocks).  Nothing is computed
  for it; printed below are the two facts it rests on, which the run above establishes:
    the two blocks have orders 4 and 256 on each side, multiplying to 1024;
    q_{T_En(-1)} = q_{T_En} on the 259 classes of both blocks: True, so an isometry of the
      forms is an anti-isometry in the sense of prop:gamma-en-two-gluing, q_{T_En} o gamma
      = -q_{S_En} (every value of a 2-elementary form lies in 1/2 ZZ/ZZ, where q = -q).

=== what the three parts say together
  (a) The splitting of A_{S_En} is not invariant under O(q_{S_En}) (witness above), so a block-compatible
      anti-isometry and a non-block-compatible one both exist, and gamma(A_{U(2)}) is a
      property of the chosen anti-isometry: the pair of forms does not determine it.
  (b) The isometry the provider constructs is NOT block-compatible.  That is a fact about the
      construction it performs, not about the gluing of the marked embedding S_En in L_K3:
      nothing computed here relates that gluing to the constructed map, and by (a) nothing
      on the abstract forms could single out a block-compatible one anyway.
  (c) A block-compatible anti-isometry exists, so the hypothesis of
      prop:gamma-cong-eq-heegner(2) is satisfiable, and by (a) it is not automatic: it is a
      genuine choice among anti-isometries of one fixed pair of forms.  Which choice the
      gluing of the marked embedding makes is the labeled-embedding data of
      prob:coble-rigidity, and that is what item 1 of #663 leaves open.
