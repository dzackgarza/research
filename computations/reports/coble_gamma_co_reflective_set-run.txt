SUMMARY
  (1) T_Co is the even lattice <2> + U(2) + E8(-2), rank 11, det -2048, signature
      (2, 9), two-elementary with invariants (11, 11, 1), agreeing with the
      chapter table; its admissible reflective squares are {1, 2, 4} and the
      square -1 is excluded by parity, so its roots are the short roots v^2 = -2
      and the long roots v^2 = -4, whose divisibility is 2 here, as
      @def:2elementary-roots and lem:divisibilityAlwaysTwoTco say;
  (2) the (-2)-vectors of T_Co give reflections the owner builds, and both the
      spinor-norm and the discriminant computations that place them in Gamma_Co are
      hand proofs recorded in the writing, not owner decisions, because O^+ and the
      discriminant action are unavailable at this runtime (the refusals above and
      the crashes in coble_owner_gap-blockers-20261105.md);
  (3) the long root e' - f' has an integral reflection in O^+(T_Co) that moves the
      discriminant, so the reflective set of Gamma_Co is not the whole root system:
      it is the short roots together with the long roots that are discriminant-
      trivial, and among the short roots nothing more has to be decided;
  (4) T_Co is not hyperbolic, so the Vinberg-algorithm route the research report
      names (@prob:coxeter-completeness item 2, B32--B34) does not apply to T_Co
      itself: HyperbolicLattices refuses it by signature, and what remains is the
      reflective-lattice question in signature (2,9), for which the owner has no
      operation.  No generation statement is made or implied here.
