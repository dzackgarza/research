SUMMARY
  (1) T_Co is the even lattice <2> + U(2) + E8(2), rank 11, det -2048, signature
      (2, 9), two-elementary with invariants (11, 11, 1), agreeing with the
      chapter table; its admissible reflective squares are {1, 2, 4}, and the square
      -1 is excluded by parity, so the reflective squares are +-2 and -4, the short
      and long roots of @def:2elementary-roots with divisibility 2 here;
  (2) the (-2)-vectors of T_Co give reflections the owner builds, and the groups
      O^+(T_Co), the spinor norm and ker(rho_T_Co) are all built here as objects; but
      the two steps that place a named reflection in Gamma_Co are decisions, and
      neither is available: the spinor one stops the interpreter in GAP, and the
      discriminant one raises in the provider before GAP is reached (probe 3 of
      coble_owner_gap-blockers-20261105.md).  Both therefore remain proofs in the
      writing and are not claimed as owner decisions here;
  (3) the reflective set of Gamma_Co is exactly the short roots.  The positive-square
      reflections fail O^+ by the spinor norm alone, and no long root lies in
      ker(rho): for v^2 = -4 the reflection is s_v(x) = x + b_B(x,v) v in the
      coordinates of B, B is unimodular and v primitive so some x has b_B(x,v) = 1,
      and s_v(x) - x = v is not in 2B.  Nothing is left to decide vector by vector;
  (4) T_Co is not hyperbolic, so the Vinberg-algorithm route the research report
      names (@prob:coxeter-completeness item 2, B32--B34) does not apply to T_Co
      itself: HyperbolicLattices refuses it by signature, and the hyperbolic
      statement belongs to the cusp lattices.  What is open for T_Co is the index
      [Gamma_Co : W(T_Co)], not "generation by a reflective set plus finitely many
      isometries", which is vacuous because Gamma_Co is finitely generated.
