  moduli-construction.md:1024, prop:quotient-stack-of-periods.  M even of signature
  (1,t), t <= 19, primitively embedded in the K3 lattice L, N = M^perp.  Under
  (i) K_M a fine moduli space for marked M-polarized K3 surfaces [@Dol96, §3],
  (ii) the period map bijective on the ample locus [@Dol96, Cor. 3.2] and surjective
  with fibre W_pi(N) on the pseudo-ample one [@Dol96, Thm. 3.1], (iii)
  Gamma_M = O(N)* [@Dol96, Prop. 3.3], the ample moduli stack is
  [O(N)* \ D^o_M] and the pseudo-ample one [O(N)* \ D_M]; and if N contains an
  m-admissible isotropic vector with m <= 2 then O(N)* is transitive on the two
  components and the stack is [O(N)^{+,*} \ D^+_M] [@Dol96, Prop. 5.6].
  The proof supplies the step the source does not state: the markings of a fixed
  M-polarized K3 are a Gamma(M)-torsor, so the moduli stack is [K_M / Gamma(M)], and
  the period map identifies that with [O(N)* \ D_M].  The pseudo-ample fibre
  W_pi(N) lies in Gamma(M), so surjectivity in place of bijectivity does not change
  the quotient.

  Hypotheses checked for the Coble lattice, M = S_Co, N = T_Co
  (moduli-construction.md:1048-1052):
  - even, signature (1,10), t = 10 <= 19: @prop:coble-period-domain(1)--(2);
  - primitive embedding: explicit, @prop:coble-transcendental;
  - (i), (ii), (iii): [@Dol96, §3], [@Dol96, Cor. 3.2 with Thm. 3.1], [@Dol96,
    Prop. 3.3], the last unconditional;
  - 2-admissible vector: the summand U(2) of T_Co has basis e, f with e^2 = f^2 = 0
    and e.f = 2, so div(e) = div(f) = 2 and e is 2-admissible; [@Dol96, Prop. 5.6]
    applies with m = 2.
  Hence F_S = [Otilde+(T_Co) \ D(T_Co)^+], the presentation item 3 asks for.

  moduli-construction.md:1068, cor:monodromy-of-the-tautological-system, from
  lattices.md, lem:orbifold-monodromy-of-a-quotient: for U = D~/Gamma with the

  (g, gamma~) -> g, with image Gamma and kernel pi_1(D~), so

  F_S = [Otilde+(T_Co) \ D(T_Co)^+], the tautological system has monodromy
  Otilde+(T_Co) itself, and nontrivial stabilisers do not shrink the image: they make
  orbifold points.

ADDENDUM (same commit): the Deligne--Mumford question of item 1, resolved negatively

  While items 3 and 4 were being written, the question left open by the earlier
  round -- whether the stabilisers Aut(S_b) are finite -- was settled: they are not.
  moduli-construction.md, lem:coble-automorphism-group-is-infinite: for every
  irreducible ten-nodal sextic the 45 Bertini involutions act on
  Pic(S) = <H,E_1,...,E_10> by
    sigma_I^*(H)   = 17H - 6 sum_{i in I} E_i,
    sigma_I^*(E_i) = 6H - 3E_i - 2 sum_{j in I, j != i} E_j  (i in I),
    sigma_I^*(E_a) = E_a,  sigma_I^*(E_b) = E_b,
  each an isometry and an involution; and for I = {1..8}, J = {1,2,3,4,5,6,9,10}
  the product has characteristic polynomial (t-1)^7 (t+1)^2 (t^2-14t+1), with
  roots 7 +- 4 sqrt 3, so it has infinite order in GL_11(Z).
  Record: computations/reports/coble_797-bertini-infinite-order.py with
  -run.txt.  The eigenspace input is [@CD12, Thm. 3.5], and its consequence on H
  agrees with the value proved independently in prop:coble-plane-model-comparison(2)
  from [@Pie26, Prop. 59].

  thm:coble-period-map-of-the-stack (moduli-construction.md:1233) states and proves
  what prop:torelli-for-the-coble-lattice had only cited for coarse spaces:
  1. the period map of the stack of labelled Coble covers is defined (H^{2,0} lies in
     T_Co by prop:coble-transcendental);
  2. it is injective on labelled objects, with a unique isomorphism: the marking makes
     phi = id_L a Hodge isometry, which preserves the polarization and its ampleness,
     so the strong Torelli theorem [@BHPV04, Ch. VIII, Thm. 11.1] in the form used at
     lem:chamber-from-marked-period gives the isomorphism, which commutes with the
     involutions because an involution is determined by its action on H^2;
  3. local Torelli: the differential is Dol96 section 3's displayed bijection d_1
     restricted to the iota-invariant parts, 9 -> 9 (lem:coble-deformations(1));
  4. descent: Gamma(M) acts, its image is O(T_Co)^* = Otilde(T_Co) [@Dol96, Prop. 3.3],
     and the induced map of quotient stacks is fully faithful with image the ample
     locus.
  So the statement DK13 leaves unnumbered for the Coble lattice is now proved here,
  and item 5's remaining gap is closed; item 5 is MET (route (b) proved, route (a)
  recorded as having no object).

ITEM 6, THE MAPS (this commit)
  prop:coble-enriques-maps-and-the-vhs (moduli-construction.md:1260) records what the
  two maps of item 2 do to the local system, with the volume's own statements:
  1. Coble -> Enriques: T_Co = delta^perp in T_En, so the Coble period domain is a
     closed submanifold of the Enriques one, and on the quotients the map is finite and
     birational onto the (-2)-Heegner divisor, exhibiting F_Co as its normalization
     (@thm:normalization) -- never an open immersion.
  2. The meridian of that divisor is s_delta (@lem:d2-vanishing-reflections(3)) and acts
     trivially on delta^perp = eta(T_Co), so restricting the Enriques system to the
     divisor gives fibre T_Co and monodromy the image of Stab_{Gamma_En}(delta) =
     R_{Gamma_En} = Otilde^+(T_Co) (@prop:gamma-co-images(1)): an upper bound for
     Gamma_Co, not Gamma_Co.
  3. Coble -> K3: (S, X -> S, iota) |-> X is a quotient by iota, fully faithful on the
     groupoids (@lem:d0-coble-stack-in-involution-stack), and it does not move the local
     system: the Coble system is the iota-anti-invariant summand T_Co of the K3 system
     (@lem:d2-local-system(3)), so a sub-local-system, not a pullback or pushforward.
