research#797, the comparison of Bbar with M_Co -- state of the two witnesses

Witness 1 (SOUND).  The Bertini involution of any eight of the ten nodes lifts to a
biregular automorphism sigma of S = Bl_10 P^2 (the lifting is [@Pie26, Prop. 59],
after Coble 1939), and sigma*(E_i) = -2K_Y - E_i for i <= 8 with K_Y = K_S - E_9 -
E_10.  From H = (sum_{i<=10} E_i - K_S)/3 one gets sigma*(H) = 17H - 6(E_1+...+E_8),
of square 1 and with H.sigma*(H) = 17, so H is not preserved and sigma is induced by
no projectivity.  Hence Aut(S) is strictly larger than the projectivity stabiliser of
Z at every irreducible ten-nodal sextic, and Bbar -> M_Co is not full.

Witness 2 (REFUTED).  The claim that A = 2H - E_1 - E_2 - E_3 is the line class of a
second plane model is false.  The ten classes H-E_1-E_2, H-E_1-E_3, H-E_2-E_3,
E_4, ..., E_10 are pairwise disjoint of square -1 and each is orthogonal to A, and
A^2 = 1, A.D = 6, so A meets the three conditions that do not involve the
exceptional classes.  But a plane model requires in addition
  -K_S = 3A - sum_k G_k,
and the computation (computations/reports/coble_797-plane-models.py, with its run
record) gives 3A - sum_k G_k = 3H - E_1 - E_2 - E_3 + E_4 + ... + E_10, which is not
-K_S = 3H - sum_{i<=10} E_i.  The contraction of the ten curves is therefore not the
plane P^2 that A is pulled back from, and no second ten-nodal sextic is produced by
this route.

Consequence.  Non-injectivity of Bbar -> M_Co on isomorphism classes is NOT
established; it is withdrawn.  What stands is: the map is essentially surjective (the
nodes determine the sextic in a fixed plane, by the Bezout count of
lem:d2-unpolarised-family(1), which closes rmk:d2-what-is-open(2)), and it is not
full (Witness 1).  The relation to write is that M_Co is the quotient of Bbar by the
plane-model equivalence generated by the automorphisms of S that are not induced by
projectivities, and the question whether two distinct plane models can present the
same S without such an automorphism is open after this refutation.
