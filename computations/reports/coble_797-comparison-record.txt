research#797, the comparison of Bbar with M_Co -- the two witnesses

CORRECTION.  An earlier version of this file, and commit b7b2dd760f, reported that
the second plane model failed: the script had the sign of E_4, ..., E_10 wrong
(a coefficient -1 where the divisor E_i has coefficient +1), which turned
3A - sum_k G_k into a class with +1 in those seven slots and made it differ from
-K_S.  With the correct convention the identity holds, m1's witness stands, and the
claim of non-injectivity is restored.  The corrected computation is
computations/reports/coble_797-plane-models.py with its run record.

Witness 1 (not full).  The Bertini involution of any eight of the ten nodes lifts to
a biregular automorphism sigma of S = Bl_10 P^2 (the lifting is [@Pie26, Prop. 59],
after Coble 1939), and sigma*(E_i) = -2K_Y - E_i for i <= 8 with K_Y = K_S - E_9 -
E_10.  From H = (sum_{i<=10} E_i - K_S)/3 one gets sigma*(H) = 17H - 6(E_1+...+E_8),
of square 1 and with H.sigma*(H) = 17, so H is not preserved and sigma is induced by
no projectivity.  Hence Aut(S) strictly contains the projectivity stabiliser of Z at
every irreducible ten-nodal sextic.

Witness 2 (not injective on isomorphism classes).  The quadratic Cremona involution
centred at p_1, p_2, p_3 replaces the plane model H by
  A = 2H - E_1 - E_2 - E_3,
whose exceptional curves are H-E_1-E_2, H-E_1-E_3, H-E_2-E_3, E_4, ..., E_10.  The
computation verifies: the ten are pairwise disjoint of square -1; A^2 = 1;
A.G_k = 0 for every k; A.D = 6 for D = -2K_S; G_k.D = 2 for every k; and
3A - sum_k G_k = -K_S.  So the contraction of the ten curves is a second plane P^2
with line class A, in which D is a sextic meeting each exceptional curve in 2; ten
double points exhaust its arithmetic genus 10, so the image Z' is an irreducible
ten-nodal sextic.  The same S, hence the same (S, X, iota), is presented by Z and by
Z', and A != H.

Consequence.  Bbar -> M_Co is essentially surjective (the nodes determine the sextic
in a fixed plane, by the Bezout count of lem:d2-unpolarised-family(1), which closes
rmk:d2-what-is-open(2)), is not full (Witness 1) and is not injective on isomorphism
classes (Witness 2).  So M_Co is the quotient of Bbar by the plane-model equivalence,
the equivalence on plane presentations generated by the Cremona transformations that
carry one ten-nodal plane model of S to another.
