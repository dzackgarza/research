## K4: the **unmarked** statement is refuted by one witness; the $\varphi$-marked statement is not decided here

Commit `ec8e318ebd19d6cfd4d8fceb98cfa29e12d63694`. So the first acceptance clause ("proved on a page of `writing/` and read MET by one Kimi review") is **NOT MET**, and the second ("refuted with a counterexample") is **MET by the unmarked assertion alone**; the item-by-item record is `computations/reports/802-acceptance-item-by-item.txt`.

The invariant is the automorphism group of an object, which an equivalence of stacks preserves: `lem:automorphism-group-under-an-equivalence` (`writing/coble/lattices-and-moduli/moduli-construction.md:1144`), for an equivalence of categories $F\colon\mca\to\mcb$ with quasi-inverse $G$ in the sense of the Stacks Project's **Definition 4.2.17**, tag 001J: "An *equivalence of categories* $F : \mathcal{A} \to \mathcal{B}$ is a functor such that there exists a functor $G : \mathcal{B} \to \mathcal{A}$ such that the compositions $F \circ G$ and $G \circ F$ are isomorphic to the identity functors". The proof uses only the unit and the counit.

### The unmarked statement, refuted

`prop:no-identification-with-the-period-domain` (`moduli-construction.md:1165`), clause (3) at `:1175`, gives one object. On the quotient side, for every subgroup $G\subseteq\Orth(T_\Co)$ and every point of $U=\bD(T_\Co)^+-Z$, the automorphism group of the object of $[U/G]$ over a point is the stabiliser $G_{\tilde x}$, and `lem:period-stabiliser-finite` — "Let $T$ be a lattice of signature $(2,n)$ and $P\subset T\tensor\RR$ a positive definite $2$-plane (a point of $\bD(T)$). Then $\Stab_{\Orth(T)}(P)=\theset{g\in\Orth(T)\mid g(P)=P}$ is finite." — makes it finite, $T_\Co$ being of signature $(2,9)$ (`prop:coble-period-domain(2)`).

On the Coble side the witness is an unnodal classical Coble surface $S_0$, the blow-up of $\PP^2$ at the ten nodes of an irreducible sextic with no $(-2)$-curve, with its $\mathrm{K3}$ double cover and covering involution: an object of $\mcM_\Co$ over a point (`def:coble-moduli-functor(1)--(2)`, `lem:d2-unpolarised-family(1)`). Its automorphism group is infinite (`lem:coble-automorphism-group-is-infinite(3)`; Cantat–Dolgachev, *Rational surfaces with a large group of automorphisms*, **Theorem 3.5**: "Let X be an unnodal Coble surface. Then Aut(X)∗ contains the subgroup of WX which is isomorphic to W10(2) := {w ∈ W10 : w(v) − v ∈ 2E10 for all v ∈ Pic(X)} under the natural identification of WX with W10"), and that theorem is quoted here with the hypothesis the page uses it under. The automorphism group of the object surjects onto $\Aut(S_0)$ (`rmk:d2-involution-moduli-of-ae22`). Infinite against finite at one object excludes an equivalence for every subgroup $G$ of $\Orth(T_\Co)$, and $\widetilde\Orth^+(T_\Co)$ is one — so no equivalence exists at which the clause $G=\widetilde\Orth^+(T_\Co)$ of the statement could be read off. **No universal claim about isotropy is used or made:** other objects of $\mcM_\Co$ have finite automorphism groups, the Coble surfaces of types I and II with one boundary component, with $\mathrm{D}_8$ and $S_4$ ([@Kon21, Theorem 1.1, Table 2], recorded at `rmk:coble-automorphisms-infinite(2)`, `moduli-construction.md:1107`); they are distinct from the one witness and do not weaken it.

### The $\varphi$-marked statement, not decided

The first equivalence of #802, the marked stack against $D(T_\Co)-Z$, is **not** refuted by anything on the page, and the earlier form of this comment was wrong to say it was. The argument there used the covering involution of a labelled object, where the marking is the eleven classes of `rmk:coble-labelled-versus-unlabelled(1)`: "The covering involution $\iota$ is a nontrivial automorphism of every labelled object — it preserves each $e_i$, preserves the period, and acts as $-\id$ on $T_\Co$ (@prop:coble-transcendental) — so the isotropy of $\mcM^{\mathrm{lab}}_\Co$ contains $\theset{\id,\iota}\cong\ZZ/2$ at every object." On the marking of `def:marked-coble-cover`, the isometry $\varphi\colon H^2(X,\bZ)\isoto\lkt$, that fails at its first clause: "$\varphi\circ\iota^*\circ\varphi^{-1} = \rho$" (`def:marked-coble-cover(1)`), and $\rho\neq\id$, so $\iota$ is not an automorphism of a $\varphi$-marked cover and no counterexample to that equivalence is supplied by it. `rmk:what-the-marked-isotropy-is(3)` (`moduli-construction.md:1195`) records exactly this and leaves the datum undecided. The eleven-class-labelled result survives as a statement about that object: `prop:no-identification-with-the-period-domain(2)` (`:1173`), "the eleven-class-labelled stack is not $U$", with the sentence that it is not a statement about the $\varphi$-marked covers of `def:marked-coble-cover`. The eleven-class labelling is not put in place of the $\varphi$-marking anywhere.

### What the page proves about the isotropy of the eleven-class-labelled stack

That it is finite, nontrivial and contains $\theset{\id,\iota}\cong\ZZ/2$ at every object, and that on the generic substack, under hypothesis (v), it is exactly $\theset{\id,\iota}=\Stab_{\widetilde\Orth^+(T_\Co)}(\pi)$, which makes the period map an equivalence onto $\qty[\widetilde\Orth^+(T_\Co)\backslash U^{\mathrm{gen}}]$ "with the isotropy $\theset{\id,\iota}$ on both sides" (`thm:gamma-co-labelled-equality(1)`, `(v)`). That is a statement about that stack; the isotropy of a stack built on the $\varphi$-marking is not computed.
