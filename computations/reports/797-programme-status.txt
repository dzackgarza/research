research#797 -- the six items of the programme, item by item, at this commit

Paths are relative to the repository root; line numbers are those of this commit.
Every claim below is either a label on the page or an explicit statement that the
item is open.  Nothing here is a new answer.

ITEM 1. The Coble moduli functor over Spec Z[1/N], its descent, stack, Deligne-Mumford
property and universal family.
  MET: N = 2 and the functor on Z[1/2]-schemes (def:coble-moduli-functor-z); fppf and
  etale descent (lem:coble-moduli-functor-fppf); the analytic version
  (def:coble-moduli-functor, lem:coble-moduli-functor-stack).
  SETTLED NEGATIVELY: the Deligne-Mumford property.  Aut(S) = W_10(2) is infinite and
  discrete (CD12 Section 5, the theorem labelled autocoble and the closing remark of
  the subsection), so the stack is not locally of finite type, is not Deligne-Mumford
  in the standard sense, and is not separated; its inertia is discrete
  (rmk:coble-automorphisms-infinite(1)-(3)).
  NOT MET: algebraicity, and the universal family as a family over a scheme.  The
  tautological universal object exists and the family over Bbar presents it without
  representing it (rmk:universal-object-of-the-coble-stack(1)-(2)).

ITEM 2. The Enriques and lattice-polarized K3 functors and the maps between them.
  MET: the partial compactification over Z[1/2] (def:enriques-partial-compactification,
  lem:enriques-partial-compactification-stack); the Coble stack as its boundary divisor
  over delta-perp, with the fixed-curve normal data (lem:coble-is-the-boundary-divisor);
  the refutation of a morphism from the Coble stack to the smooth Enriques stack, with
  the boundary relation of DK13 Section 1 (rmk:coble-enriques-not-a-map).
  PARTIAL: the map to the K3-with-involution moduli is stated for the Coble component
  (lem:d0-coble-stack-in-involution-stack) and not for the Enriques one.
  NOT MET: a functor of Enriques surfaces with its stack property and universal
  family; the two-lattice structure of the partial compactification is not yet on the
  page.

ITEM 3. Quotient-stack presentations of the unpolarized and S-polarized K3 stacks.
  NOT ON THIS TREE.  The labelled period-map theorem and the marking-group proposition
  are proved on the item-3/4 branch and are absent from this tree; the unpolarized case
  is expressly left unproved on the page.

ITEM 4. Monodromy from a local system on a stack.
  The general exact-sequence statement is on the page; the identification of the
  tautological system with the universal family's VHS is not.

ITEM 5. Torelli.
  NOT MET.  The coarse statement is DK13 Proposition 3.2; the stack-level statement is
  defective as reviewed (object injectivity and a differential do not identify inertia;
  no ample class is constructed where BHPV04 Chapter VIII Theorem 11.1 needs one; the
  step from a constructible image to a proper closed analytic complement is not proved).

ITEM 6. Pushing, pulling and restricting the VHS; Gamma_Co and Gamma_{Co,2}.
  PROVED: the local system on the labelled stack is the pullback of the one on the
  stack of Coble surfaces, so rho^lab = rho_Co o p_* with p_* onto, and
  Gamma_Co = rho_Co(pi_1(M_Co)) = rho^lab(pi_1(M^lab,S_10)) -- an IMAGE, with
  rho^lab(ker p_*) = 1 automatically (prop:pi1-of-the-plane-model-quotient(1)-(4));
  the isotropy and plane-model contributions are carried by loops of the labelled
  stack and are neither added nor divided out (rmk:correction-monodromy-is-an-image).
  CONDITIONAL: Gamma_Co = Otilde^+(T_Co) given (L) the labelled equality, with no
  further input (prop:gamma-co-on-the-coble-stack).
  OPEN: (L) itself -- the labelled monodromy is all of Otilde^+(T_Co), equivalently
  the connectedness of the period image on the labelled stack, which is
  thm:gamma-co-is-stable(2) and items 3-4's open step.  This is now the ONLY open
  input for Gamma_Co.
  OPEN: everything about Gamma_{Co,2}; no statement identifies it.
  WITHDRAWN: the quotient formula Gamma_Co = Otilde^+/rho(N), the isotropy subgroup
  F_b as a separate input, the input rho(N) = 1, and the alternative
  Gamma_Co = Otilde^+/ {+-1} (bc991006fc).
