cor:lifting-unimodular: bar-phi o gamma = gamma o bar-psi     True
lem:coble-discriminant-actions-conjugate: bar-phi = gamma bar-psi gamma^{-1}   True

substituting bar-psi = 1 (S_Co fixed pointwise): bar-phi = gamma o gamma^{-1} = 1.

verdict: the lemma holds.  An isometry fixing S_Co pointwise acts trivially on A_{T_Co}, so the
  monodromy of a family whose local system fixes S_Co pointwise lies in ker(rho_{T_Co}) -- item 1e
  for a MARKED family.  It does not cover the unmarked family, where only H and alpha are fixed:
  the swap of e' and f' fixes h and has rho != 1, so fixing two classes is not enough.
