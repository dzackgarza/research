Boundary lattice at a 0-cusp of F_{2d} with p_*(delta) > 1: d = p^2.
Sources: AE23 Section 10C, printed p. 38; AET23 Def. 4.1 and Fig. 1, printed pp. 21-22.

d = 4, p = 2
  delta = g + 2 e1 + 2 f1, delta^2 = 0, primitive = True
  delta/p in L*: pairings with (g, e1, f1) = (-4, 1, 1), all integral
  delta/p^2 in L*: pairing with e1 = 1/2, not integral -> p_*(delta) = 2
  u = e1 - f1: u.delta = 0, u^2 = -2
  v = g + 4 f1: v.delta = 0, v^2 = -8, delta = v + 2 u
  every (a,b,c) in K with b + c = 4 a equals a v + b u
  delta^perp/delta: rank 19 = 1 + 2 + 16, first summand -2, discriminant 2
  printed first summand -2d/p_* = -4, discriminant 4
  -> the printed lattice has discriminant 4; the cusp has 2
