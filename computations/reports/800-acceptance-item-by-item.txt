THE STATEMENT OF THE ISSUE
"Two marked Coble covers (X, iota, phi) and (X', iota', phi') with the same
period in D(T_Co) are isomorphic by an isomorphism compatible with iota and the
markings. The chamber of positive roots, not an ample class, is what strong
Torelli (BHPV VIII Thm 11.1) needs here."

THE NOTION OF MARKED COBLE COVER USED
The volume's own: @rmk:marked-coble-covers-after-huybrechts
(writing/coble/lattices-and-moduli/moduli-construction.md:565-582), restated
as @def:marked-coble-cover, page :6-22. A triple (X, iota, phi) with phi:
H^2(X,Z) -> Lambda an isometry with phi iota^* phi^{-1} = rho, rho the
involution with (+1)-part M = <2> + <-2>^10 and (-1)-part T_Co; the period is
the value of the period map P_Co into D(T_Co); an isomorphism is an f with
f iota f^{-1} = iota' and phi f^* = phi'.

VERDICT: the statement is FALSE.

@prop:marked-coble-period-not-injective, page :24-31, proof :34-59.
On one classical Coble cover X of [@DK13, §3], with c := phi([E_0]) of square 2,
the reflection s_c acts trivially on A_{S_Co} (:38-40), so s_c + id_{T_Co}
extends to an isometry g of L_K3 fixing T_Co pointwise (:41,
@thm:automorphism-lifting-criterion); phi' := g o phi is a second marking with
the same period (:43-48) that sends [E_0] to its negative and fixes
[E_1],...,[E_10]; and no isomorphism carries phi to phi' (:50-58), because such
an f would have f^* = the reflection in the effective class [E_0], which sends
that class to its negative, against f^*(A).[E_0] > 0 for A ample.

WHAT THE COUNTEREXAMPLE DOES NOT DECIDE
The pair differs on the class of E_0, the only one of the eleven of positive
square, and the reflection reverses the sign of the pairing with that class, so
the two marked covers do not carry the same chamber of positive roots
(@rmk:marked-coble-period-scope, page :61-68). A statement whose datum includes
the chamber, or whose markings fix the eleven classes, is a different statement
and is not decided on this page.
