4. what this does not do
   4.1 the labels.  A21 compares tuples (L_K3, I_Co, I_dP, H, alpha, C): commuting
       nonsymplectic involutions, H^2 = 4 fixed by both, alpha^2 = -2 with H.alpha = 0 fixed
       by I_Co, and a chamber with alpha effective and H nef and big (heegner report,
       sec-labeled-lattice-data).  None is built here, so this is an unlabelled comparison of
       polarizations, necessary for the labeled one and not a substitute for it.
   4.2 the marked pair.  Matching complements is necessary, not sufficient, for an isometry
       carrying h~_Co to h.  The criterion of thm:coble-heegner-line-unique needs an indefinite
       complement and a positive vector in signature (1,10) has a negative definite one, so the
       vector-level question has to be decided directly.
   4.3 the ambient.  L_K3 and the two primitive embeddings are not built, so no isometry is
       extended over it with isometry_extends and no chamber is transported.
