5. each of the ten points is an ordinary node, decided point by point
   At a singular point of f the lowest term has degree at least 2; the point is an
   ordinary node exactly when that lowest term is a nondegenerate quadratic form, whose
   two factors are then distinct linear forms and give the two smooth branches.  For a
   singular point of a plane curve the quadratic part is nondegenerate exactly when the
   Hessian determinant f_xx f_yy - f_xy^2 is nonzero there, so the check is that
   determinant, evaluated at each point in the number field Q[y]/(its factor).
   f_xx f_yy - f_xy^2 = 16200*x**6*y + 162000*x**5 - 1125*x**4*y**4 + 255600*x**3*y**3 - 259200*x**2*y**2 + 16200*x*y**6 + 144000*x*y + 162000*y**5 - 14400
   factor of degree 2: 2 points; the Hessian determinant there is nonzero in Q[y]/(g),
     so every point over this factor has a nondegenerate quadratic part
   factor of degree 4: 4 points; the Hessian determinant there is nonzero in Q[y]/(g),
     so every point over this factor has a nondegenerate quadratic part
   factor of degree 4: 4 points; the Hessian determinant there is nonzero in Q[y]/(g),
     so every point over this factor has a nondegenerate quadratic part
   the ten points are accounted for: 10
   hence each of the ten singular points is an ordinary node, and with the ten distinct
   points and total delta 10 of section 3 no singular point is left over

7. the double plane w^2 = F
   at an ordinary node a linear change makes F = x*y + (terms of degree >= 3), and then
   w^2 - F = w^2 - x*y + (higher) has quadratic part w^2 - x*y, whose matrix
     [[1, 0, 0], [0, 0, -1/2], [0, -1/2, 0]], determinant -1/4
   is nondegenerate, so the surface has an ordinary double point (A_1) over the node

8. the marking
   the minimal resolution of the double plane is a K3 (the branch curve is a sextic),
   and the ten nodes give ten disjoint (-2)-curves E_1..E_10 over them; with e_0 = f^*H
   of square 2 and H.E_i = 0, the classes e_0, e_1..e_10 span <2> + <-2>^10 = S_Co,
   which is the marking of @prop:coble-cover-marking and of @thm:theta-co-coordinates.
