5. why each of the ten points is an ordinary node
   Write m_p for the multiplicity of the point p and mu_p for the length of the local
   algebra C{x,y}/(f, f_x, f_y) at p, which is the Milnor number.  Two exact facts:
     (a) the degree of the singular scheme is the sum of the mu_p, and here it is 10;
     (b) rationality gives sum of delta_p = 10, and delta_p <= m_p(m_p-1)/2 for each p,
         so sum m_p(m_p-1)/2 >= 10.
   Also mu_p >= (m_p-1)^2.  If some m_p were at least 3, that point alone would give
   mu_p >= 4 and m_p(m_p-1)/2 >= 3, so (a) would leave at most 6 further points while
   (b) would need at least 7 of them; hence every m_p = 2.  Then (a) gives at most ten
   points and (b) at least ten, so there are exactly ten, and 10 = sum of mu_p >= 10
   with mu_p >= 1 forces mu_p = 1 and delta_p = 1 at each: every one is an ordinary node.
   (Cross-check with Milnor's formula mu_p = 2 delta_p - r_p + 1, r_p the number of
   branches: ten nodes give r_p = 2 and mu_p = 1, summing to 10, as computed.)

6. the two points at infinity
   F(x,y,0) = 5*x**3*y**3, so z = 0 meets the curve at (1:0:0) and (0:1:0) only
   chart x = 1: F(1,y,z) = 27*y**5*z + 5*y**3 + 150*y**2*z**2 - 120*y*z**4 + 32*z**6 + 27*z
     its linear term at (y,z) = (0,0) is 27*z, nonzero, so (1:0:0) is smooth
   chart y = 1: F(x,1,z) = 27*x**5*z + 5*x**3 + 150*x**2*z**2 - 120*x*z**4 + 32*z**6 + 27*z
     likewise smooth at (0:1:0), so all ten singularities lie in the chart z = 1

7. the double plane w^2 = F
   at an ordinary node a linear change makes F = x*y + (terms of degree >= 3), and then
   w^2 - F = w^2 - x*y + (higher) has quadratic part w^2 - x*y, whose matrix
     [[1, 0, 0], [0, 0, -1/2], [0, -1/2, 0]], determinant -1/4
   is nondegenerate, so the surface has an ordinary double point (A_1) over the node

8. the marking
   the minimal resolution of the double plane is a K3 (the branch curve is a sextic),
   and the ten nodes give ten disjoint (-2)-curves E_1..E_10 over them; with e_0 = f^*H
   of square 2 and H.E_i = 0, the classes e_0, e_1..e_10 span <2> + <-2>^10 = S_Co,
   which is the marking of @prop:coble-cover-marking and of @thm:theta-co-coordinates.
