ACCEPTANCE, item by item

ITEM 1. A page states the functor and its morphisms.
VERDICT: MET.  moduli-construction.md:523, def:coble-moduli-functor: over a complex
space Y the objects are (S -> Y, X -> S, iota) with S -> Y flat, proper and of finite
presentation with the fibres of lem:d2-unpolarised-family(1), X -> S the relative
double cover branched along -2K_{S/Y} (which exists by the 2-divisibility of
lem:d2-unpolarised-family(2)), and iota the covering involution; morphisms are
Cartesian squares over Y commuting with pi and iota; pullback is the fibre product.

ITEM 2. ... and proves that it is a stack.
VERDICT: MET.  :548, lem:coble-moduli-functor-stack, for the etale topology hence
also the analytic one: isomorphisms are a sheaf (closed conditions on maps of
spaces); objects descend by faithfully flat descent (SGA 1, Expose VIII); the fibre
condition is checked fibre-wise; the branch divisor and the cyclic double cover
Spec(O + O(-D)) are determined by the descended family, so pi and iota descend.

ITEM 3. ... and states its algebraicity, Deligne-Mumford property and coarse space,
each proved or cited by numbered statement.
VERDICT: NOT MET.
  Algebraicity: not stated.  The route is available and cheap but is not written: the
  family of lem:d2-unpolarised-family(2) over V gives V -> M_Co, and if that map is
  smooth and surjective then M_Co is algebraic (a stack with a smooth atlas); what is
  missing is the versality of that family, i.e. the surjectivity of the differential
  onto the deformation space of the surface.
  Deligne-Mumford: NOT MET, and the obstruction is named on the page
  (:495, rmk:d2-involution-moduli-of-ae22): the stabiliser of a geometric point is
  Aut(S_b), which is never trivial (the Bertini involutions, :511); the standard
  ampleness argument for finiteness is unavailable because |H| is nef and not ample
  (:513); and what the DM property needs is the finiteness of the subgroup of
  Orth(Pic(S_b)) generated by the 45 Bertini involutions, which nothing read decides.
  So the DM property is open, with one explicit missing input.
  Coarse space: partial only.  The AE22 route is recorded (:495): for S = M of type
  (11,11,1) the coarse space of the K3-with-rho-marking problem is
  F_S = (D_S - Delta)/Gamma_rho with Gamma_rho = Orth(T_Co), the full isometry group.
  Its identification with the classical M_Co is [@DK13, Prop. 3.2], an open subset of
  D(N)/Orth(N) with N = T_Co.  What is NOT stated is the coarse space of the Coble
  stack itself, i.e. that it is that image.

ITEM 4. It proves that the D2a family over B (or [B/S10]) is, or is not, the
universal family over an open substack.
VERDICT: MET.  :581, prop:coble-plane-model-comparison: the map phi from Bbar to
M_Co is (1) essentially surjective; (2) not full, because the Bertini involution of
eight of the ten nodes is an automorphism of S induced by no projectivity, its action
sending H to 17H - 6 sum_{i<=8} E_i, of square 1 while H.(that class) = 17
[@Pie26, Prop. 59]; and (3) not injective on isomorphism classes, because the
quadratic Cremona involution at three nodes presents S again with line class
A = 2H - E1 - E2 - E3, where 3A - sum_k G_k = -K_S and A.(-2K_S) = 6, so the plane
branch curve is again an irreducible ten-nodal sextic, different from Z because
A != H.  Hence the answer is NOT: Bbar is not an open substack of M_Co and the family
over B is not universal there.  The computation is
computations/reports/coble_797-plane-models.py with its run record.

ITEM 5. def:gamma-co-groups is restated from the stack.
VERDICT: NOT MET.  lattices.md:116, def:gamma-co-groups, still defines Gamma_Co as
the monodromy group over the labelled base B.  By ITEM 4 that is the monodromy of a
plane presentation, not of the stack; and the stack's monodromy differs from it by the
plane-model equivalence (ITEM 4(4)).

RELATED, NOT ACCEPTANCE: the comparison with the K3-with-involution moduli is stated
