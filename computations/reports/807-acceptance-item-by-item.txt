WHAT IS PROVED, IN THE ISSUE'S TERMS

  The issue's disjunction was: every Coble surface with | -2K | a single smooth
  curve is the blow-up of the ten nodes of an irreducible plane sextic, so the
  classical substack is all of M_Co; or a non-classical one exists, and M_Co has
  a further component.

  Proved (thm:coble-smooth-antibicanonical-is-a-plane-blowup):
  (1) such an S has C rational with C^2 = -4 and K_S^2 = -1, and is minimal as a
      Coble surface;
  (2) S is basic: it admits a birational morphism onto P^2, is a blow-up
      Bl_{p_1,...,p_10} P^2, and -K_S = 3H - sum E_i, C = 6H - 2 sum E_i;
  (3) the image Z = pi(C) is an irreducible plane sextic, C -> Z is its
      normalisation, Z has multiplicity 2 at the image of each centre and no
      other singularity, and delta(Z) = 10 = p_a(Z);
  (4) when the ten centres are distinct, Z has exactly ten double points, each
      of delta = 1, each an ordinary node or an ordinary cusp.

  Hence NO object of the functor lies outside the plane-model family, and the
  second alternative of the issue is refuted: the widening of the functor does
  not produce a further component of M_Co.  The strict form of the first
  alternative, "ten NODES", is NOT proved in general; see the two cases below.

THE TWO CASES THE PROOF LEAVES (named, not assumed either way)

  (a) A double point of Z that is an ordinary cusp.  By
      lem:coble-double-point-of-the-sextic a double point is a cusp exactly
      when C meets the corresponding (-1)-curve in one point of multiplicity
      two, equivalently when the preimage of that curve in the K3 double cover
      is a pair of (-2)-curves meeting once rather than one smooth (-2)-curve.
      In the plane model of [DZ99, Theorem 4.3] this is [DZ99, Theorem 2.5]
      with a non-multiple fibre of Kodaira type II; the other types that
      [DZ99, Theorem 6.5] and thm:dz-k3-type-criterion allow -- I_n with
      n >= 2, III, IV -- leave C reducible and so are not objects of the
      functor, and type I_1 is the classical ten-nodal sextic.
  (b) Two of the ten centres infinitely near.  Then the plane sextic has a
      double point with an infinitely near double point over it, of delta = 2,
      and Z has nine double points rather than ten.

  The single lemma that closes the issue: the surfaces of case (a) and of case
  (b) are (or are not) again the blow-up of the ten nodes of some irreducible
  plane sextic, i.e. they admit a second plane model whose ten centres are
  distinct nodes.  By lem:coble-double-point-of-the-sextic(3) that is the
  question whether such an S carries ten pairwise disjoint (-1)-curves meeting
  C twice each whose preimages in the K3 cover are smooth; the model supplies
  one whose preimage is a nodal pair in case (a) and two that meet each other
  in case (b).

THE ARGUMENT, IN SEVEN LINES (for the reviewer)

  1. C = -2K_S, so p_a(C) = h^0(-K_S) = 0 by [DZ99, Lemma 1.2(1)]; adjunction
     gives C^2 = -4 and K_S^2 = -1; K^2 = -1 makes S minimal [DZ99, §1.1].
  2. S carries a (-1)-curve E [DZ99, Lemma 1.10]; p_a(C+2E) = 1
     [DZ99, Corollary 1.6].
  3. With Y the blowdown, p_a(C+2E) = h^0(-K_Y) = 1 [DZ99, Lemma 1.5(1)], and
     2D_0, D_0 in |-K_Y|, is a second member of |-2K_Y| besides C_Y = pi(C), so
     h^0(-2K_Y) >= 2 and the mobile part M of |-2K_S + 2E| is nonzero.
  4. p_a(M) <= p_a(M+P) = 1 [DZ99, Lemma 1.2(1)], so p_a(M) is 0 or 1.
  5. p_a(M) = 0 is rational type, which is never K3-type [DZ99, Theorem 6.3],
     while C reduced makes S K3-type [DZ99, Definition 6.1].  So p_a(M) = 1.
  6. Elliptic type, so basic [DZ99, Theorem 4.3]: S -> P^2 birational, and
     K_S^2 = 9 - k = -1 gives k = 10.
  7. Then C = 6H - 2 sum E_i and C smooth irreducible makes Z = pi(C) an
     irreducible sextic with ten double points and C its normalisation.
