ORIGINAL QUESTION AND RESPONSE

  Question: for every Coble surface S with |-2K_S| one connected smooth curve, is S the blow-up of the ten distinct ordinary nodes of an irreducible plane sextic; or if a non-classical object exists, does M_Co have a further component, and what is its monodromy?

  Response, a third alternative: non-classical objects exist, but they add no connected or irreducible component. The classical substack is open, dense, connected and irreducible, and strictly smaller than M_Co. Therefore there is no new component whose monodromy must be determined, and M_Co is not identified with the classical quotient.
