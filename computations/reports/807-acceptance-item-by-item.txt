THE QUESTION AND THE ANSWER

  Question: is every Coble surface S with |-2K_S| one connected smooth curve
  the blow-up of the ten nodes of an irreducible plane sextic, or does a
  non-classical one exist, and then does M_Co have a further component?

  Answer: a non-classical one exists, and M_Co has no further component.
    thm:coble-smooth-antibicanonical-is-a-plane-blowup    every object is a blow-up of P^2 at ten points, possibly
                                                          infinitely near
    prop:coble-plane-models-are-double-point-sextics      the plane models are exactly the irreducible rational sextics
                                                          whose singular points, infinitely near ones included, are
                                                          double points
    ex:coble-sextics-with-an-a15-point                    Z_8 and Z_64: A_15 + 2A_1 and A_15 + A_1 + A_2
    prop:coble-e8-configuration-excludes-ten-distinct-centres
                                                          an E_8 configuration of (-2)-curves excludes ten disjoint
                                                          (-1)-curves; S_{Z_8}, S_{Z_64} are not classical
    thm:coble-classical-substack-is-open-dense-and-proper the classical substack is open, dense and proper; every object
                                                          is a limit of classical ones; M_Co is connected

THE THREE REPAIRS OF REVIEW 42bl

  1. Statements that said every object is the blow-up of the ten nodes of an irreducible sextic.
     lem:d0-coble-stack-in-involution-stack, clause 2 and its proof: now the proved statement.  Over C every K3 surface
       with an involution of type (11,11,1) has quotient an object of M_Co (steps 4-6 of the proof of
       thm:coble-period-surjectivity), the quotient is a blow-up at the ten double points, infinitely near ones
       included, of an irreducible rational sextic, and a presentation by ten distinct nodes exists exactly for the
       classical objects.  The old necessity proof took the ten nodes from lem:d2-unpolarised-family(1), which is a
       statement about the classical family; the old paragraph on a Noether-Lefschetz condition (iii) is replaced by
       the statement of where the ten-node condition holds.
     lem:coble-moduli-functor-stack, clause 3 and proof (3): the fibre condition is the one of
       def:coble-moduli-functor(1).  Recheck of the proof: (1) and (2) never used a plane model; (3) used the fibre
       condition only as a property of a geometric fibre, and the cyclic cover only through a line bundle D with
       2D = -2K; pi and iota are data of the object and descend by (2).  The lemma holds for the widened class.
     def:enriques-partial-compactification(2) and lem:coble-is-the-boundary-divisor, proof (1), (4): the quotient of a
       complex K3 surface by an involution with fixed locus one smooth rational curve is an object of M_Co
       (steps 5-6 of the proof of thm:coble-period-surjectivity); the sentence "the set of nodes of C is the image
       of R" is removed (C is smooth).
     Two defects of the proof of thm:coble-smooth-antibicanonical-is-a-plane-blowup that the review named are also
     repaired in step 5: p_a(Z) = 10 and the geometric genus of Z is p_a(C) = 0 (the text said p_a(Z) = p_a(C) = 0, and
     that the normalisation of Z is an isomorphism); the multiplicity 2 is proved at every centre, infinitely near
     ones included, as mult_{p_i} C_{i-1} from [C] = 6H - 2 sum E_i with E_i the total transforms.

  2. The W(E_10) statement on the whole stack.
     lem:coble-canonical-class-and-ample-component-in-families (new): for every object S the pair (canonical class,
       ample component) of the invariant lattice; every isomorphism of objects and every transport in a family over
       an arcwise connected, locally simply connected complex space carries it to itself; the group that fixes it is
       W(E_10) in every plane frame.  No stack and no descent.  The earlier proof took a relatively ample class from
       "f is projective (def:coble-moduli-functor(1))"; that definition asks f proper.  The new proof uses Kaehler
       forms: on the universal deformation of the K3 cover, r^* omega_0 for a tubular retraction r tames the complex
       structure of the nearby fibres, and two forms that tame one complex structure are joined by a segment of
       symplectic forms, so their classes lie in one component of the positive cone.  Inputs: [Huy16, Ch. 6,
       Cor. 2.7] (the universal deformation, smooth base), lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families(1),
       lem:transport-order-on-the-classical-coble-stack(4)-(5), [Dol07, (5.12)].
     cor:coble-monodromy-on-m-of-the-whole-stack: clause 1 is unconditional: the group generated by the transports
       of all families over spaces and the isometries of all isomorphisms of objects is W(E_10).  Clause 2, the
       monodromy group of a local system M on the stack M_Co, is conditional on (A), a chart of M_Co
       (research#797 item 1; the volume has a chart for the classical substack only), and on (D), the descent along
       the 2-morphisms (research#797 item 4, rmk:the-descent-stops-at-the-2-morphisms,
       lem:orbifold-monodromy-of-a-nonfree-quotient(3)).  (D) is not proved.  The lower bound no longer cites
       thm:coble-monodromy-on-m-classical as a result: it uses the ten reflections as matrices m(r) of isomorphisms
       of surfaces (lem:coble-image-on-m-is-the-weyl-group(1)-(2), lem:cremona-second-plane-model-general).

  3. thm:coble-monodromy-on-m-classical, the sentence on families: restricted to arcwise connected, locally simply
     connected complex spaces, and proved by the new lemma (4), not by lem:coble-family-base-change applied to a map
     from a space to the stack.
