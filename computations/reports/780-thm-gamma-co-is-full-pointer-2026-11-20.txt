THE THEOREM.  `thm:gamma-co-is-full`, writing/coble/lattices-and-moduli/lattices.md, on
main@7a4f1049.  Let M_Co be the moduli space of Coble surfaces, isomorphic to an open subset of
D(N)/O(N) with N = T_Co, and let Gamma_Co be the group of def:gamma-co-groups(1) -- the image of the
monodromy of the universal family of unpolarized Coble surfaces over that open subset.  Then

    Gamma_Co = O(T_Co).

Inputs, each cited at its step: [@DK13, Prop. 3.2, printed p. 513] for the moduli identification;
contractibility of the bounded symmetric domain D(T_Co); SGA1, Expose X, Corollaire 3.3, printed
p. 213, for the codimension-two statement in its etale form; [@Voi07, Thm. 3.16, printed p. 78] for
the meridian acting as s_delta; @prop:gamma-co-reflective-set for s_delta lying in O(T_Co).

WHAT IT DECIDES.  `cor:gamma-co-inclusions`, same page: under the theorem's hypothesis the reverse
inclusion O~^+(T_Co) <= Gamma_Co is proved, the forward inclusion Gamma_Co <= O~^+(T_Co) is refuted
(O~^+ is a proper subgroup, and @prop:gamma-co-reflective-set(2) exhibits an element of the
difference), and the equality of @conj:gamma-co-stable is therefore false, with it the Gamma_Co form
of @prop:gamma-co-strong-lifting.

WHAT IS OPEN, and it is the reading and not an inclusion:

  (a) whether the family of def:gamma-co-groups(1) is the universal family over the open subset of
      [@DK13, Prop. 3.2] -- if it is, the corollary applies; if it is not, the moduli identification
      that the theorem's proof uses does not apply to it and nothing is decided;
  (b) consequently, only in the second branch, the bulk-versus-boundary question: with the generation
      done in the contractible cover, pi_1^orb of the quotient is generated by the deck group together
      with the meridians, so a failure of Gamma_Co <= O~^+ would have to come either from a met
      Heegner divisor of square other than -2 or from the monodromy of the bulk;
  (c) the (-2)-versus-(-4) Heegner divisor criterion of rmk:gamma-co-nodal-pair-proposal belongs to
      branch (b) only; it is superseded in branch (a).
