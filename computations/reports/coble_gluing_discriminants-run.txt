THE MATHEMATICAL CONSEQUENCE, computed from the group action instead
  If gamma_bw is a block-compatible anti-isometry and gamma_prov is not, then
  phi = gamma_prov^{-1} o gamma_bw is an isometry of q_{S_Co} carrying A_{U(2)} to
  gamma_prov^{-1}(A_{U(2)}^T), which is not A_{U(2)}.  Rather than build phi, the orbit
  partition of O(q_{S_Co}) on A_{S_Co} decides its existence directly:
    O(q_{S_Co}) orbits on the 2048 classes: 6, sizes [1, 1, 496, 496, 527, 527]   (66 s)
    orbits meeting the U(2) block but not contained in it: 2
    witness, in one orbit of size 496:
      x = [1] + [2]   (in the U(2) block, q = 1)
      y = [1] + [3] + [4] + [5] + [7] + [8]   (outside the U(2) block, q = 1)
    so some phi in O(q_{S_Co}) carries x to y and moves the U(2) block:
    phi(A_{U(2)}) != A_{U(2)}.

  Hence the summand decomposition of A_{S_Co} is NOT invariant under O(q_{S_Co}), and
  block-compatibility of an anti-isometry is a property of that anti-isometry, not of
  the pair of forms.  No explicit phi is printed: the witness pair in one orbit is what
  certifies its existence, and the routes to an explicit one are closed at this pin
  (vector_equivalence_witness is definite-only, and no module-morphism constructor for
  the block-wise composite is used here).
