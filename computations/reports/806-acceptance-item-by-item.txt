  SUPERSEDED: this was the reading of the per-loop route before the replacement.
  The current disposition is CURRENT STATUS above.  Historically the note read:
  the lemma proves the per-loop transport of a loop of the quotient and the two
  exact sequences, and it asserts no local system on the quotient.  Whether the
  programme's uses of a tautological local system on a stack are proved is a
  separate question, and the pages answer it as conditional, with the input
  named (the eight rows below).

  The lemma is corrected and proved: lattices.md:177 (statement), :198 (proof).  It
  posits no local system on the quotient orbifold, and it no longer calls an
  arbitrary abelian group a lattice.  For a loop lambda the proof builds the
  transport system on S^1 --- P = lambda^{-1}U~ is a Gamma-torsor, (P x T)/Gamma is
  a covering of S^1 with fibre T --- and computes in it (:202): the chart torsor has
  (0,h) ~ (1,hg^{-1}), the transport system has (0,t) ~ (1,phi(g)t), and the
  transport is phi(g)^{-1}.  What is proved, for U~ a connected manifold, Gamma a
  group acting properly discontinuously with finite stabilizers, U = U~/Gamma and
  phi: Gamma -> Aut(T):
    (1) a loop of U at x is a pair (g, gamma~); the assignment
        lambda |-> psi(lambda)^{-1} is a surjective homomorphism
        pi_1^orb(U,x~) -> Gamma with kernel pi_1(U~,x~), that is,
        1 -> pi_1(U~) -> pi_1^orb(U) -> Gamma -> 1 is exact (:196);
    (2) the representation defined by the transports is rho = phi o psi^{-1}, with
        im rho = phi(Gamma) and ker rho = {lambda : psi(lambda) in ker phi}, and the
        exact sequence 1 -> pi_1(U~) -> ker rho -> ker phi -> 1 (:210); for phi
        injective, im rho = Gamma and ker rho = pi_1(U~).
  The old form claimed im rho = Gamma and ker rho = pi_1(U~) for every Gamma-module;
  the counterexample is research#803's, recorded at lattices.md:213
  (rmk:orbifold-monodromy-depends-on-the-equivariant-structure): U~ a point, T = Z,
  Gamma = C_2 acting trivially, im rho = 1, ker rho = C_2.
