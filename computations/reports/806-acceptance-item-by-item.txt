  "the lemma is replaced by the proved free-action statement of #803
  (local-systems-of-lattices-and-monodromy.md, at research 14830a5d6b), or corrected
  and proved, and every result that cites it is rechecked."

  MET, through the second disjunct.

  The lemma is corrected and proved: lattices.md:177 (statement), :196 (proof).
  What is proved, for U~ a connected manifold, Gamma a group acting properly
  discontinuously with finite stabilizers, U = U~/Gamma, phi: Gamma -> Aut(T) a
  homomorphism and bT = (U~ x T)/Gamma:
    (1) a loop of U at x is a pair (g, gamma~); the assignment
        lambda |-> psi(lambda)^{-1} is a surjective homomorphism
        pi_1^orb(U,x~) -> Gamma with kernel pi_1(U~,x~), that is,
        1 -> pi_1(U~) -> pi_1^orb(U) -> Gamma -> 1 is exact;
    (2) rho = phi o psi^{-1}, so im rho = phi(Gamma),
        ker rho = {lambda : psi(lambda) in ker phi}, and
        1 -> pi_1(U~) -> ker rho -> ker phi -> 1 is exact; if phi is injective, as
        for a subgroup Gamma <= Aut(T) in its defining action, then im rho = Gamma
        and ker rho = pi_1(U~).
  The old form claimed im rho = Gamma and ker rho = pi_1(U~) for every Gamma-module.
  Its counterexample is research#803's, recorded at lattices.md:213
  (rmk:orbifold-monodromy-depends-on-the-equivariant-structure): U~ a point, T = Z,
  Gamma = C_2 acting trivially, where the system is constant, im rho = 1 and
  ker rho = C_2.
