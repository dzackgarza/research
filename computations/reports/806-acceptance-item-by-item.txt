CONVENTIONS CHECKED (def:transport-and-monodromy: gamma delta is delta followed by
gamma)

  - Chart torsor: P|_I = I x Gamma by (s,h) -> (s, h gamma~(s)); at the two ends
    (0,h) represents (0,h x~) and (1,h') represents (1,h' g x~), so the gluing is
    (0,h) ~ (1,h g^{-1}) (:202).
  - Transport system: (0,t) ~ (1, phi(g) t) in (P x T)/Gamma, since
    (0,x~) = (1,x~) and (1,g x~, phi(g)t) = (1,x~,t); the transport around S^1 is
    phi(g)^{-1} (:202).
  - Product: lambda = (g,gamma~), mu = (h,delta~); lambda mu is mu followed by
    lambda, whose lift from x~ is delta~ followed by the h-translate of gamma~ and
    ends at h g x~; so psi(lambda mu) = h g = psi(mu) psi(lambda) and
    rho(lambda mu) = phi(hg)^{-1} = phi(g)^{-1} phi(h)^{-1} = rho(lambda) rho(mu)
    (:204).
  - Item 2: with (sigma,gamma~) and (tau,delta~) the same computation gives
    tau sigma and phi_{tau sigma}^{-1} = phi_sigma^{-1} phi_tau^{-1} (:1170).
  - lem:d2-label-lift(2) was corrected earlier to the same convention: the composite
    ends at sigma' sigma s and the homomorphism is sigma -> sigma^{-1} (:1120, :1129).
