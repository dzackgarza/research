  The lemma is corrected and proved: lattices.md:177 (statement), :196 (proof).
  The local system is defined by its pullbacks along maps from topological spaces
  (:181), and the transport is computed on the space S^1 (:200).  What is proved,
  for U~ a connected manifold, Gamma a group acting properly discontinuously with
  finite stabilizers, U = U~/Gamma, phi: Gamma -> Aut(T) and T the local system
  defined by the equivariant structure g.(u~,t) = (g u~, phi(g) t):
    (1) a loop of U at x is a pair (g, gamma~); the assignment
        lambda |-> psi(lambda)^{-1} is a surjective homomorphism
        pi_1^orb(U,x~) -> Gamma with kernel pi_1(U~,x~), that is,
        1 -> pi_1(U~) -> pi_1^orb(U) -> Gamma -> 1 is exact (:194);
    (2) rho = phi o psi^{-1}, so im rho = phi(Gamma),
        ker rho = {lambda : psi(lambda) in ker phi}, and
        1 -> pi_1(U~) -> ker rho -> ker phi -> 1 is exact (:208); if phi is
        injective, as for a subgroup Gamma <= Aut(T) in its defining action, then
        im rho = Gamma and ker rho = pi_1(U~).
  The old form claimed im rho = Gamma and ker rho = pi_1(U~) for every Gamma-module;
  its counterexample is research#803's, recorded at lattices.md:211
  (rmk:orbifold-monodromy-depends-on-the-equivariant-structure): U~ a point, T = Z,
  Gamma = C_2 acting trivially, where the system is constant, im rho = 1 and
  ker rho = C_2.

  Inputs, each a numbered statement read in Noohi, Foundations of topological stacks

THE REVIEW'S FINDINGS, AND WHERE EACH IS DISPOSED OF

  R1. The tau construction is contradictory at a fixed point (U~ = *, Gamma = C_2,
      T = Z, phi(s) = -id forces tau o (-id) = tau), and an arbitrary t in T is not
      the value of a Gamma-equivariant section at a fixed point.  REMOVED.  The
      proof constructs no tau and takes no stalks.  For a loop with pair (g,gamma~)
      it pulls back the torsor and computes on S^1: lambda^{-1}T = (P x T)/Gamma,
      P = lambda^{-1}U~ trivialised by gamma~, the ends identified by
      (0,t) ~ (1, phi(g) t), and the transport is phi(g)^{-1} (:200).  In the
      reviewer's countermodel the gluing is (0,t) ~ (1,-t) and the transport is -1,
      which is the monodromy of the system.

  R2. The kernel argument is false with stabilizers: at U~ = * every lifted path is
      closed.  REPLACED (:206).  The kernel is described on the pair: ker psi
      consists of the pairs (1,gamma~) with gamma~ a loop of U~, and two such pairs
      are equal exactly when the loops of U~ are homotopic; hence
      ker psi = p_* pi_1(U~) = pi_1(U~) by Noohi, Proposition 18.15.  The isotropy
      loop at a fixed point has pair (-1, const) and is not in the kernel.

  R3. The sheaf/stalk/interval-transport calculus on the orbifold is not a standing
      premise of this page.  MET by not using it.  The lemma defines T by its
      pullbacks along maps from spaces (:181) and computes the transport with
      ordinary transport on S^1 (:200); no stalks, inverse images, or sheaves on
      the stack beyond that definition are used.

  R4. The defining-action uses were unproved as consequences of the submitted
      lemma.  MET once R1--R3 are repaired: each of those results now cites clause
      (2) with an injective phi, and im rho = phi(Gamma) = Gamma follows.

  R5. lem:d2-label-lift(2) had the product order backwards and was not rechecked;
      the same defective homomorphism is reused at :1145.  REPAIRED.  The statement
      now gives the homomorphism (sigma,gamma~) |-> sigma^{-1} and the
      anti-homomorphism (sigma,gamma~) |-> sigma (:1095); the proof computes the
      composite as delta~ followed by the sigma'-translate of gamma~, ending at
      sigma' sigma s (:1104); and the use in
      lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families(3) is corrected to the same map and to
      the transport by sigma^{-1} (:1145).  The earlier "MET, unchanged" is
      withdrawn.

  R6. The earlier report named lem:d2-local-system(3) at :1145.  CORRECTED: the
      block is lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families, item (3).
