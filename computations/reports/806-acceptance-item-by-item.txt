  The review asks for: T = (U~ x T)/Gamma is a local system on the orbifold U, and
  its monodromy along every loop equals the per-loop transport.  New
  lem:tautological-system-descends at lattices.md:218, proof :228, proves it.

  1. For every map f: W -> U from a topological space, f^*T is the sheaf whose
     sections over an open O are the Gamma-equivariant maps f^{-1}U~|_O -> T,
     with f^{-1}U~ = W x_U U~; this is the sheaf of sections of the covering
     (f^{-1}U~ x T)/Gamma -> W, hence locally constant with fibre T, and a local
     system of lattices when T is one and phi preserves its form.  The chart and
     its pullback are a Gamma-torsor and a covering: Noohi, Definition 3.3,
     Proposition 3.4, Definition 4.5, Example 4.6(1), Definition 18.10.
  2. (fh)^*T = h^*f^*T canonically and each f^*T is an ordinary sheaf on W; these
     are the sheaf conditions on the site of U, whose covers are the open
     coverings of spaces (Noohi, §3 and §4), a sheaf on a category fibred in
     groupoids being [@stacks-06TR].  So T is a local system on the topological
     stack U, the descent of the equivariant system, the object written
     (U~ x T)/Gamma.
  3. p^{-1}U~ = U~ x_U U~ = Gamma x U~ (Noohi, Proposition 3.4), so p^*T = U~ x T
     is trivial: T is locally constant.
  4. For a loop lambda with pair (g,gamma~), lambda^*T is the transport system
     (P x T)/Gamma of @lem:orbifold-monodromy-of-a-quotient(2) with
     P = lambda^{-1}U~, whose transport is phi(g)^{-1}; every loop has such a pair,
     so the monodromy of T along every loop is the per-loop transport and the
     representation is rho = phi o psi^{-1}.

  This is the descent that the earlier objection ("defined by its pullbacks")
  named: the pullback is constructed as equivariant maps and the sheaf axioms are
  verified, rather than assumed.

CONVENTIONS CHECKED (def:transport-and-monodromy: gamma delta is delta followed by
gamma)

  - Chart torsor: P|_I = I x Gamma by (s,h) -> (s, h gamma~(s)); at the two ends
    (0,h) represents (0,h x~) and (1,h') represents (1,h' g x~), so the gluing is
    (0,h) ~ (1,h g^{-1}) (:202).
  - Transport system: (0,t) ~ (1, phi(g) t) in (P x T)/Gamma, since
    (0,x~) = (1,x~) and (1,g x~, phi(g)t) = (1,x~,t); the transport around S^1 is
    phi(g)^{-1} (:202).
  - Product: lambda = (g,gamma~), mu = (h,delta~); lambda mu is mu followed by
    lambda, whose lift from x~ is delta~ followed by the h-translate of gamma~ and
    ends at h g x~; so psi(lambda mu) = h g = psi(mu) psi(lambda) and
    rho(lambda mu) = phi(hg)^{-1} = phi(g)^{-1} phi(h)^{-1} = rho(lambda) rho(mu)
    (:204).
  - Item 2: with (sigma,gamma~) and (tau,delta~) the same computation gives
    tau sigma and phi_{tau sigma}^{-1} = phi_sigma^{-1} phi_tau^{-1} (:1170).
  - lem:d2-label-lift(2) was corrected earlier to the same convention: the composite
    ends at sigma' sigma s and the homomorphism is sigma -> sigma^{-1} (:1120, :1129).
