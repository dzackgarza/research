ITEM 1 -- one of the statements is proved and the other corrected: MET.

  Which one was false: item (3).  Refuted at
  writing/coble/lattices-and-moduli/moduli-construction.md:872 with its proof at :885, commit
  80f692f038.  M + T_Co has index 2^11 in L_K3, since |A_M| = |A_{T_Co}| = 2^11 and L_K3 is
  unimodular (moduli-construction.md:846, lattices.md:243); so sigma + id is an isometry of a
  proper sublattice, and by cor:lifting-unimodular it extends exactly when
  rho-bar_T(id) = gamma rho-bar_M(sigma) gamma^{-1}, which fails for every non-identity
  permutation (:855, :846).

  What is proved in its place: lem:relabelling-discriminant-action,
  moduli-construction.md:842 with proof :853-860, commit 80f692f038.  (1) the index 2^11 and
  that no isometry of L_K3 is sigma on M and the identity on T_Co (:846); (2) every permutation
  does extend, the class of its restriction to T_Co modulo Otilde(T_Co) is independent of the
  extension, and sigma -> gamma rho-bar_M(sigma) gamma^{-1} is an injective homomorphism onto
  Theta of order 10! (:847-852); (3) the relabelling arrows realise exactly Theta, so
  rho-bar(Gamma_Co(Bbar)) = Theta and the index is 10! (:850).

  The other statement stands: lem:ten-a1-permutation-forces-strictness,
  moduli-construction.md:371 with proof :379-381 -- its gluing argument is complete -- and the
  strictness is independently the arithmetic of thm:gamma-co-sextic-group(3),
  lattices.md:484-495, where [Gamma_10A_1 : Otilde^+(T_Co)] = |Theta| = 10! > 1.

  Reviews: gvy8 and jh5x read this item as the narrow disjunct and no wider ("MET only as the
  narrow disjunct"; "the old assertion ... is correctly refuted and replaced"); lyil reads it
  MET.
