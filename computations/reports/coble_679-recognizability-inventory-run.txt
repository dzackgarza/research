Conclusion.  In the version-controlled deposits, recognizability is asserted for the
fixed locus of a nonsymplectic involution and for the ramification divisor; for the flex
divisor AE23a states it as a conjecture; and for a divisor built from a section and the
fibres of an elliptic fibration -- ABE22's s + m sum f_i, the unit's only candidate --
nothing states it either way, ABE22 itself included, whose paper does not use the word.  So question 1 of @qst:recognizable-semifan cannot be settled
from these deposits, and the input it needs is a recognizability proof for that divisor.
