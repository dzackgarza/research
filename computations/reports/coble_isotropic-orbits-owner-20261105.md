## What returns


So at the fixed pin the orthogonal group of the discriminant form of `T_Co` is built, its
order is `46 998 591 897 600`, and the 528 isotropic classes are recovered — the three
numbers `prop:coble-discriminant-group` and `prop:coble-q-fibers` state, now from a run at
the new pin rather than from history.

**And the stabilizer of the class of `ζ/2` is the whole group.** That is
`prop:coble-discriminant-group` again — every isometry of `q_{T_Co}` fixes that class — and
it settles a reading question in #689: "the stabilizer of the polarization class" cannot mean
the stabilizer of `ζ/2`, since that is `O(A_{T_Co})` itself, whose orbits on the 528
isotropic classes are the two of `thm:coble-isotropic-class-orbits`, `[1, 527]`, and not
`[1, 255, 272]`. The group that gives `[1, 255, 272]` must be the stabilizer of a class of
*value* `1`, the class of `½h̃_Co` as `GAPS.md` records it; and its order is forced:
`46 998 591 897 600 / 496 = 94 755 225 600`, the number that record carries, since the
`q = 1` fibre has `496` elements (`prop:coble-q-fibers`) and one orbit.

## What does not return, and what that costs

```
value-1 class found: [10] | q = [1] in Fraction Field of Integer Ring / (2)Integer Ring
```
then, at `O.stabilizer_of_element(c1)` for that class, no result within **1800 s**, at which
point the run was cut. So the two orbit decompositions remain unsettled:

- #689 `[1, 255, 272]` under the value-1 stabilizer, and
- #688 `[1, 2, 120, 135, 270]` under `G_Co`,

and the route attempted — enumerate the whole finite orthogonal group, then stabilize an
element inside it — is the expensive one. The cheaper shape is the one the page's own remark
describes: the stabilizer as a subgroup generated by named isometries, i.e. the loop over the
`496` classes avoided by defining the group by its action rather than by its order. That is a
computation for the next unit and not a claim about either list, which are still unproved.
