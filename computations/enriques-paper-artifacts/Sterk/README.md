# Sterk cusp artifacts

`Sterk_1` … `Sterk_5` hold plots of the elliptic subdiagrams of the Coxeter diagrams at the five Sterk 0-cusps of the Enriques period space (Sterk, *Compactifications of the Period Space of Enriques Surfaces I*, Math. Z. 207 (1991), 1–36). Generated by `archives/notebooks/Classification of Sterk Elliptic Subdiagrams.ipynb` from the root data in `computations/scripts/init.sage`.

**Known discrepancy:** the in-tree Vinberg computations (VinAl and Julia) yield 9–10 simple roots with 1–2 ideal vertices per cusp, against Sterk's published counts 12, 10, 12, 11, 14. Working hypothesis and the verbatim computed root sets: `notes/computations/sterk-root-count-discrepancy.md`.
