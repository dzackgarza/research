$\Lambda_h(d) = U^2 \oplus A_1(-1)^2 \oplus \langle -2d \rangle$ for $d \geq 1$: the orthogonal complement in $\Lambda = U^3 \oplus A_1(-1)^2$ of a polarization $h$ with $b(h, h) = 2d$ and divisibility $1$. The period domain $M_{OG6,2d^1} = \mathcal{D}/\operatorname{Mon}^2(\Lambda, h)$ is a partial compactification of the moduli space of primitively polarized hyperkähler sixfolds of OG6 type of degree $2d$ and divisibility $1$; it is irreducible and nonempty for every $d \geq 1$ (I. Barros, P. Beri, L. Flapan and B. Williams, "Cones of Noether–Lefschetz divisors and moduli spaces of hyperkähler manifolds", Math. Ann. 394 (2026), DOI 10.1007/s00208-026-03372-1, Section 4). The ambient lattice is the Beauville–Bogomolov–Fujiki lattice of OG6 type (A. Rapagnetta, "On the Beauville form of the known irreducible symplectic varieties", Math. Ann. 340 (2008), 77-95, the table of the introduction); its monodromy is the full group $O^+(\Lambda)$ (G. Mongardi and A. Rapagnetta, "Monodromy and birational geometry of O'Grady's sixfolds", J. Math. Pures Appl. 146 (2021), 31-68).
