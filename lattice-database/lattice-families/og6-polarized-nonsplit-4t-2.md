$\Lambda_h(t) = U^2 \oplus Q_t$ for $t \geq 1$, with $Q_t = \left[\begin{smallmatrix}-2&0&1\\0&-2&1\\1&1&-2t\end{smallmatrix}\right]$: the orthogonal complement in $\Lambda = U^3 \oplus A_1(-1)^2$ of a polarization $h$ with $b(h, h) = 2d$ and divisibility $2$, where $d = 4t - 2$. The period domain $M_{OG6,2d^2} = \mathcal{D}/\operatorname{Mon}^2(\Lambda, h)$ is a partial compactification of the moduli space of primitively polarized hyperkähler sixfolds of OG6 type of degree $2d$ and divisibility $2$; it is irreducible, and for divisibility $2$ it is nonempty exactly for $d \equiv 2, 3 \pmod 4$ (I. Barros, P. Beri, L. Flapan and B. Williams, "Cones of Noether–Lefschetz divisors and moduli spaces of hyperkähler manifolds", Math. Ann. 394 (2026), DOI 10.1007/s00208-026-03372-1, Section 4). The ambient lattice is the Beauville–Bogomolov–Fujiki lattice of OG6 type (A. Rapagnetta, "On the Beauville form of the known irreducible symplectic varieties", Math. Ann. 340 (2008), 77-95, the table of the introduction).
