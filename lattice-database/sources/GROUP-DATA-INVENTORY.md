For a nondegenerate integral lattice with Gram matrix $B$, the finite group $A_L=L^*/L$ and its bilinear form are determined by $B$; for even $L$ so is $q_L$. An integral matrix $g$ satisfying $g^\mathsf TBg=B$ induces $x+L\mapsto gx+L$. Thus the canonical homomorphism $\rho_L:O(L)\to O(A_L,q_L)$ needs no source-defined encoding. A complete generating set for $O(L)$ permits direct computation of its finite image. A set of generators for a specified proper subgroup computes only that subgroup's image. Höhn–Mason's Table 1 indices are claims about the full orthogonal groups named in its column definitions; its stabilizer matrices alone do not prove those full-group indices.

The [current definite-lattice orbit computation](../src/latticedb/sage_genus.py) already obtains generators of $O(L)$ with PARI `qfauto`, reduces them on $A_L$, and enumerates their image. This is a direct route to its order and index for definite lattices. For indefinite lattices in the [Miranda–Morrison range](../theory/orbits.md#mm-cokernel), the local formula computes $MM(L)$ without first listing generators of infinite $O(L)$. The order of a subgroup, the index of its image, and $MM(L)$ refer to different groups unless their domains have been identified.
