Let $L$ be an [indefinite](lattices.html#definiteness) lattice.

## Isotropic lattices {#isotropic}

::: {.definition}
$L$ is *isotropic* when $b(x, x) = 0$ for some nonzero $x \in L$, and *anisotropic* otherwise.
:::

## Hyperbolic lattices {#hyperbolic}

::: {.definition}
A lattice of rank $n + 1 \geq 2$ is *hyperbolic* when its form is nondegenerate with signature $(1, n)$ or $(n, 1)$.
:::

## Reflective lattices {#reflective}

::: {.definition}
A hyperbolic lattice $L$ is *reflective* when the subgroup of $O(L)$ generated by the [reflections](roots.html#hyperbolic) $s_r$, $r \in \Phi(L)$, has finite index in $O(L)$.
:::

This property is [declared](records.html#declared); the notes cite its source.
