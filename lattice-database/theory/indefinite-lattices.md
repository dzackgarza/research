Let $L$ be an [indefinite](lattices.html#definiteness) lattice: $b(x, x)$ takes both signs.

## Isotropic lattices {#isotropic}

$L$ is *isotropic* when $b(x, x) = 0$ for some nonzero $x \in L$.

## Hyperbolic lattices {#hyperbolic}

A lattice of rank $n + 1 \geq 2$ is *hyperbolic* when its form is nondegenerate with signature $(1, n)$ or $(n, 1)$.

## Reflective lattices {#reflective}

A hyperbolic lattice $L$ is *reflective* when the subgroup of $O(L)$ that the reflections $s_r$, $r \in \Phi(L)$, generate has finite index in $O(L)$.
A record declares this, and its notes give the source.
[Roots](roots.html#hyperbolic) describes the group that the reflections generate.
