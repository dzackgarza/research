Let $L$ be an [integral](lattices.html#integral) lattice that is [nondegenerate](lattices.html#degenerate).

## Dual lattice {#dual-lattice}

::: {.definition}
The *dual lattice* $L^*$ is $\{x \in L \otimes_{\mathbb{Z}} \mathbb{Q} : b(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ for all } y \in L\}$, with the $\mathbb{Q}$-bilinear extension of $b$.
:::

$L^*$ contains $L$, and its [chosen basis](named-lattices.html#dual-lattices) is dual to that of $L$.

## Discriminant group {#discriminant-group}

::: {.definition}
The *discriminant group* $A_L$ of $L$ is the cokernel of the correlation $L \to \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(L, \mathbb{Z})$, $x \mapsto b(x, -)$.
:::

::: {.proposition}
The isomorphism $L^* \to \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(L, \mathbb{Z})$, $y \mapsto b(y, -)$, gives $A_L \cong L^*/L$, a finite group of order $|\det L|$.
:::

The page states $A_L$ by its invariant factors $d_1 \mid d_2 \mid \dots$, each greater than 1; the empty list is the trivial group.

::: {.definition}
The *discriminant form* is $b_{A_L}(x + L, y + L) = b(x, y) + \mathbb{Z}$, with values in $\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$.
:::

## Unimodular lattices {#unimodular}

::: {.definition}
$L$ is *unimodular* when the correlation $L \to \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(L, \mathbb{Z})$ is an isomorphism; equivalently $A_L = 0$, and equivalently $\det L = \pm 1$.
:::

## 2-elementary lattices {#two-elementary}

::: {.definition}
$L$ is *2-elementary* when $A_L \cong (\mathbb{Z}/2)^a$ with $a \geq 1$.
:::

::: {.definition}
An even lattice $L$ with $2 A_L = 0$ has *Nikulin's invariants* $(r, a, \delta)$: $r$ is the rank, $A_L \cong (\mathbb{Z}/2)^a$, and $\delta = 0$ exactly when $b(x, x)$ is an integer for every $x$ in the dual lattice, else $\delta = 1$.
The even unimodular lattices are the case $a = 0$, with $\delta = 0$.
:::

::: {.proposition}
$\delta = 0$ exactly when every diagonal entry of $G^{-1}$ is an integer, for the Gram matrix $G$ of $L$.
:::

::: {.proof}
The basis of $L^*$ dual to the chosen basis of $L$ has Gram matrix $G^{-1}$.
Since $2 L^* \subseteq L$, every $2 b(x, y)$ with $x, y \in L^*$ is an integer, so for $x = \sum_i c_i f_i$ the value $b(x, x) = \sum_i c_i^2 (G^{-1})_{ii} + \sum_{i < j} 2 c_i c_j (G^{-1})_{ij}$ is an integer for every $x$ exactly when every $(G^{-1})_{ii}$ is.
:::

::: {.theorem data-name="Nikulin"}
An indefinite even 2-elementary lattice is determined by its signature and $(r, a, \delta)$ ([Nikulin 1980](https://doi.org/10.1070/IM1980v014n01ABEH001060): V. V. Nikulin, *Integral symmetric bilinear forms and some of their applications*, Math.
USSR-Izv.
14 (1980), 103–167).
:::

::: {.theorem data-name="Nikulin"}
An even hyperbolic lattice $S$ with $2 A_S = 0$ is determined by $(r, a, \delta)$.
The isomorphism classes of involutions of the K3 lattice whose invariant lattice is hyperbolic correspond to the isomorphism classes of their invariant lattices, which are the even hyperbolic lattices $S$ with $2 A_S = 0$ that have a primitive embedding in the K3 lattice; Table 1 of the source lists them, and they are the invariant lattices of the nonsymplectic involutions of K3 surfaces ([Nikulin 1983](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01094757): V. V. Nikulin, *Factor groups of groups of automorphisms of hyperbolic forms with respect to subgroups generated by 2-reflections. Algebrogeometric applications*, J. Soviet Math. 22 (1983), 1401–1475, Theorem 4.3.2, Table 1 and Remark 4.5.3).
:::
