The Conway group $Co_0 := O(\mathcal L)$, the automorphism group of the Leech lattice $\mathcal L$, with order $2^{22} \cdot 3^{9} \cdot 5^{4} \cdot 7^{2} \cdot 11 \cdot 13 \cdot 23$ (Laza–Zheng, Section 3.1). Its center is $\mu_2 = \{\pm \mathrm{id}\}$, and the quotient $Co_1 := Co_0/Z(Co_0)$ is one of the largest sporadic simple groups. A group of symplectic automorphisms of a K3 surface, of a hyperkähler manifold of $K3^{[n]}$ type, or of a cubic fourfold can be realized as a subgroup of $Co_0$, so only the prime factors $2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13$ and $23$ can occur in its order.
