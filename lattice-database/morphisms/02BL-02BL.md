The generators of the pointwise stabilizer $C$ of the fixed-point lattice $B$ of `lattices[8,7]` of Höhn and Mason, arXiv:1505.06420, as automorphisms of the coinvariant lattice $A = B^\perp$.
Through the gluing of $A(-1)$ and the invariant lattice into the K3 lattice, $C$ is the group $\mathfrak{G}_{16} \cong D_{10}$ of symplectic isometries.
