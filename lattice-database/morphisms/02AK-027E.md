The invariant and coinvariant lattices of the finite symplectic groups of Hashimoto in the K3 lattice. The anti-isometry maps the generators of the normal form of SageMath of one discriminant form to those of the other twisted by $-1$; three hyperbolic planes are split off and `qfisom` of PARI/GP maps the remainder onto $E_8(-1)^2$.
