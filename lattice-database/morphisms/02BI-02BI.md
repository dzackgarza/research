The generators of the pointwise stabilizer $C$ of the fixed-point lattice $B$ of `lattices[8,4]` of Höhn and Mason, arXiv:1505.06420, as automorphisms of the coinvariant lattice $A = B^\perp$.
Through the gluing of $A(-1)$ and the invariant lattice into the K3 lattice, $C$ is the group $\mathfrak{G}_{22} \cong C_2 \times D_8$ of symplectic isometries.
`latticedb check` computes these maps again from `sources/hoehn_mason/lattices_8_4.json` with `latticedb.hoehn_mason.automorphisms` and the order of the group they generate.
