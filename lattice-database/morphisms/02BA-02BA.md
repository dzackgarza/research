The generators of the pointwise stabilizer $C$ of the fixed-point lattice $B$ of `lattices[6,21]` of Höhn and Mason, arXiv:1505.06420, as automorphisms of the coinvariant lattice $A = B^\perp$. Through the gluing of $A(-1)$ and the invariant lattice into the K3 lattice, $C$ is the group $\mathfrak{G}_{32} \cong \mathrm{Hol}(C_5)$ of symplectic isometries. `latticedb check` computes these maps again from `sources/hoehn_mason/lattices_6_21.json` with `latticedb.hoehn_mason.automorphisms` and the order of the group they generate.
