The Hilbert scheme of length-n subschemes of a K3 surface is an irreducible holomorphic symplectic variety of complex dimension 2n [@Beauville1983].
Göttsche's generating function gives its Hodge–Poincaré series [@Goettsche1994].
The [hyperkähler catalogue](https://hyperkaehler.info/) lists values for individual n [@HyperkaehlerInfo].
