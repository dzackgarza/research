For an abelian surface A, the generalized Kummer variety is the fiber over zero of the sum map from A^[n+1] to A. Its complex dimension is 2n [@Beauville1983].
The Hodge series is given by Göttsche and Soergel [@GoettscheSoergel1993].
The [hyperkähler catalogue](https://hyperkaehler.info/) lists individual values [@HyperkaehlerInfo].
