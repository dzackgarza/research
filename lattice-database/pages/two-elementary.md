---
title: 2-elementary lattices
summary: Integral lattices with discriminant group $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^a$, $a \geq 1$.
where:
  properties: [2-elementary]
---

A nondegenerate integral lattice is [2-elementary](../theory/discriminant-forms.html#two-elementary) when its discriminant group is isomorphic to $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^a$ with $a \geq 1$.
