---
title: 2-elementary lattices
summary: Integral lattices whose discriminant group is a sum of copies of $\mathbb{Z}/2$.
where:
  properties: [2-elementary]
---

The *discriminant group* of a nondegenerate integral lattice $(L, b)$ is the cokernel of the correlation $L \to \operatorname{Hom}(L, \mathbb{Z})$, $x \mapsto b(x, -)$. The lattice is *2-elementary* when this group is $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^a$ with $a \geq 1$.
