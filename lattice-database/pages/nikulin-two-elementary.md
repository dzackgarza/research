title: Nikulin's 2-elementary lattices
summary: The invariant lattices of the nonsymplectic involutions of K3 surfaces, by Nikulin's invariants $(r, a, \delta)$.
where:
  families: nikulin-two-elementary

V. V. Nikulin, *Factor groups of groups of automorphisms of hyperbolic forms with respect to subgroups generated by 2-reflections. Algebrogeometric applications*, J. Soviet Math. 22 (1983), 1401–1475, [DOI 10.1007/BF01094757](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01094757), lists in Table 1 the even [hyperbolic](../theory/indefinite-lattices.html#hyperbolic) lattices $L$ with $2 A_L = 0$ that have a primitive embedding in the K3 lattice.
By Remark 4.5.3 these are the invariant lattices of the nonsymplectic involutions of K3 surfaces, and by Theorem 4.3.2 [Nikulin's invariants](../theory/discriminant-forms.html#two-elementary) $(r, a, \delta)$ determine each of them.
The table has 75 rows.

The members of this collection are the 62 rows with $a < r$, one record for each row.
Each record states $\delta$, and its page shows $(r, a, \delta)$.

The 13 rows with $a = r$ are the lattices $M(2)$ for a unimodular lattice $M$, since $2 L^* \subseteq L$ and $|A_L| = 2^r$ give $L^* = \tfrac{1}{2} L$.
The catalogue records $M$ and not its twist $M(2)$:

$M(2)$ has $\delta = 0$ exactly when $M$ is even: the Gram matrix of $M(2)$ is $2G$, and the diagonal entries of $(2G)^{-1} = \tfrac{1}{2} G^{-1}$ are integers exactly when those of $G^{-1}$ are even, which for a unimodular $M$ holds exactly when $M$ is even.
