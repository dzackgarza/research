title: Even hyperbolic 2-elementary lattices with $r + a = 22$
summary: The lattices of Alexeev's Theorem 1.1, with the reflectivity of each.
where:
  families: r-plus-a-22
---

An even 2-elementary lattice has invariants $(r, a, \delta)$: $r$ is the rank, the discriminant group is $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^a$, and $\delta = 0$ exactly when $b(x, x)$ is an integer for every $x$ in the dual lattice.

The members of this collection are the even hyperbolic 2-elementary lattices with $r + a = 22$.
The field `hyperbolic.reflective` of each record is from V. Alexeev, *Reflective hyperbolic 2-elementary lattices, K3 surfaces and hyperkahler manifolds*, [arXiv:2209.09110v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.09110v4), Theorem 1.1: the lattices with $(r, a, \delta)$ equal to $(17, 5, 1)$, $(18, 4, 1)$ and $(19, 3, 1)$ are not reflective, and the other lattices on this line are reflective.
