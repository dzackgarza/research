The members of this collection are the even [hyperbolic](../theory/indefinite-lattices.html#hyperbolic) 2-elementary lattices with $r + a = 22$, for [Nikulin's invariants](../theory/discriminant-forms.html#two-elementary) $(r, a, \delta)$.
The [reflectivity](../theory/indefinite-lattices.html#reflective) of each record is from V. Alexeev, *Reflective hyperbolic 2-elementary lattices, K3 surfaces and hyperkahler manifolds*, [arXiv:2209.09110v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.09110v4), Theorem 1.1: the lattices with $(r, a, \delta)$ equal to $(17, 5, 1)$, $(18, 4, 1)$ and $(19, 3, 1)$ are not reflective, and the other lattices with $r + a = 22$ are reflective.
