---
title: Degenerate lattices
summary: Lattices on which the correlation has a nonzero kernel.
where:
  properties: [degenerate]
---

A lattice $(L, b)$ is *degenerate* when the correlation $L \to \operatorname{Hom}(L, \mathbb{Q})$, $x \mapsto b(x, -)$, has a nonzero kernel. That kernel is the *radical* of $b$, and the determinant of the Gram tensor is $0$. The discriminant group and the genus symbol are defined for nondegenerate lattices, and the schema rejects them on these records.
