Let $L$ be a nondegenerate integral lattice.
A *root* of $L$ is a primitive $r \in L$ with $b(r, r) \neq 0$ and $s_r \in O(L)$, where $s_r(x) = x - \frac{2 b(x, r)}{b(r, r)} r$; equivalently, $2 b(r, L) \subseteq b(r, r) \mathbb{Z}$.
For $S \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$, let $\Phi_S(L)$ be the set of roots with $b(r, r) \in S$ and $R_S(L) := \mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(L)$.

A positive definite $L$ is a *root lattice* when $R_{\{2\}}(L) = L$; a negative definite $L$, when $R_{\{-2\}}(L) = L$.
A root lattice is an orthogonal sum of lattices $A_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$ and $E_8$ (Witt's theorem: Conway and Sloane, *Sphere Packings, Lattices and Groups*, 3rd edition, Chapter 4, §3).

The field `root_system` of a definite integral record states the ADE type of $\Phi_{\{\pm 2\}}(L)$.
The database records no $\Phi_S(L)$ for another $S$, and none for an indefinite lattice.
$R_{\{2\}}(L)$ is not primitive in $L$ in general: $R_{\{2\}}(\mathbb{Z}^n) = D_n$ has index 2 for $n \geq 4$.
The primitive sublattices of $L$ generated by subsets of $\Phi_{\{2\}}(L)$ have no maximum in general, and two maximal ones need not be isometric.

The records of the family [`root-lattice`](../database.html?family=root-lattice) give $A_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$ and $E_8$ in a basis of simple roots $e_1, \dots, e_n$, numbered as SageMath's `CartanMatrix` numbers them.
The component $b(e_i, e_j)$ is the entry $(i, j)$ of the Cartan matrix.
The [dual lattices](../database.html?family=dual-root-lattice) of these lattices are separate records.
