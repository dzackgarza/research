---
title: Root lattices
summary: The lattices $A_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$ and $E_8$, in a basis of simple roots.
where:
  families: root-lattice
---

Each record gives the lattice in a basis of simple roots $e_1, \dots, e_n$, numbered as SageMath's `CartanMatrix` numbers them. The component $b(e_i, e_j)$ is the entry $(i, j)$ of the Cartan matrix, so $b(e_i, e_i) = 2$ and the lattice is positive definite and even.

The [dual lattices](../database.html?family=dual-root-lattice) and the [orthogonal sums](../database.html?family=root-lattice-sum) of root lattices are separate records.
