Let $L$ be a lattice.
The form $b$ can be indefinite or degenerate, and its values need not be integers.

## Roots

A *root* of $L$ is a primitive $r \in L$ with $b(r, r) \neq 0$ and $s_r \in O(L)$, where $s_r(x) = x - \frac{2 b(x, r)}{b(r, r)} r$; equivalently, $2 b(r, L) \subseteq b(r, r) \mathbb{Z}$.
$\Phi(L)$ is the set of roots of $L$, and $\Phi_S(L) := \{r \in \Phi(L) : b(r, r) \in S\}$ for $S \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$.

$L$ is an *$S$-root lattice* when $L = \mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(L)$, and a *root lattice* when $L = \mathbb{Z}\Phi(L)$.

Let $L(k)$ be $L$ with the form $k b$, $k \in \mathbb{Q}^\times$.
Then $\Phi(L(k)) = \Phi(L)$ and $\Phi_{kS}(L(k)) = \Phi_S(L)$: $E_8(2)$ is a $\{4\}$-root lattice.

Let $L$ be integral and nondegenerate, and $r \in \Phi(L)$ with $b(r, L) = d\mathbb{Z}$, $d > 0$.
Then $b(r, r) \in \{\pm d, \pm 2d\}$, and $d$ divides the exponent of the discriminant group.
Each $r \in L$ with $b(r, r) \in \{\pm 1, \pm 2\}$ is a root.

## Root sublattices

$\mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(L)$ is stable under $O(L)$.
It is not primitive in $L$ in general: $\mathbb{Z}\Phi_{\{2\}}(\mathbb{Z}^n) = \{x : \sum_i x_i \in 2\mathbb{Z}\}$ has index 2 for $n \geq 2$.

Let $P_S(L)$ be the set of the primitive sublattices $M \subseteq L$ with $M = \mathbb{Z}(M \cap \Phi_S(L))$, ordered by inclusion.
Each $M \in P_S(L)$ is an $S$-root lattice, because $M \cap \Phi(L) \subseteq \Phi(M)$.
$P_S(L)$ has a maximum exactly when $\mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(L)$ is primitive in $L$, and the maximum is then $\mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(L)$: each $\mathbb{Z}r$, $r \in \Phi_S(L)$, is in $P_S(L)$.
$R_S(L)$ is the maximum of $P_S(L)$ when it exists, and $R(L) := R_{\mathbb{Q}}(L)$.
When $\mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(L)$ is not primitive, $P_S(L)$ has maximal elements and no maximum.
The maximal elements of $P_{\{2\}}(\mathbb{Z}^n)$, $n \geq 2$, are the $2^{n-1}$ sublattices $\{x : \sum_i \epsilon_i x_i = 0\}$, $\epsilon \in \{\pm 1\}^n$; each is isometric to $A_{n-1}$.

## Hyperbolic lattices

Let $L$ have signature $(1, n)$ and $S \subseteq \mathbb{Q}_{<0}$.
$W_S(L) := \langle s_r : r \in \Phi_S(L) \rangle$ is a discrete group generated by reflections of the hyperbolic space $\mathbb{H}^n$ of $L \otimes \mathbb{R}$.
Let $P$ be a fundamental polyhedron of $W_S(L)$ with walls $\alpha_i^\perp$, $\alpha_i \in \Phi_S(L)$.
Each mirror of $W_S(L)$ is $w(\alpha_i^\perp)$ for some $w \in W_S(L)$ and some $i$, so $\Phi_S(L) = \{\pm w \alpha_i\}$.
$W_S(L)$ is generated by the $s_{\alpha_j}$, and $s_{\alpha_j}(x) \in x + \mathbb{Z}\alpha_j$, so $\mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(L) = \mathbb{Z}\langle \alpha_i \rangle$.
$L$ is an $S$-root lattice exactly when the $\alpha_i$ generate $L$.

## Definite lattices

Witt's theorem (Conway and Sloane, *Sphere Packings, Lattices and Groups*, 3rd edition, Chapter 4, §3): a positive definite $\{2\}$-root lattice is an orthogonal sum of lattices $A_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$ and $E_8$.
The field `root_system` of a definite integral record states the ADE type of $\Phi_{\{\pm 2\}}(L)$.

## The label

The build searches for roots among the $r = \sum_i c_i e_i$ with $c_i \in \{-1, 0, 1\}$ and at most three $c_i \neq 0$, in the basis of the record.
When the roots that it finds generate $L$, the lattice has the label `root lattice`, and its page states a set $S$ with $L = \mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(L)$.
The build also assigns the label when `root_system` gives $[L : \mathbb{Z}\Phi_{\{\pm 2\}}(L)] = 1$.
A lattice without the label is not decided.
$S$ is not unique: $\mathbb{Z}^n = \mathbb{Z}\Phi_{\{1\}}(\mathbb{Z}^n)$, and $\mathbb{Z}^n = \mathbb{Z}\Phi_S(\mathbb{Z}^n)$ for each $S \ni 1$.

## ADE lattices in the database

The records of the family [`root-lattice`](../database.html?family=root-lattice) give $A_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$ and $E_8$ in a basis of simple roots $e_1, \dots, e_n$, numbered as SageMath's `CartanMatrix` numbers them.
The component $b(e_i, e_j)$ is the entry $(i, j)$ of the Cartan matrix.
The [dual lattices](../database.html?family=dual-root-lattice) of these lattices are separate records.
