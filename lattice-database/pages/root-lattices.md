A lattice $L$ is a *root lattice* when $L = \mathbb{Z}\Phi(L)$; [roots](../theory/roots.html) gives the definitions and what a record states.

## ADE lattices in the database

The records of the family [`root-lattice`](../database.html?family=root-lattice) give $A_n$, $D_n$, $E_6$, $E_7$ and $E_8$ in the [basis of simple roots](../theory/named-lattices.html#root-lattices).
The [dual lattices](../database.html?family=dual-root-lattice) of these lattices are separate records.
