The Gram tensor of each lattice is the section `GRAM` of an entry of the [Catalogue of Lattices](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/) of G. Nebe and N. J. A. Sloane.
The page of each lattice links to its entry.
