The CI symmetric pair has transvection group $Sp(3)$, isotropy group $U(3)$, and real rank 3. Its noncompact Cartan dual is ci-3-noncompact. The invariant complex structure makes this a Hermitian symmetric space. The classification and duality are recorded in [@Leung2010GeometricStructures; @Milne2005Shimura].
