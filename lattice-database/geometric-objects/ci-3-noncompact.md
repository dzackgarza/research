The CI symmetric pair has transvection group $Sp(6,R)$, isotropy group $U(3)$, and real rank 3. Its compact Cartan dual is ci-3-compact. The invariant complex structure makes this a Hermitian symmetric space. The classification and duality are recorded in [@Leung2010GeometricStructures; @Milne2005Shimura].
