The EVII symmetric pair has transvection group $E7$, isotropy group $(E6 x U(1))/Z3$, and real rank 3. Its noncompact Cartan dual is evii-noncompact. The invariant complex structure makes this a Hermitian symmetric space. The classification and duality are recorded in [@Milne2005Shimura; @BerndtOlmosRodriguez2020].
