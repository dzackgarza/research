The AIII symmetric pair has transvection group $SU(4)$, isotropy group $S(U(2) x U(2))$, and real rank 2. Its noncompact Cartan dual is aiii-22-noncompact. The invariant complex structure makes this a Hermitian symmetric space. The classification and duality are recorded in [@Leung2010GeometricStructures; @Milne2005Shimura].
