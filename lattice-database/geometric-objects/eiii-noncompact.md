The EIII symmetric pair has transvection group $E6(-14)$, isotropy group $(Spin(10) x U(1))/Z4$, and real rank 2. Its compact Cartan dual is eiii-compact. The invariant complex structure makes this a Hermitian symmetric space. The classification and duality are recorded in [@Milne2005Shimura; @BerndtOlmosRodriguez2020].
