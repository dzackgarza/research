The DIII symmetric pair has transvection group $SO(10)$, isotropy group $U(5)$, and real rank 2. Its noncompact Cartan dual is diii-5-noncompact. The invariant complex structure makes this a Hermitian symmetric space. The classification and duality are recorded in [@Leung2010GeometricStructures; @Milne2005Shimura].
