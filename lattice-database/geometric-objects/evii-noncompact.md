The EVII symmetric pair has transvection group $E7(-25)$, isotropy group $(E6 x U(1))/Z3$, and real rank 3. Its compact Cartan dual is evii-compact. The invariant complex structure makes this a Hermitian symmetric space. The classification and duality are recorded in [@Milne2005Shimura; @BerndtOlmosRodriguez2020].
