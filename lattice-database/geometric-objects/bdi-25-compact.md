The BDI symmetric pair has transvection group $SO(7)$, isotropy group $SO(2) x SO(5)$, and real rank 2. Its noncompact Cartan dual is bdi-25-noncompact. The invariant complex structure makes this a Hermitian symmetric space [@KunnemannTamvakisSchubertForms; @Milne2005Shimura].
