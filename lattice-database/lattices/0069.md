$X_n$, for $X$ one of $A$, $D$, $E$, is the root lattice of that type: $e_1, \dots, e_n$ is a basis of simple roots, numbered as SageMath's `CartanMatrix` numbers them, and $b(e_i, e_j)$ is the entry $(i, j)$ of the Cartan matrix. $L^*$ is the dual lattice $\{x \in L \otimes \mathbb{Q} : b(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ for all } y \in L\}$ with the form $b$. Its basis is $e_1^*, \dots, e_n^*$ with $b(e_i^*, e_j) = \delta_{ij}$.
