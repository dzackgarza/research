$L_{2d}$ for $d = 11$: the orthogonal complement $h^\perp \cong U^2 \oplus E_8(-1)^2 \oplus \langle -22 \rangle$ of a primitive vector $h$ with $b(h, h) = 22$ in the K3 lattice $\mathrm{II}_{3,19} \cong U^3 \oplus E_8(-1)^2$, by equation (4) of Gritsenko, Hulek and Sankaran.
The period domain of K3 surfaces with a polarization of degree $2d$ is defined from this lattice.
