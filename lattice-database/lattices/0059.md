Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice.
Row 40 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G = \langle 4 \rangle^3 \oplus \langle -4 \rangle^2$, which is this lattice twisted by $-4$, for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{40} \cong Q_8 * Q_8$, $\mathfrak{G}_{13} \cong Q_8$ and $\mathfrak{G}_{24} \cong Q_8 * C_4$ of Table 10.2.

The invariant and coinvariant lattices of the finite symplectic groups of Hashimoto in the K3 lattice.
