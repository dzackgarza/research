Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 81 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{81} \cong M_{20}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{64} \cong 2^4 C_5$, $\mathfrak{G}_{73} \cong 2^4 D_{10}$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(2)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.
