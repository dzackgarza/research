Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 2 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the group $\mathfrak{G}_{2} \cong C_3$ of Table 10.2, and this record is $\Lambda^G$.
