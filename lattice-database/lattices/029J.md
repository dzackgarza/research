For an Enriques surface $S$ with K3 cover $\tilde S$, the covering involution acts on $H^2(\tilde S, \mathbb{Z}) \cong E_8(-1)^2 \oplus U^3$ with invariant lattice $M \cong E_8(-2) \oplus U(2)$; the anti-invariant lattice $N = M^\perp \cong E_8(-2) \oplus U(2) \oplus U$, of signature $(2, 10)$, is the period lattice of Enriques surfaces (Allcock).
Allcock (Lemma 1) shows that $\mathrm{Aut}(N) \cong \mathrm{Aut}(I_{2,10})$.
