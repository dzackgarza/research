Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice.
For the group $\mathfrak{G}_{21} \cong C_2^4$ of symplectic isometries in Table 10.2 of Hashimoto, the coinvariant lattice $\Lambda_G = (\Lambda^G)^\perp$ twisted by $-1$ is this lattice; $\Lambda^G$ is in row 21 of Table 10.3. The coinvariant lattice `lattices[9,1]` of Höhn and Mason, the orthogonal complement in the Leech lattice of their fixed-point lattice number 1 of rank 9, is isometric to this lattice, and its discriminant form is the form $q$ of row 21 of Table 10.3.

The generators of the pointwise stabilizer $C$ of the fixed-point lattice $B$ of `lattices[9,1]` of Höhn and Mason, arXiv:1505.06420, as automorphisms of the coinvariant lattice $A = B^\perp$.
Through the gluing of $A(-1)$ and the invariant lattice into the K3 lattice, $C$ is the group $\mathfrak{G}_{21} \cong C_2^4$ of symplectic isometries.
