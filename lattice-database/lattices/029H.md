The Nikulin lattice $N = \langle N_1, \ldots, N_8, \hat N \rangle$ with $\hat N = \tfrac{1}{2}(N_1 + \cdots + N_8)$, $N_i^2 = -2$ and $N_i \cdot N_j = 0$ for $i \neq j$ (van Geemen and Sarti, Section 1.5). The classes $N_i$ are the exceptional curves of the resolution of the quotient of a K3 surface by a Nikulin (symplectic) involution, and $N$ is negative definite of rank 8 with discriminant group $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^6$.

This record is $N(-1)$, so that its form is positive definite.
The basis is $N_1, \ldots, N_7, \hat N$.
