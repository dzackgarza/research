$U$ is the lattice with basis $e, f$ and $b(e, e) = b(f, f) = 0$, $b(e, f) = 1$.
$L(k)$ is the module $L$ with the form $k b$.

Roots: $L(k)$ has the roots of $L$ for $k \neq 0$, because the reflection $s_r$ of $L(k)$ is the reflection $s_r$ of $L$.
The roots of $U$ are $\pm(e + f)$ and $\pm(e - f)$; the record of $U$ has the proof.
In $U(2)$, $b(e + f, e + f) = 4$ and $b(e - f, e - f) = -4$.
$\mathbb{Z}\Phi(L)$ is $\{a e + c f : a \equiv c \bmod 2\}$, which has index 2 in $L$ and the orthogonal basis $e + f$, $e - f$.
