Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice.
For the group $\mathfrak{G}_{1} \cong C_2$ of symplectic isometries in Table 10.2 of Hashimoto, the coinvariant lattice $\Lambda_G = (\Lambda^G)^\perp$ twisted by $-1$ is this lattice twisted by 2; $\Lambda^G$ is in row 1 of Table 10.3. The coinvariant lattice `lattices[16,1]` of Höhn and Mason, the orthogonal complement in the Leech lattice of their fixed-point lattice number 1 of rank 16, is isometric to this lattice twisted by 2, and its discriminant form is the form $q$ of row 1 of Table 10.3.
