Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 56 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{56} \cong \Gamma_{25} a_1$, $\mathfrak{G}_{41} \cong \Gamma_7 a_1$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(-4)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.
