Catalogue of Lattices archive entry `union:229` names `GZ9b.12`.

Lift the ternary Golay code of length 12 to Z_9, adjoin vector (111111111111) & apply Construction A. In "The Ternary Golay Code, the Integers Mod 9 and the Coxeter-Todd Lattice" by A. R. Calderbank and N. J. A. Sloane (IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory, 42 (1996), 636-637) we incorrectly claimed that this is isomorphic to the Coxeter-Todd lattice K12. However, the center density is only 1/3^6 not 1/3^3.
