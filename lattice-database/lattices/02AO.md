Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 80 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{80} \cong F_{384}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{28} \cong \Gamma_2 d$, $\mathfrak{G}_{29} \cong Q_{16}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{43} \cong \Gamma_7 a_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{44} \cong \Gamma_3 e$, $\mathfrak{G}_{45} \cong \Gamma_6 a_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{58} \cong \Gamma_{22} a_1$, $\mathfrak{G}_{59} \cong \Gamma_{23} a_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{60} \cong \Gamma_{26} a_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{67} \cong 4^2 D_6$, $\mathfrak{G}_{71} \cong F_{128}$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(4)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.
