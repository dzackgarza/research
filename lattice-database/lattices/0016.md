This is an even hyperbolic unimodular lattice, so $A_L = 0$ and its [invariants](../theory/discriminant-forms.html#two-elementary) are $(r, a, \delta) = (2, 0, 0)$.

Nikulin lists it in Table 1 of *Factor groups of groups of automorphisms of hyperbolic forms with respect to subgroups generated by 2-reflections* (1983), the table of the even hyperbolic lattices $L$ with $2 A_L = 0$ that have a primitive embedding in the K3 lattice.
These are the invariant lattices of the nonsymplectic involutions of K3 surfaces (Remark 4.5.3), and Theorem 4.3.2 states that $(r, a, \delta)$ determines each of them.

Roots: let $r = a e + c f$ be a root.
$r$ is primitive, so $\gcd(a, c) = 1$ and $b(r, L) = \mathbb{Z}$.
$b(r, r) = 2ac$ divides $2 b(r, x)$ for each $x$ in $L$, so $2ac$ divides 2, and $ac = \pm 1$.
The roots are therefore $\pm(e + f)$, with $b(r, r) = 2$, and $\pm(e - f)$, with $b(r, r) = -2$.
$\mathbb{Z}\Phi(L)$ is $\{a e + c f : a \equiv c \bmod 2\}$, which has index 2 in $L$ and the orthogonal basis $e + f$, $e - f$.
