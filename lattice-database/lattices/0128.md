This is an even hyperbolic unimodular lattice, so $A_L = 0$ and its [invariants](../theory/discriminant-forms.html#two-elementary) are $(r, a, \delta) = (10, 0, 0)$.

Nikulin lists it in Table 1 of *Factor groups of groups of automorphisms of hyperbolic forms with respect to subgroups generated by 2-reflections* (1983), the table of the even hyperbolic lattices $L$ with $2 A_L = 0$ that have a primitive embedding in the K3 lattice.
These are the invariant lattices of the nonsymplectic involutions of K3 surfaces (Remark 4.5.3), and Theorem 4.3.2 states that $(r, a, \delta)$ determines each of them.

The Enriques lattice $E_{10} = U \oplus E_8(-1)$: $\mathrm{Num}(S)$ for an Enriques surface $S$ (Dolgachev, Section 1).
