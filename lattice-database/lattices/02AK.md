Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 77 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{77} \cong T_{192}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{37} \cong T_{24}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{69} \cong (Q_8 * Q_8) \rtimes C_3$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(4)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.

### Morphism provenance to 027E

The invariant and coinvariant lattices of the finite symplectic groups of Hashimoto in the K3 lattice.
The anti-isometry maps the generators of the normal form of SageMath of one discriminant form to those of the other twisted by $-1$; three hyperbolic planes are split off and `qfisom` of PARI/GP maps the remainder onto $E_8(-1)^2$.

Source entry `brandt_intrau:Brandt_1.html:4` gives this Gram tensor.

Source entry `watson:3:21` gives this Gram tensor.

Catalogue of Lattices archive entry `union:766` names `hexagonal P (classical holotype)`.

One of the Bravais lattices. Holotype = smallest determinant of any classically integral lattice of this type. I1+A2.
