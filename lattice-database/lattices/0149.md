Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice.
For the group $\mathfrak{G}_{3} \cong C_2^2$ of symplectic isometries in Table 10.2 of Hashimoto, the coinvariant lattice $\Lambda_G = (\Lambda^G)^\perp$ twisted by $-1$ is this lattice; $\Lambda^G$ is in row 3 of Table 10.3. The coinvariant lattice `lattices[12,1]` of Höhn and Mason, the orthogonal complement in the Leech lattice of their fixed-point lattice number 1 of rank 12, is isometric to this lattice, and its discriminant form is the form $q$ of row 3 of Table 10.3.
