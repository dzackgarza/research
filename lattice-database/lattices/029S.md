Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 16 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{16} \cong D_{10}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{5} \cong C_5$ of Table 10.2, and this record is $\Lambda^G$.
