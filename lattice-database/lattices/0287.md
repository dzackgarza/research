$H^2(Y, \mathbb{Z})$ with the Beauville-Bogomolov form, for $Y$ deformation equivalent to O'Grady's variety of dimension 6: $U^3 \oplus \langle -2 \rangle^2$ (Rapagnetta).

Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice.
Row 9 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G = U(2)^3 \oplus \langle -4 \rangle^2$, which is this lattice twisted by 2, for the group $\mathfrak{G}_9 \cong C_2^3$ of Table 10.2.
