Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 78 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{78} \cong \mathfrak{A}_{4,4}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{52} \cong 2^2 (C_2 \times C_6)$, $\mathfrak{G}_{53} \cong 2^2 Q_{12}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{68} \cong 2^3 D_{12}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{72} \cong \mathfrak{A}_4^2$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(2)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.
