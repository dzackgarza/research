Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 33 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{33} \cong C_7 \rtimes C_3$, $\mathfrak{G}_{8} \cong C_7$ of Table 10.2, and this record is $\Lambda^G(-1)$; $\Lambda^G$ has signature $(3, 1)$ and the corpus records the sign with $n_+ \leq n_-$.

Roots: $e_{3}$, $e_{1} + e_{3}$, $e_{2} + e_{3}$ and $e_{1} - e_{2} + 2 e_{3} + e_{4}$ have $b(r, r) = -2$ and $b(r, L) = \mathbb{Z}$, and $-2$ divides $2$.
These roots generate $L$, so $L = \mathbb{Z}\Phi(L)$.
