Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 48 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{48} \cong \mathfrak{S}_{3,3}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{31} \cong C_3 \times D_6$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(-3)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.
