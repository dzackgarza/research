$L^*$ is the dual lattice $\{x \in L \otimes \mathbb{Q} : b(x, y) \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ for all } y \in L\}$ with the form $b$.
Its basis is $e_1^*, \dots, e_n^*$ with $b(e_i^*, e_j) = \delta_{ij}$.
