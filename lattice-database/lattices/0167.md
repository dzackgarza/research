$U$ is the lattice with basis $e, f$ and $b(e, e) = b(f, f) = 0$, $b(e, f) = 1$.
$X_n$, for $X$ one of $A$, $D$, $E$, is the root lattice of that type: $e_1, \dots, e_n$ is a basis of simple roots, numbered as SageMath's `CartanMatrix` numbers them, and $b(e_i, e_j)$ is the entry $(i, j)$ of the Cartan matrix.
$L(k)$ is the module $L$ with the form $k b$.
The basis of an orthogonal sum is the union of the bases of the summands, in the order written.

This is the even hyperbolic 2-elementary lattice with invariants $(r, a, \delta) = (13, 9, 1)$: $r$ is the rank, the discriminant group is $(\mathbb{Z}/2)^a$, and $\delta = 0$ exactly when $b(x, x)$ is an integer for every $x$ in the dual lattice.
An indefinite even 2-elementary lattice is determined by its signature and $(r, a, \delta)$ (Nikulin; see the reference).

The lattice is on the line $r + a = 22$, and it is reflective (Alexeev, Theorem 1.1).
