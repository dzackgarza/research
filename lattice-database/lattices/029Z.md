Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 32 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the group $\mathfrak{G}_{32} \cong \mathrm{Hol}(C_5)$ of Table 10.2, and this record is $\Lambda^G(-1)$; $\Lambda^G$ has signature $(3, 1)$ and the corpus records the sign with $n_+ \leq n_-$.

Roots: $e_{3}$ has $b(r, r) = -4$ and $b(r, L) = 2\mathbb{Z}$, and $-4$ divides $4$.
$e_{1} + e_{2}$ and $2 e_{1} - e_{2} + 2 e_{3} + e_{4}$ have $b(r, r) = -10$ and $b(r, L) = 5\mathbb{Z}$, and $-10$ divides $10$.
$2 e_{1} - 2 e_{2} + 2 e_{3} + e_{4}$ has $b(r, r) = 10$ and $b(r, L) = 10\mathbb{Z}$, and $10$ divides $20$.
These roots generate $L$, so $L = \mathbb{Z}\Phi(L)$.
