Catalogue of Lattices archive entry `union:358` names `LAMBDA24 (The Leech Lattice)`.

The Leech lattice is the unique Niemeier lattice containing no roots: the one of Niemeier's 24 even unimodular positive definite lattices of rank 24 whose root span is $\varnothing$ (Laza–Zheng, Section 3.1, Theorem 3.2). Its automorphism group is the Conway group $Co_0 = O(\mathcal L)$, whose order is $2^{22} \cdot 3^{9} \cdot 5^{4} \cdot 7^{2} \cdot 11 \cdot 13 \cdot 23$.
