Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
The coinvariant lattice $\Lambda_G = (\Lambda^G)^\perp$ is negative definite of rank 16 and has no vector of norm $-2$; this record is $\Lambda_G(-1)$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{16} \cong D_{10}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{5} \cong C_5$ of Table 10.2 of Hashimoto, whose invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ is in row 16 of Table 10.3.

The Gram tensor is the LLL reduction of the coinvariant lattice `lattices[8,7]` of Höhn and Mason: the orthogonal complement in the Leech lattice of the fixed-point lattice $B$ of rank 8 that is number 7 of rank 8 in their Table 1. It satisfies their K3 criterion $\operatorname{rk} B \geq 5$, $\operatorname{rk} B - \operatorname{rk} A_B \geq 2$, and among the coinvariant lattices that satisfy it, it is the only one whose rank and discriminant form are those of $\Lambda_G(-1)$: its discriminant form is the form $q$ of row 16 of Table 10.3. The sum of its twist by $-1$ and $\Lambda^G$ therefore glues to an even unimodular lattice of signature $(3, 19)$, on which the stabilizer of $B$ in $\mathrm{Co}_0$ acts with this coinvariant lattice.
