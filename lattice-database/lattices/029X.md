Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 26 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{26} \cong SD_{16}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{14} \cong C_8$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(-2)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.

### Morphism provenance to 027E

The invariant and coinvariant lattices of the finite symplectic groups of Hashimoto in the K3 lattice.
The anti-isometry maps the generators of the normal form of SageMath of one discriminant form to those of the other twisted by $-1$; three hyperbolic planes are split off and `qfisom` of PARI/GP maps the remainder onto $E_8(-1)^2$.
