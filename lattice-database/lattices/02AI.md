Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 75 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{75} \cong 4^2 \mathfrak{A}_4$, $\mathfrak{G}_{25} \cong C_4^2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{27} \cong C_2 \times Q_8$, $\mathfrak{G}_{42} \cong \Gamma_4 c_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{50} \cong 4^2 C_3$, $\mathfrak{G}_{57} \cong \Gamma_{13} a_1$ of Table 10.2, and this record is the lattice $M$ with $\Lambda^G = M(-2)$; the corpus records $M$ and not its twist.
