Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice.
For the group $\mathfrak{G}_{2} \cong C_3$ of symplectic isometries in Table 10.2 of Hashimoto, the coinvariant lattice $\Lambda_G = (\Lambda^G)^\perp$ twisted by $-1$ is this lattice; $\Lambda^G$ is in row 2 of Table 10.3. The coinvariant lattice `lattices[12,2]` of Höhn and Mason, the orthogonal complement in the Leech lattice of their fixed-point lattice number 2 of rank 12, is isometric to this lattice, and its discriminant form is the form $q$ of row 2 of Table 10.3.

### Morphism provenance to 0150

The generators of the pointwise stabilizer $C$ of the fixed-point lattice $B$ of `lattices[12,2]` of Höhn and Mason, arXiv:1505.06420, as automorphisms of the coinvariant lattice $A = B^\perp$.
Through the gluing of $A(-1)$ and the invariant lattice into the K3 lattice, $C$ is the group $\mathfrak{G}_{2} \cong C_3$ of symplectic isometries.
The archived source for these maps is `sources/hoehn_mason/lattices_12_2.json`. Source-intake tests exercise representative importer paths; mathematical verification uses the stored morphisms and checks their form equations independently.

### Morphism provenance to 027E

The invariant and coinvariant lattices of the finite symplectic groups of Hashimoto in the K3 lattice.
The anti-isometry maps the generators of the normal form of SageMath of one discriminant form to those of the other twisted by $-1$; three hyperbolic planes are split off and `qfisom` of PARI/GP maps the remainder onto $E_8(-1)^2$.
