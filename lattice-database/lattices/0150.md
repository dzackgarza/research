The Gram tensor is the section `GRAM` of the entry `K12` of the Catalogue of Lattices.

The catalogue entry gives this reference text: For the long history of this lattice, and many constructions, see J. H. Conway and N. J. A. Sloane, The Coxeter-Todd Lattice, The Mitchell Group and Related Sphere Packings, Math.
Proc.
Comb.
Phil.
Soc.
volume 93 1983 pages 421-440. Also SPLAG 127.

Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice.
For the group $\mathfrak{G}_{2} \cong C_3$ of symplectic isometries in Table 10.2 of Hashimoto, the coinvariant lattice $\Lambda_G = (\Lambda^G)^\perp$ twisted by $-1$ is this lattice; $\Lambda^G$ is in row 2 of Table 10.3. The coinvariant lattice `lattices[12,2]` of Höhn and Mason, the orthogonal complement in the Leech lattice of their fixed-point lattice number 2 of rank 12, is isometric to this lattice, and its discriminant form is the form $q$ of row 2 of Table 10.3.
