$H^2(Y, \mathbb{Z})$ with the Beauville-Bogomolov form, for $Y$ deformation equivalent to the Hilbert scheme $S^{[2]}$ of $2$ points on a K3 surface $S$: $U^3 \oplus E_8(-1)^2 \oplus \langle -2 \rangle$ (Rapagnetta).

For a hyperkähler manifold of $K3^{[2]}$ type, this is the full cohomology lattice with respect to the Beauville–Bogomolov form: even, but not unimodular, and up to sign $A_1 \oplus E_{8}^{\oplus 2} \oplus U^{\oplus 3}$ (Laza–Zheng, Remark 2.9). This contrasts with the full cohomology lattice of a cubic fourfold, which is odd unimodular, and with the $K3$ case, which is even unimodular.
