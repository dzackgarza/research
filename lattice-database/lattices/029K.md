The even unimodular lattice $L_{2,26} = 2U \oplus 3E_8(-1)$ of signature $(2, 26)$. The Borcherds form $\Phi_{12}$ is the unique modular form of weight 12 and character $\det$ for $O^+(L_{2,26})$, and its quasi-pullbacks along embeddings $L_{2d} \hookrightarrow L_{2,26}$ give cusp forms on the moduli of polarized K3 surfaces (Gritsenko, Hulek and Sankaran, Section 6).
