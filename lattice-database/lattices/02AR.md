Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
The coinvariant lattice $\Lambda_G = (\Lambda^G)^\perp$ is negative definite of rank 19 and has no vector of norm $-2$; this record is $\Lambda_G(-1)$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{80} \cong F_{384}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{28} \cong \Gamma_2 d$, $\mathfrak{G}_{29} \cong Q_{16}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{43} \cong \Gamma_7 a_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{44} \cong \Gamma_3 e$, $\mathfrak{G}_{45} \cong \Gamma_6 a_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{58} \cong \Gamma_{22} a_1$, $\mathfrak{G}_{59} \cong \Gamma_{23} a_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{60} \cong \Gamma_{26} a_2$, $\mathfrak{G}_{67} \cong 4^2 D_6$, $\mathfrak{G}_{71} \cong F_{128}$ of Table 10.2 of Hashimoto, whose invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ is in row 80 of Table 10.3.

The Gram tensor is the LLL reduction of the coinvariant lattice `lattices[5,6]` of Höhn and Mason: the orthogonal complement in the Leech lattice of the fixed-point lattice $B$ of rank 5 that is number 6 of rank 5 in their Table 1. It satisfies their K3 criterion $\operatorname{rk} B \geq 5$, $\operatorname{rk} B - \operatorname{rk} A_B \geq 2$, and among the coinvariant lattices that satisfy it, it is the only one whose rank and discriminant form are those of $\Lambda_G(-1)$: its discriminant form is the form $q$ of row 80 of Table 10.3. The sum of its twist by $-1$ and $\Lambda^G$ therefore glues to an even unimodular lattice of signature $(3, 19)$, on which the stabilizer of $B$ in $\mathrm{Co}_0$ acts with this coinvariant lattice.
