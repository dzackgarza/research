$L_{2d}$ for $d = 9$: the orthogonal complement in the K3 lattice $\mathrm{II}_{3,19} = U^3 \oplus E_8(-1)^2$ of a primitive vector $h$ with $b(h, h) = 2d = 18$, which is $U^2 \oplus E_8(-1)^2 \oplus \langle -2d \rangle$, by equation (4) of Gritsenko, Hulek and Sankaran.
The period domain of K3 surfaces with a polarization of degree $2d$ is built on this lattice.
