Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 61 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the groups $\mathfrak{G}_{61} \cong \mathfrak{A}_{4,3}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{19} \cong C_2 \times C_6$, $\mathfrak{G}_{20} \cong Q_{12}$, $\mathfrak{G}_{36} \cong C_3 \rtimes D_8$, $\mathfrak{G}_{47} \cong C_3 \times \mathfrak{A}_4$ of Table 10.2, and this record is $\Lambda^G(-1)$; $\Lambda^G$ has signature $(3, 1)$ and the corpus records the sign with $n_+ \leq n_-$.
