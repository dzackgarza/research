$H^2(Y, \mathbb{Z})$ with the Beauville-Bogomolov form, for $Y$ deformation equivalent to O'Grady's variety of dimension 10: $U^3 \oplus E_8(-1)^2 \oplus \Lambda$, where Rapagnetta's $\Lambda$ has the Gram tensor $\begin{pmatrix} -6 & 3 \\ 3 & -2 \end{pmatrix}$ in his basis $v_1, v_2$.
In the basis $v_1 + v_2, v_2$ the Gram tensor is that of $A_2(-1)$, which is the basis of the record.
