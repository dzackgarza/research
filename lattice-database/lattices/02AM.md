Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 79 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the group $\mathfrak{G}_{79} \cong \mathfrak{A}_6$ of Table 10.2, and this record is $\Lambda^G$.
The genus of $\Lambda^G$ in row 79 has two classes, and the table lists both; the other is the record (6 0 3; 0 6 3; 3 3 8).
