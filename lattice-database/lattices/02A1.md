Let $\Lambda = \mathrm{II}_{3,19}$ be the K3 lattice and $G \subset O(\Lambda)$ a finite group of symplectic isometries.
Row 34 of Table 10.3 of Hashimoto gives the invariant lattice $\Lambda^G$ for the group $\mathfrak{G}_{34} \cong \mathfrak{S}_4$ of Table 10.2, and this record is $\Lambda^G(-1)$; $\Lambda^G$ has signature $(3, 2)$ and the corpus records the sign with $n_+ \leq n_-$.

The printed row 34 of Table 10.3 names $U \oplus A_2(2) \oplus \langle -12 \rangle$, of determinant $144$; its columns $d = 576$ and $q = 4_5^{+3}, 3^{+2}$ are those of $U \oplus A_2(4) \oplus \langle -12 \rangle$, whose genus has one class, so the columns determine $\Lambda^G$ and the record is that lattice.

Roots: write $r = a e_{1} + c e_{2} + w$ with $w$ in the span $W$ of $e_{3}, \dots, e_{5}$.
$b(e_{1}, e_{2}) = -1$ is odd, $W$ is orthogonal to $e_{1}$ and $e_{2}$, and $b(w, w) \in 4\mathbb{Z}$ for $w$ in $W$, because the Gram tensor of $W$ has its diagonal components in $4\mathbb{Z}$ and the others in $2\mathbb{Z}$.
So $b(r, e_{2}) = -a$, $b(r, e_{1}) = -c$ and $b(r, r) = -2 a c + b(w, w)$.
If $a$ is odd and $c$ is even, then $b(r, r) \equiv 0 \bmod 4$, and $b(r, r)$ divides $2 b(r, e_{2}) = -2 a \equiv 2 \bmod 4$, which is impossible; the same holds with $a$ and $c$ exchanged.
Each root is therefore in $L' = \{a \equiv c \bmod 2\}$, which has index 2 in $L$.
$e_{1} + e_{2}$ has $b(r, r) = -2$ and $b(r, L) = \mathbb{Z}$, and $-2$ divides $2$.
$e_{1} - e_{2}$ has $b(r, r) = 2$ and $b(r, L) = \mathbb{Z}$, and $2$ divides $2$.
$e_{3}$ and $e_{4}$ have $b(r, r) = -8$ and $b(r, L) = 4\mathbb{Z}$, and $-8$ divides $8$.
$e_{5}$ has $b(r, r) = 12$ and $b(r, L) = 12\mathbb{Z}$, and $12$ divides $24$.
These roots are in $L'$, and their invariant factors in $L$ are $1, \dots, 1, 2$, so they generate $L'$: $\mathbb{Z}\Phi(L) = L'$, of index 2 in $L$.

### Morphism provenance to 027E

The invariant and coinvariant lattices of the finite symplectic groups of Hashimoto in the K3 lattice.
The anti-isometry maps the generators of the normal form of SageMath of one discriminant form to those of the other twisted by $-1$; three hyperbolic planes are split off and `qfisom` of PARI/GP maps the remainder onto $E_8(-1)^2$.
