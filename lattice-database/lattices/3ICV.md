The Borcherds lattice $\Lambda_B = \mathrm{II}_{26,2} \cong E_{8}^{\oplus 3} \oplus U^{\oplus 2}$, the unique even unimodular lattice of signature $(26, 2)$, in the sign convention of Laza–Zheng. It is the smallest even unimodular lattice, with definite orthogonal complement, that contains the primitive cubic lattice $\Lambda_0 = E_{8}^{\oplus 2} \oplus U^{\oplus 2} \oplus A_{2}$: the embedding $\Lambda_0 \hookrightarrow \Lambda_B$ has positive definite orthogonal complement $E_6$ (Laza–Zheng, Section 2.4 and Lemma 2.12). Borcherds studied the automorphic forms on the associated type IV symmetric domain. For every Niemeier lattice $N$, $\Lambda_B \cong N \oplus U^{\oplus 2}$ (Laza–Zheng, Remark 2.11), and the classification of the Niemeier lattices follows from the classification of isotropic vectors in $\mathrm{II}_{25,1}$, equivalently of the rank 2 totally isotropic subspaces of $\Lambda_B$.
