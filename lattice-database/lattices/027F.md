$U$ is the lattice with basis $e, f$ and $b(e, e) = b(f, f) = 0$, $b(e, f) = 1$.
$X_n$, for $X$ one of $A$, $D$, $E$, is the root lattice of that type: $e_1, \dots, e_n$ is a basis of simple roots, numbered as SageMath's `CartanMatrix` numbers them, and $b(e_i, e_j)$ is the entry $(i, j)$ of the Cartan matrix.
$\langle a \rangle$ is the lattice of rank 1 with $b(e_1, e_1) = a$.
$L(k)$ is the module $L$ with the form $k b$.
The basis of an orthogonal sum is the union of the bases of the summands, in the order written.

The Mukai lattice: $H^*(S, \mathbb{Z})$ with the Mukai pairing, for a complex K3 surface $S$, is the even unimodular lattice of signature $(4, 20)$, $U^4 \oplus E_8(-1)^2$ (Huybrechts, Section 1.1).
