The lattice $\Lambda_0 = E_{8}^{\oplus 2} \oplus U^{\oplus 2} \oplus A_{2}$, with $E_8$ and $A_2$ the standard root lattices and $U$ the hyperbolic plane. For a smooth cubic fourfold $X$, the primitive cohomology $H^{4}(X, \mathbb{Z})_{\mathrm{prim}} = \eta_X^{\perp}$, the orthogonal complement of the square $\eta_X$ of the hyperplane class, carries the intersection pairing and is isomorphic as a lattice to $\Lambda_0$ (Laza–Zheng, Section 2.1). It carries a polarized Hodge structure of K3 type, with Hodge numbers $(0, 1, 20, 1, 0)$, and the period domain for Hodge structures on it is the 20-dimensional type IV domain $\{x \in \mathbb{P}((\Lambda_0)_{\mathbb C}) : (x, x) = 0,\ (x, x) < 0\}^{+}$. The global monodromy group of cubic fourfolds is $O^{+}(\Lambda_0) \cap \widetilde{O}(\Lambda_0)$, where $\widetilde{O}(\Lambda_0)$ acts trivially on the discriminant group $A_{\Lambda_0} \cong \mathbb{Z}/3$.
