The orthogonal complement $A_2^\perp \cong E_8(-1)^{2} \oplus U^{2} \oplus A_2(-1)$, of signature $(2, 20)$, of $A_2$ in the extended K3 lattice (Huybrechts, equation (2.3)). For a smooth cubic fourfold $X$ it is the primitive cohomology $H^4(X, \mathbb{Z})_{\mathrm{prim}}$ with the intersection form reversed in sign (Section 3.1); its period domain is the period domain of cubic fourfolds.
