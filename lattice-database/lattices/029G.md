The Kummer lattice $K$ is the minimal primitive sublattice of $H^2(\mathrm{Km}(A), \mathbb{Z})$ that contains the classes of the 16 rational curves $K_p$, $p \in A[2] \cong (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^4$, of a Kummer surface (Garbagnati and Sarti, Definition 2.1). It is negative definite, even, of rank 16 and discriminant $2^6$ (Proposition 2.2). A K3 surface is a Kummer surface if and only if $K$ embeds primitively in its Néron–Severi lattice (Nikulin).

This record is $K(-1)$, so that its form is positive definite.
The basis is a Hermite normal form basis of the span, in $\tfrac{1}{2}\bigoplus_p \mathbb{Z} K_p$, of the classes $K_p$, the class $\tfrac{1}{2}\sum_p K_p$ and the 30 classes $\tfrac{1}{2}\sum_{p \in W} K_p$ for the affine hyperplanes $W$ of $(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})^4$ (Remark 2.3 (1)–(2)). The determinant of this span is $2^6$, the discriminant of $K$, so the span is $K$.
