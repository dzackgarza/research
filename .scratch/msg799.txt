research(#799): the invariant lattice of a family of Coble covers, and its monodromy S_10

Two deltas.

(1) moduli-construction.md:664 said "a single curve C, smooth and hence
irreducible". Smoothness does not give irreducibility of a curve: a disjoint
union of two smooth curves is smooth and disconnected, and that is exactly the
case the definition has to exclude, since [@DZ99, §6, Proposition 6.4(2)] gives
Coble surfaces whose anti-bicanonical divisor is smooth with n > 1 components.
The phrase now states the definition's condition, one connected smooth curve,
and says that neither half implies the other.

(2) New lemma lem:coble-invariant-lattice-in-families, the statement of #799.

For a family of Coble covers (an object of def:coble-moduli-functor) with
M_b = H^2(X_b)^iota:
  1. b |-> M_b is a local system of lattices of fibre M = <2> + <-2>^10, type
     (11,11,1), rank 11, signature (1,10), primitive in R^2f_*Z; pi_b^* is
     injective into it, an isomorphism after tensoring Q, and doubles the form;
     over Z on the labelled family.
  2. On the labelled base the eleven classes of lem:d2-local-system(2) are
     global sections spanning the fibre, so transport is the identity in that
     frame: the system is the constant M and the eleven classes are a marking
     of M in families. That is the issue's title.
  3. On the unlabelled base Bbar = [B/S_10] the monodromy is EXACTLY S_10,
     order 10! = 3 628 800, permuting the ten classes and fixing the first; so
     it is not the constant system there, and p^* M = M along the finite etale
     S_10-torsor of lem:d2-labelling-cover trivializes it.
  4. On the stack the monodromy is S_10, and for a family over any connected
     base a subgroup of it, trivial exactly when the ten classes are named.

So the constant-system form holds in the finite etale topology on the base, with
the eleven classes, and the monodromy is stated exactly. This is the issue's
"or it is a stated finite group" branch; it is not a departure from anything in
the volume, and it agrees with lem:d2-local-system(4), which is where the
permutation of the classes is recorded.

The proof uses only earlier blocks: rmk:d2-involution-moduli-of-ae22(1) and
prop:coble-period-domain(1)-(2) for the fibre and its type, thm:two-elementary-
classification for uniqueness, lem:d2-local-system(2),(4), rmk:d2a-family-over-
the-bases for the sections, lem:d2-labelling-cover and lem:d2-label-lift(2) for
the torsor and the S_10 sequence, prop:coble-plane-model-comparison(1) and
prop:coble-stack-smooth-connected(2) for essential surjectivity and
connectedness of Bbar. Constant local system with trivial monodromy:
[@Voi07, Corollary 3.10], as in lem:sublocal-system-of-constant-sheaf.
