# Mathematical sources {#sec-mathematical-sources}

## Higher categories

The category tower, internal hom, and adjunctions in @def-infinity-category-universe, @def-internal-hom, and @def-core are axioms of the framework.
Kerodon [@Lur26] supplies model-specific comparison results for quasicategories, cores, groupoid completion, equivalences, and truncated objects.
The ordinary categorical specialization uses [@Mac94].

## Categories of elements

The variance convention and the discrete-fibration projection in @def-category-of-elements follow [@Rie16, §2.4] and [@nlab:category_of_elements]. The Grothendieck construction is described in [@nlab:grothendieck_construction].

The definition of an abelian category in @def-abelian-category and its kernel and cokernel criteria follow [@stacks-0109].

## Properties and chosen structure

The fullness-and-faithfulness classification in @def-property-structure-stuff follows [@BS07, §2]. Pseudo-pullbacks and their relation to strict pullbacks along isofibrations are treated in [@nlab:2-pullback; @nlab:isofibration].

## Forms, lattices, and arithmetic invariants

Integral lattices and their discriminant forms are treated in [@Nik80]. The exact sequence in @thm-miranda-morrison follows [@MM09, Thm. V.5.1]. The pointed-set sequence used to distinguish a fiber after $\pi_0$ from the components of a homotopy fiber is in [@May99].

Associative unital $R$-algebras as monoids in $R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}$ follow [@nlab:associative_algebra], which also supplies the full subcategory $R\text{-}\mathbf{CAlg}$ of commutative objects. A Frobenius form is a linear form $\varepsilon$ for which $\varepsilon\circ\mu$ is a perfect pairing [@nlab:frobenius_algebra]. Module bilinear forms $\operatorname{Hom}_{R\text{-}\mathbf{Mod}}(M\otimes_R M,R)$ and algebra bilinear forms $\operatorname{Hom}_{R\text{-}\mathbf{Alg}}(A\otimes_R A,R)$ are defined in @def-module-bilinear-form and @def-algebra-bilinear-form; the tensor-hom identification of the former with $\operatorname{Bil}_{R,R}(M)$ uses [@Mac94]. For $A$ in $R\text{-}\mathbf{CAlg}$, the classification of algebra bilinear forms is the coproduct universal property of $A\otimes_R A$ in $R\text{-}\mathbf{CAlg}$ (@prp-characters-pair).
