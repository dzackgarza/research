# Reflection groups of lattices

derived in conversation and are not yet checked against a source. Before any of

## Definitions

Let `L` be a nondegenerate lattice over `ZZ`, with `K = QQ` and `L_K = L ⊗ K`.

- `W(L) := ⟨ s_v : v ∈ L_K, s_v ∈ O(L) ⟩`, the group generated by every integral reflection.
- For `S ⊆ ZZ`, `W_S(L) := ⟨ s_v ∈ W(L) : v² ∈ S ⟩`.

## Which norms occur

Take `v` primitive with `v² ≠ 0`, and let `div(v)` be the positive generator of `(v, L)`.

- `s_v(x) = x − 2(x,v)/v² · v` is integral if and only if `v²` divides `2·div(v)`.
- `div(v)` always divides `v²`, because `(v, v) ∈ (v, L)`.
- So `v² ∈ {±div(v), ±2·div(v)}`.
- `v/div(v) ∈ L^∨`, so `div(v)` divides the exponent `e` of `A_L = L^∨/L`.

Therefore `W(L) = W_S(L)` with `S = {n : n | 2e}`, restricted to the signs the form admits. `S` is finite.

- Even 2-elementary lattices: `e ≤ 2` and `v²` is even, so `S = {±2, ±4}` (Nikulin's setting).
- Even unimodular lattices: `S = {±2}`.
- In general `W(L) ≠ W_{−2,−4}(L)`. In `U ⊕ ⟨−6⟩`, the generator `v` of `⟨−6⟩` has `v² = −6` and `div(v) = 6`, so `s_v` is integral and `−6 ∉ {−2, −4}`.

## When W(L) is a Coxeter group

- **Definite `L`.** `W(L)` is a finite reflection group, hence a finite Coxeter group, with the Coxeter system of the root system formed by the roots of norm in `S`.
- **Hyperbolic `L`, signature `(1, n)`.** Take the reflections in vectors with `v² < 0`. They act on `H^n` as reflections in hyperplanes. A discrete group generated by reflections is a Coxeter group (Vinberg), with Coxeter generators the walls of a chamber. There can be infinitely many walls, and then `W(L)` has infinite rank; this is the non-reflective case. A reflection in a vector with `v² > 0` exchanges the two cone components, so the Coxeter object is `W(L) ∩ O^+(L)`.
- **Signature `(p, q)` with `p, q ≥ 2`.** The fixed hyperplanes do not cut the symmetric space into chambers. No theorem is known to us that makes `W(L)` a Coxeter group here, so none is assumed.
