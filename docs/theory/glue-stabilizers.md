## The glue stabilizer

Let `S ⊕ T ↪ L` be a primitive extension with `T = S^⊥`, discriminant
reductions `ρ_S: O(S) → O(A_S)` and `ρ_T: O(T) → O(A_T)`, and glue
anti-isometry `γ: H_S → H_T` between subgroups `H_S ≤ A_S` and `H_T ≤ A_T`.
The existing `Lattices.glue_map` returns `γ` as `H_S → H_T(−1)`.

- An isometry `g ∈ O(L)` with `g(S) = S` is the same datum as a pair
  `(g_S, g_T)` such that `ρ_S(g_S)` preserves `H_S`, `ρ_T(g_T)` preserves
  `H_T`, and `γ ∘ ρ̄_S(g_S) = ρ̄_T(g_T) ∘ γ`, where the bar means restriction to `H`.
- Hence `Stab_{O(L)}(S) ≅ O(S)_{H_S} ×_{O(H)} O(T)_{H_T}`, a pullback of groups over `O(H)`.
- For `h ∈ S`: `Γ_{h,T} = pr_T(O(S,h) ×_{O(H)} O(T)) = ρ̄_T^{-1}(γ ρ̄_S(O(S,h)_{H_S}) γ^{-1})`.
- When `L` is unimodular, `H_S = A_S`, `H_T = A_T`, and the formula becomes `ρ_T^{-1}(γ ρ_S(O(S,h)) γ^{-1})`.
- `Γ_{h,T}` is the image in `O(T)` of `{g ∈ O(L) : g(S) = S, g(h) = h}`. It equals the image of the whole stabilizer of `h` only when every `g` fixing `h` preserves `S`.

Leads:
- Peters–Sterk, *Symmetric and Quadratic Forms* (June 2024), Props. 15.1.1–15.1.3 and 1.7.4 (already cited by `glue_map`).
- Nikulin, *Integral symmetric bilinear forms and some of their applications* (1979): Prop. 1.4.1 on overlattices and isotropic subgroups; Cor. 1.5.2 and Thm. 1.14.2 on the extension of isometries and on the surjectivity of `O(L) → O(A_L)`. Check the numbering.
- Dolgachev, *Mirror symmetry for lattice polarized K3 surfaces*, for the lattice-polarized setting where `Γ_{h,T}` appears.

## Generators of finite-index subgroups

`Γ_{h,T}` has finite index in `O(T)_{H_T}`, at most `|O(H_T)|`. Given generators of the ambient group:

1. Let the ambient group act on the finite coset space through `ρ̄_T`.
2. Enumerate a transversal. `finite_image_lifts` already does this.
3. Schreier's lemma gives generators of the subgroup.
4. With relations of the ambient group, Reidemeister–Schreier gives a presentation.

This needs only a membership test and the finite index, not a description by generators.

Leads:
- Holt, Eick, O'Brien, *Handbook of Computational Group Theory*: Schreier generators, coset enumeration (Todd–Coxeter), Reidemeister–Schreier, and subgroups given by a membership test.
- Seress, *Permutation Group Algorithms*: Schreier–Sims and the orbit–stabilizer algorithms.
- Magnus, Karrass, Solitar, *Combinatorial Group Theory*, §2.3: Reidemeister–Schreier.
- GAP: `PreImage`, `SubgroupByProperty`, `Stabilizer`, coset enumeration, and the `fga` package for finitely generated free groups.

## Generators of arithmetic ambient groups

The hard input is a generating set of `O(T)` for indefinite `T`, e.g. signature `(2, n)`.

Leads:
- Borel–Harish-Chandra: arithmetic groups are finitely presented.
- Grunewald–Segal, on algorithms for arithmetic groups.
- Vinberg (1972) for reflective hyperbolic lattices.
- Siegel's fundamental domains for `O(p, q)`.
- OSCAR/Hecke `orthogonal_group`, which covers definite lattices. Check its indefinite coverage.
- Eichler transvections: `L.eichler_transvection` already exists. Their generation of `Õ⁺(L)` for `L ⊇ U ⊕ U` is Gritsenko–Hulek–Sankaran, *Abelianisation of orthogonal groups*; check the statement.

## Pullbacks

`A ×_C B` exists as a set in any category that has products and equalizers. `Cat.fiber_product` (`abstract_categories/cat.py`) already defines it once, from `product` and `equalizer`. Leaf categories supply structure on it:

- a subgroup of `A × B` for groups;
- a submodule for modules;
- a subalgebra for algebras;
- the fibre product of schemes, which is not the fibre product of underlying sets.

Leads:
- Mac Lane, *Categories for the Working Mathematician*, V.2 and V.4.
- Riehl, *Category Theory in Context*, ch. 3.
- Stacks Project, the section on fibre products of schemes.
- The repository's limits and colimits contract in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Images of predicate-defined subsets

The image of a subgroup given by generators is generated by the images of the generators. The image of a subset given only by a predicate is in general not computable: this is the existential projection `{f(x) : P(x)}`.

Research leads:
- SymPy `ImageSet` and `ConditionSet`, which represent the image symbolically.
- Quantifier elimination: Tarski–Seidenberg over real closed fields; Presburger arithmetic over `ZZ`.
- Constructive image theorems: Chevalley's theorem for constructible sets under morphisms of finite type, computed by elimination and Gröbner bases.
- Finite-index case: when the predicate subgroup has finite index in a group with generators, the Schreier route above gives it generators, and the image then follows.
